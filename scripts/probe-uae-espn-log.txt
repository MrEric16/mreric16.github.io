The 2026–27 '''UAE Pro League''' (also known as '''ADNOC Pro League''' for sponsorship reasons) is the 52nd edition of the [[UAE Pro League]]. [[Al Ain FC|Al Ain]] are the defending champions.<ref>{{Cite web|title=UAE Pro League Holds 2025-26 Sports Season Draw for ADNOC Pro League, ADIB Cup|url=https://www.uaeproleague.ae/en/news-and-gallery/adnoc-pro-league-543#:~:text=The%20opening%20round%20will%20not,Al%20Nasr%2C%20and%20Al%20Dhafra|website=uaeproleague|access-date=5 November 2025 |date=5 November 2025}}</ref>

== Teams ==

14 teams will compete in the league - the top 12 teams from the [[2025–26 UAE Pro League|previous season]] and the 2 teams promoted from the [[2025–26 UAE First Division League]].
