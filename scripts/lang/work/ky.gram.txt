[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Kyrgyz often drops the pronoun, because the verb ending shows who is speaking. There is no he / she difference: "ал" means both.
row: I | мен | men
row: you (informal) | сен | sen
row: he / she / it | ал | al
row: we | биз | beez
row: you (formal or plural) | сиз | seez
row: they | алар | ah-LAR
ex: I am a student | мен студентмин | men stoo-dent-MEEN
ex: you are tall | сен узунсуң | sen oo-zoon-SOONG
ex: she is my older sister | ал менин эжем | al meh-NIN eh-ZHEM
ex: we are friends | биз достубуз | beez dos-too-BOOZ
ex: they are teachers | алар мугалимдер | ah-LAR moo-gah-leem-DER
## Singular and plural
> Add -лар, -лер, -дар, -дер, -тар, -тер or -лор, -лөр, by vowel harmony and the last sound. After a number, the noun stays singular: эки китеп (two book).
row: китеп (book) | китептер | kee-tep-TER
row: үй (house) | үйлөр | ewy-LUR
row: бала (child) | балдар | bal-DAR
row: after a number: singular | эки китеп | eh-KEE kee-TEP
ex: one house, houses | бир үй, үйлөр | beer EWY ewy-LUR
ex: one book, books | бир китеп, китептер | beer kee-TEP kee-tep-TER
ex: one cat, cats | бир мышык, мышыктар | beer muh-SHUHK muh-shuhk-TAR
ex: two books | эки китеп | eh-KEE kee-TEP
ex: the children | балдар | bal-DAR
## To be: -мын, -сың, -быз
> There is no separate verb "to be". Add an ending to the noun or adjective. The ending changes by vowel harmony and the last sound: -мын / -мин / -бын / -бин / -пын / -пин.
row: I am | -мын / -мин | muhn
row: you are | -сың / -сиң | suhng
row: he / she is | (no ending) | -
row: we are | -быз / -биз | buhz
row: you are (formal) | -сыз / -сиз | suhz
row: they are | -лар / -лер | lar
ex: I am a student | мен студентмин | men stoo-dent-MEEN
ex: you are tired | сен чарчагансың | sen char-chah-gan-SUHNG
ex: he is a doctor | ал дарыгер | al dah-ruh-GER
ex: we are happy | биз бактылуубуз | beez bak-tuh-loo-BOOZ
ex: they are at home | алар үйдө | ah-LAR ewy-DUR
## Bar and jok: have, there is
> Kyrgyz says "at me there is" (бар) or "at me there is not" (жок). The ending -да / -де means "at" or "in".
row: there is | бар | bar
row: there is not | жок | zhok
row: I have | менде ... бар | men-DEH bar
row: I do not have | менде ... жок | men-DEH zhok
ex: I have an older brother | менде аке бар | men-DEH ah-KEH bar
ex: I have a car | менде машина бар | men-DEH mah-SHEE-nah bar
ex: I do not have time | менде убакыт жок | men-DEH oo-bah-KUHT zhok
ex: there is a problem | көйгөй бар | kuy-GUY bar
ex: there is no water | суу жок | soo zhok
## Present tense
> Verb stem + а / е / й + person ending. Келүү (to come): кел-е-м.
row: I come | келем | keh-LEM
row: you come | келесиң | keh-leh-SEENG
row: he / she comes | келет | keh-LET
row: we come | келебиз | keh-leh-BEEZ
row: you come (formal) | келесиз | keh-leh-SEEZ
row: they come | келишет | keh-lee-SHET
ex: I am eating bread | мен нан жеп жатам | men nan zhep zhah-TAM
ex: you are reading a book | сен китеп окуп жатасың | sen kee-TEP o-KOOP zhah-tah-SUHNG
ex: she works in a bank | ал банкта иштейт | al bank-TAH eesh-TAYT
ex: we drink tea | биз чай ичебиз | beez chai ee-cheh-BEEZ
ex: they are sleeping | алар уктап жатышат | ah-LAR ook-TAP zhah-tuh-SHAT
## Basic sentences
> The verb goes last. Order: who, where or when, what, verb. Kyrgyz has no articles: "китеп" means "a book" or "the book".
row: I read a book | мен китеп окуйм | men kee-TEP o-KUIM
row: she drinks tea | ал чай ичет | al chai ee-CHET
row: we go to school | биз мектепке барабыз | beez mek-tep-KEH bah-rah-BUHZ
ex: I read a book | мен китеп окуйм | men kee-TEP o-KUIM
ex: he drinks tea | ал чай ичет | al chai ee-CHET
ex: we go to school | биз мектепке барабыз | beez mek-tep-KEH bah-rah-BUHZ
ex: they eat bread | алар нан жешет | ah-LAR nan zhe-SHET
ex: the teacher writes | мугалим жазат | moo-gah-LEEM zhah-ZAT
## Negation
> Put -ба / -бе / -па / -пе / -бо / -бө / -по / -пө after the verb stem: барам (I go), барбайм (I do not go). For nouns use "эмес" (is not). "Жок" means "there is not". Always compare with the positive form.
row: I go | барам | bah-RAM
row: I do not go | барбайм | bar-BAIM
row: is | (no ending) | -
row: is not | эмес | eh-MES
ex: I want | мен каалайм | men kah-LAIM
ex: I do not want | мен каалабайм | men kah-lah-BAIM
ex: I speak English | мен англисче сүйлөйм | men ahn-glees-CHEH sewy-LUIM
ex: I do not speak English | мен англисче сүйлөбөйм | men ahn-glees-CHEH sewy-lur-BUIM
ex: I like it | мага жагат | mah-GAH zhah-GAT
ex: I do not like it | мага жакпайт | mah-GAH zhak-PAIT
ex: she is here | ал бул жерде | al bool zher-DEH
ex: she is not here | ал бул жерде эмес | al bool zher-DEH eh-MES
ex: I have a car | менде машина бар | men-DEH mah-SHEE-nah bar
ex: I do not have a car | менде машина жок | men-DEH mah-SHEE-nah zhok
ex: I understand | түшүнөм | tew-shew-NUM
ex: I do not understand | түшүнбөйм | tew-shewn-BUIM
ex: we are tired | биз чарчагандабыз | beez char-chah-gan-dah-BUHZ
ex: we are not tired | биз чарчаган жокпуз | beez char-chah-GAN zhok-POOZ
ex: he works on Sundays | ал жекшемби күнү иштейт | al zhek-shem-BEE kew-NEW eesh-TAYT
ex: he does not work on Sundays | ал жекшемби күнү иштебейт | al zhek-shem-BEE kew-NEW eesh-teh-BAYT
## Asking questions
> Add the little word -бы / -би / -бу / -бү / -пы / -пи after the word you are asking about. It is the same for every question.
row: you are a student | сиз студентсиз | seez stoo-dent-SEEZ
row: are you a student? | сиз студентсизби? | seez stoo-dent-seez-BEE
row: he is here | ал бул жерде | al bool zher-DEH
row: is he here? | ал бул жердеби? | al bool zher-deh-BEE
ex: is this your book? | бул сиздин китебиңизби? | bool seez-DEEN kee-teh-beeng-eez-BEE
ex: are they here? | алар бул жердеби? | ah-LAR bool zher-deh-BEE
ex: do you like tea? | сиз чайды жакшы көрөсүзбү? | seez chai-DUH zhak-SHUH kur-rur-sewz-BEW
ex: is it cold? | суукпу? | soo-ook-POO
ex: do you speak English? | сиз англисче сүйлөйсүзбү? | seez ahn-glees-CHEH sewy-lur-sewz-BEW

## Possessives and liking
> Endings go on the noun: -ым / -им (my), -ың / -иң (your), -ы / -и (his / her). To say you like something, use "мага жагат" (to me it is pleasing).
row: my book | китебим | kee-teh-BEEM
row: your book | китебиң | kee-teh-BEENG
row: his / her book | китеби | kee-teh-BEE
row: our book | китебибиз | kee-teh-bee-BEEZ
row: your book (formal) | китебиңиз | kee-teh-beeng-EEZ
ex: my phone | телефоным | teh-leh-fo-NUHM
ex: your house (formal) | үйүңүз | ewy-ewng-EWZ
ex: his sister | анын сиңдиси | ah-NUHN sing-dee-SEE
ex: I like music | мага музыка жагат | mah-GAH moo-zuh-KAH zhah-GAT
ex: I love football | мен футболду жакшы көрөм | men foot-bol-DOO zhak-SHUH kur-RUM
## Formal vs informal
> сен (informal): friends, family, children. сиз (formal): strangers, older people, work. Verb endings change: -сың (informal), -сыз (formal).
row: how are you? | кандайсың? / кандайсыз? | kan-dai-SUHNG / kan-dai-SUHZ
row: you speak | сүйлөйсүң / сүйлөйсүз | sewy-lur-SEWNG / sewy-lur-SEWZ
row: your name | атың / атыңыз | ah-TUHNG / ah-tuhng-UHZ
ex: how are you? (informal) | кандайсың? | kan-dai-SUHNG
ex: how are you? (formal) | кандайсыз? | kan-dai-SUHZ
ex: where are you going? (informal) | кайда барасың? | kai-DAH bah-rah-SUHNG
ex: where are you going? (formal) | кайда барасыз? | kai-DAH bah-rah-SUHZ
ex: please sit down (formal) | отуруңуз, сураныч | o-too-roong-OOZ soo-rah-NUHCH
## Past and future (simple)
> Past: -ды / -ди / -ты / -ти + person ending (бардым = I went). Future uses the present form with a time word: эртең барам (tomorrow I go).
row: I went | бардым | bar-DUHM
row: you went | бардың | bar-DUHNG
row: he went | барды | bar-DUH
row: we went | бардык | bar-DUHK
ex: yesterday I was tired | кечээ чарчадым | keh-CHEH char-chah-DUHM
ex: I will study tomorrow | эртең окуйм | er-TENG o-KUIM
ex: we went to the park | биз паркка бардык | beez park-KAH bar-DUHK
ex: I saw him | мен аны көрдүм | men ah-NUH kurd-EWM
ex: she wrote a letter | ал кат жазды | al kat zhaz-DUH

## Can, must, want
> Can: verb + а алуу (сүзө алам, I can swim). Must: "керек" (needed). Want: -гым келет (жегим келет, I want to eat).
row: I can swim | сүзө алам | sew-ZUR ah-LAM
row: I cannot swim | сүзө албайм | sew-ZUR al-BAIM
row: I must go | баруум керек | bah-roo-OOM keh-REK
row: I want to go | баргым келет | bar-GUHM keh-LET
ex: I can swim | мен сүзө алам | men sew-ZUR ah-LAM
ex: I cannot swim | мен сүзө албайм | men sew-ZUR al-BAIM
ex: can you help me? | мага жардам бере аласызбы? | mah-GAH zhar-DAM beh-REH ah-lah-suhz-BUH
ex: we must go | биз барышыбыз керек | beez bah-ruh-shuh-BUHZ keh-REK
ex: we must not go | биз барбашыбыз керек | beez bar-bah-shuh-BUHZ keh-REK
ex: I want to eat | жегим келет | zheh-GEEM keh-LET
ex: I do not want to eat | жегим келбейт | zheh-GEEM kel-BAYT
ex: you can come | келсеңиз болот | kel-seng-EEZ bo-LOT
ex: you cannot come | келсеңиз болбойт | kel-seng-EEZ bol-BOIT
ex: she wants to study | анын окугусу келет | ah-NUHN o-koo-goo-SOO keh-LET
ex: I must work today | бүгүн иштешим керек | bew-GEWN eesh-teh-SHEEM keh-REK
ex: you must not run | жүгүргө болбойт | zhew-gewr-GUR bol-BOIT
## In, on, at
> Kyrgyz uses endings, not words in front: -да / -де (in, on, at), -га / -ге (to), -дан / -ден (from), -дын / -дин (of).
row: in / on / at | -да / -де | dah / deh
row: to | -га / -ге | gah / geh
row: from | -дан / -ден | dan / den
row: of | -дын / -дин | duhn / deen
ex: the book is on the table | китеп үстөлдө | kee-TEP ews-tul-DUR
ex: the cat is under the table | мышык үстөлдүн астында | muh-SHUHK ews-tul-DEWN as-tuhn-DAH
ex: I am at school | мен мектептемин | men mek-tep-teh-MEEN
ex: I am at home | мен үйдөмүн | men ewy-dur-MEWN
ex: the shop is next to the bank | дүкөн банктын жанында | dew-KUN bank-TUHN zhah-nuhn-DAH
ex: the park is near the station | парк вокзалга жакын | park vok-zal-GAH zhah-KUHN
ex: we meet in the morning | биз эртең менен жолугабыз | beez er-TENG meh-NEN zho-loo-gah-BUHZ
ex: the lesson is at nine | сабак саат тогузда | sah-BAK sah-AT toh-gooz-DAH
ex: the key is in the bag | ачкыч сумкада | ach-KUHCH soom-kah-DAH
## Comparing things
> Add -ыраак / -ирээк to the adjective for "more": чоң, чоңураак. For "the most" put "эң" before it: эң чоң. "Than" is -дан / -ден.
row: big, bigger, the biggest | чоң, чоңураак, эң чоң | chong chong-oo-RAHK eng CHONG
row: small, smaller, the smallest | кичине, кичирээк, эң кичине | kee-chee-NEH kee-chee-REK eng kee-chee-NEH
ex: big, bigger, the biggest | чоң, чоңураак, эң чоң | chong chong-oo-RAHK eng CHONG
ex: small, smaller, the smallest | кичине, кичирээк, эң кичине | kee-chee-NEH kee-chee-REK eng kee-chee-NEH
ex: this is better than that | бул тигиден жакшы | bool tee-gee-DEN zhak-SHUH
ex: this is worse than that | бул тигиден жаман | bool tee-gee-DEN zhah-MAN
ex: she is taller than me | ал менден узунураак | al men-DEN oo-zoo-noo-RAHK
ex: Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand | Ташкент Самарканддан чоңураак | tash-KENT sah-mar-kan-DAN chong-oo-RAHK
ex: it is the cheapest | бул эң арзан | bool eng ar-ZAN
ex: the same as | окшош | ok-SHOSH
