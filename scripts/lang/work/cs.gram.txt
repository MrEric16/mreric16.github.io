[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Czech often drops the pronoun, because the verb ending shows who is speaking. "On" is he, "ona" is she, "ono" is it. "Ty" is informal and "vy" is formal.
row: I | já | yah
row: you (informal) | ty | ti
row: he / she / it | on / ona / ono | on / O-nah / O-no
row: we | my | mi
row: you (formal or plural) | vy | vi
row: they | oni | O-nyi
ex: I am a student | jsem student | yem STOO-dent
ex: you are tall | jsi vysoký | ysi VI-so-kee
ex: she is my older sister | je to moje starší sestra | yeh to MO-yeh STAR-shee SES-trah
ex: we are friends | jsme přátelé | ysmeh PRZHAH-teh-leh
ex: they are teachers | jsou učitelé | ysoh OO-chi-teh-leh
## Singular and plural
> The ending changes with gender. Feminine -a becomes -y: kniha, knihy. Masculine often adds -y or -i: dům, domy. Neuter -o becomes -a: město, města. After 5 and higher, the noun takes a different form.
row: kniha (book) | knihy | KNYI-hi
row: dům (house) | domy | DO-mi
row: město (town) | města | MNYES-tah
row: dítě (child) | děti | DYEH-tyi
ex: one house, houses | jeden dům, domy | YEH-den doom DO-mi
ex: one book, books | jedna kniha, knihy | YED-nah KNYI-hah KNYI-hi
ex: one cat, cats | jedna kočka, kočky | YED-nah KOCH-kah KOCH-ki
ex: two books | dvě knihy | dvye KNYI-hi
ex: the children | děti | DYEH-tyi
## To be: být
> "Být" (to be) is irregular. Learn all the forms. The pronoun is usually dropped.
row: I am | jsem | yem
row: you are | jsi | ysi
row: he / she is | je | yeh
row: we are | jsme | ysmeh
row: you are (formal) | jste | yste
row: they are | jsou | ysoh
ex: I am a student | jsem student | yem STOO-dent
ex: you are tired | jsi unavený | ysi OO-nah-veh-nee
ex: he is a doctor | je lékař | yeh LEH-karzh
ex: we are happy | jsme šťastní | ysmeh SHTYAST-nyee
ex: they are at home | jsou doma | ysoh DO-mah
## Mít: have
> "Mít" (to have): mám, máš, má, máme, máte, mají. "Je" or "jsou" also means "there is".
row: I have | mám | mahm
row: you have | máš | mahsh
row: he / she has | má | mah
row: we have | máme | MAH-meh
row: you have (formal) | máte | MAH-teh
row: they have | mají | MAH-yee
ex: I have an older brother | mám staršího bratra | mahm STAR-shee-ho BRAH-trah
ex: I have a car | mám auto | mahm OW-to
ex: I do not have time | nemám čas | NEH-mahm chas
ex: there is a problem | je tu problém | yeh too PRO-blehm
ex: there is no water | není tu voda | NEH-nyee too VO-dah
## Present tense
> The ending shows the person. Pracovat (to work): pracuji, pracuješ, pracuje, pracujeme, pracujete, pracují.
row: I work | pracuji | PRAH-tsoo-yi
row: you work | pracuješ | PRAH-tsoo-yesh
row: he / she works | pracuje | PRAH-tsoo-yeh
row: we work | pracujeme | PRAH-tsoo-yeh-meh
row: you work (formal) | pracujete | PRAH-tsoo-yeh-teh
row: they work | pracují | PRAH-tsoo-yee
ex: I am eating bread | jím chléb | yeem khlehp
ex: you are reading a book | čteš knihu | chtesh KNYI-hoo
ex: she works in a bank | pracuje v bance | PRAH-tsoo-yeh v BAN-tseh
ex: we drink tea | pijeme čaj | PI-yeh-meh chay
ex: they are sleeping | spí | spee
## Basic sentences
> The usual order is who, verb, what, but Czech is flexible. There are no articles: "kniha" means "a book" or "the book".
row: I read a book | čtu knihu | chtoo KNYI-hoo
row: she drinks tea | pije čaj | PI-yeh chay
row: we go to school | jdeme do školy | YDEH-meh do SHKO-li
ex: I read a book | čtu knihu | chtoo KNYI-hoo
ex: he drinks tea | pije čaj | PI-yeh chay
ex: we go to school | jdeme do školy | YDEH-meh do SHKO-li
ex: they eat bread | jedí chléb | YEH-dyee khlehp
ex: the teacher writes | učitel píše | OO-chi-tel PEE-sheh
## Negation
> Put "ne-" in front of the verb: mám (I have), nemám (I do not have). Czech uses double negatives: nikdy nic nevím.
row: I go | jdu | ydoo
row: I do not go | nejdu | NEY-doo
row: is | je | yeh
row: is not | není | NEH-nyee
ex: I want | chci | khtsi
ex: I do not want | nechci | NEKH-tsi
ex: I speak English | mluvím anglicky | MLOO-veem AN-glits-ki
ex: I do not speak English | nemluvím anglicky | NEH-mloo-veem AN-glits-ki
ex: I like it | líbí se mi to | LEE-bee seh mi to
ex: I do not like it | nelíbí se mi to | NEH-lee-bee seh mi to
ex: she is here | je tady | yeh TAH-di
ex: she is not here | není tady | NEH-nyee TAH-di
ex: I have a car | mám auto | mahm OW-to
ex: I do not have a car | nemám auto | NEH-mahm OW-to
ex: I understand | rozumím | RO-zoo-meem
ex: I do not understand | nerozumím | NEH-ro-zoo-meem
ex: we are tired | jsme unavení | ysmeh OO-nah-veh-nyee
ex: we are not tired | nejsme unavení | NEY-smeh OO-nah-veh-nyee
ex: he works on Sundays | v neděli pracuje | v NEH-dyeh-li PRAH-tsoo-yeh
ex: he does not work on Sundays | v neděli nepracuje | v NEH-dyeh-li NEH-prah-tsoo-yeh
## Asking questions
> The word order does not change. Just raise your voice at the end. You can also start with "Je to ...?"
row: you are a student | jsi student
row: are you a student? | jsi student?
row: he is here | je tady
row: is he here? | je tady?
ex: is this your book? | je to tvoje kniha? | yeh to TVO-yeh KNYI-hah
ex: are they here? | jsou tady? | ysoh TAH-di
ex: do you like tea? | máš rád čaj? | mahsh raht chay
ex: is it cold? | je zima? | yeh ZI-mah
ex: do you speak English? | mluvíte anglicky? | MLOO-vee-teh AN-glits-ki

## Possessives and liking
> The word agrees with the noun: můj / moje / mé (my), tvůj / tvoje (your), jeho (his), její (her), náš / naše (our), váš / vaše (your, formal), jejich (their). To like something, say "mám rád" (I have ... glad) or "líbí se mi".
row: my book | moje kniha | MO-yeh KNYI-hah
row: your book | tvoje kniha | TVO-yeh KNYI-hah
row: his / her book | jeho kniha / její kniha | YEH-ho KNYI-hah / YEH-yee KNYI-hah
row: our book | naše kniha | NAH-sheh KNYI-hah
row: your book (formal) | vaše kniha | VAH-sheh KNYI-hah
ex: my phone | můj telefon | mooy TEH-leh-fon
ex: your house (formal) | váš dům | vahsh doom
ex: his sister | jeho sestra | YEH-ho SES-trah
ex: I like music | mám rád hudbu | mahm raht HOOD-boo
ex: I love football | miluji fotbal | MI-loo-yi FOT-bal
## Formal vs informal
> ty (informal): friends, family, children. vy (formal): strangers, older people, work. The verb changes: -š (informal), -te (formal). Czechs use "vy" more than English speakers expect.
row: how are you? | jak se máš? / jak se máte? | yak seh mahsh / yak seh MAH-teh
row: you speak | mluvíš / mluvíte | MLOO-veesh / MLOO-vee-teh
row: your name | tvé jméno / vaše jméno | tveh YMEH-no / VAH-sheh YMEH-no
ex: how are you? (informal) | jak se máš? | yak seh mahsh
ex: how are you? (formal) | jak se máte? | yak seh MAH-teh
ex: where are you going? (informal) | kam jdeš? | kam ydesh
ex: where are you going? (formal) | kam jdete? | kam YDEH-teh
ex: please sit down (formal) | posaďte se, prosím | PO-sad-yte seh, PRO-seem
## Past and future (simple)
> Past: the stem + -l + a form of "to be": byl jsem (I was). Future: "budu" + the basic verb: budu pracovat (I will work).
row: I went | šel jsem | shel yem
row: you went | šel jsi | shel ysi
row: he went | šel | shel
row: we went | šli jsme | shli ysmeh
ex: yesterday I was tired | včera jsem byl unavený | FCHE-rah yem bil OO-nah-veh-nee
ex: I will study tomorrow | zítra budu studovat | ZEE-trah BOO-doo STOO-do-vat
ex: we went to the park | šli jsme do parku | shli ysmeh do PAR-koo
ex: I saw him | viděl jsem ho | VI-dyel yem ho
ex: she wrote a letter | napsala dopis | NAP-sah-lah DO-pis

row: do not come | nechoď | NEH-khoty
row: sit down | sedni si | SED-nyi si

ex: do not come here | nechoď sem | NEH-khoty sem
ex: sit down | sedni si | SED-nyi si

## Can, must, want
> Moci (can): mohu / můžu. Muset (must): musím. Chtít (want): chci. The second verb stays in its basic form.
row: I can swim | umím plavat
row: I cannot swim | neumím plavat
row: I must go | musím jít
row: I want to go | chci jít
ex: I can swim | umím plavat | OO-meem PLAH-vat
ex: I cannot swim | neumím plavat | NEH-oo-meem PLAH-vat
ex: can you help me? | můžete mi pomoci? | MOO-zheh-teh mi PO-mo-tsi
ex: we must go | musíme jít | MOO-see-meh yeet
ex: we must not go | nesmíme jít | NEH-smee-meh yeet
ex: I want to eat | chci jíst | khtsi yeest
ex: I do not want to eat | nechci jíst | NEKH-tsi yeest
ex: you can come | můžeš přijít | MOO-zhesh PRZHI-yeet
ex: you cannot come | nemůžeš přijít | NEH-moo-zhesh PRZHI-yeet
ex: she wants to study | chce studovat | khtseh STOO-do-vat
ex: I must work today | dnes musím pracovat | dnes MOO-seem PRAH-tso-vat
ex: you must not run | nesmíš běhat | NEH-smeesh BYEH-hat
## In, on, at
> Czech uses small words, and the noun changes its ending after them: v (in), na (on), u (at, by), do (to, into), z (from), s (with), pod (under).
row: in | v | v
row: on | na | nah
row: at / by | u | oo
row: to / into | do | do
ex: the book is on the table | kniha je na stole | KNYI-hah yeh nah STO-leh
ex: the cat is under the table | kočka je pod stolem | KOCH-kah yeh pod STO-lem
ex: I am at school | jsem ve škole | yem veh SHKO-leh
ex: I am at home | jsem doma | yem DO-mah
ex: the shop is next to the bank | obchod je vedle banky | OP-khot yeh VED-leh BAN-ki
ex: the park is near the station | park je blízko nádraží | park yeh BLEES-ko NAH-drah-zhee
ex: we meet in the morning | sejdeme se ráno | SEY-deh-meh seh RAH-no
ex: the lesson is at nine | hodina je v devět | HO-dyi-nah yeh v DEH-vyet
ex: the key is in the bag | klíč je v tašce | kleech yeh f TASH-tseh
## Comparing things
> Add -ější / -ší for "more": velký, větší (irregular). For "the most" put "nej-" in front: největší. "Than" is "než".
row: big, bigger, the biggest | velký, větší, největší
row: small, smaller, the smallest | malý, menší, nejmenší
ex: big, bigger, the biggest | velký, větší, největší | VEL-kee VYET-shee NEY-vyet-shee
ex: small, smaller, the smallest | malý, menší, nejmenší | MAH-lee MEN-shee NEY-men-shee
ex: this is better than that | tohle je lepší než tamto | TOH-leh yeh LEP-shee nezh TAM-to
ex: this is worse than that | tohle je horší než tamto | TOH-leh yeh HOR-shee nezh TAM-to
ex: she is taller than me | je vyšší než já | yeh VISH-shee nezh yah
ex: Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand | Taškent je větší než Samarkand | TASH-kent yeh VYET-shee nezh SAH-mar-kant
ex: it is the cheapest | je to nejlevnější | yeh to NEY-lev-nyeh-yshee
ex: the same as | stejný jako | STEY-nee YAH-ko
