[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Lithuanian has "tu" (you, informal) and "jūs" (you, formal or plural). Verbs change for each person. "Jis" is he and "ji" is she, and the plural splits too: "jie" (men) and "jos" (women).
row: I | aš | ash
row: you (informal) | tu | too
row: he | jis | yis
row: she | ji | yee
row: we | mes | mes
row: you (formal / plural) | jūs | yoos
row: they (m) | jie | yee-eh
row: they (f) | jos | yos
ex: I am a student | aš esu studentas | ash eh-SOO stoo-DEN-tas
ex: you are tall | tu esi aukštas | too eh-SEE OWK-shtas
ex: she is my older sister | ji yra mano vyresnė sesuo | yee ee-RAH MAH-noh vee-RES-neh seh-SOO-oh
ex: we are friends | mes esame draugai | mes eh-SAH-meh drow-GAI
ex: they are teachers | jie yra mokytojai | yee-eh ee-RAH moh-kee-TOH-yai
## Singular and plural
> Lithuanian has no "the" or "a". Masculine nouns end in -as, -is, -ys, -us; feminine in -a, -ė. Plural masculine: -ai, -iai, -ys: knyga, knygos; vaikas, vaikai.
row: the book | knyga | KNEE-gah
row: the books | knygos | KNEE-gos
row: the child | vaikas | VAI-kas
row: the children | vaikai | vai-KAI
ex: one house, houses | namas, namai | NAH-mas, nah-MAI
ex: one book, books | knyga, knygos | KNEE-gah, KNEE-gos
ex: one cat, cats | katė, katės | kah-TEH, kah-TEHS
ex: two books | dvi knygos | dvee KNEE-gos
ex: the children | vaikai | vai-KAI
## To be: būti
> The verb "būti" (to be) changes for each person: esu, esi, yra. "Yra" is also used for "there is".
row: I am | aš esu | ash eh-SOO
row: you are (informal) | tu esi | too eh-SEE
row: he / she is | jis / ji yra | yis / yee ee-RAH
row: we are | mes esame | mes eh-SAH-meh
row: you are (formal / plural) | jūs esate | yoos eh-SAH-teh
row: they are | jie / jos yra | yee-eh / yos ee-RAH
ex: I am a student | aš esu studentas | ash eh-SOO stoo-DEN-tas
ex: you are tired | tu esi pavargęs | too eh-SEE pah-VAR-gehs
ex: he is a doctor | jis yra gydytojas | yis ee-RAH GEE-dee-toh-yas
ex: we are happy | mes esame laimingi | mes eh-SAH-meh lie-MIN-gee
ex: they are at home | jie yra namie | yee-eh ee-RAH nah-MYEH
## Having: turėti
> Lithuanian uses "turėti" (to have): turiu mašiną (I have a car). For "I do not have", use "neturiu". "There is no" is "nėra" and takes the genitive.
row: I have a car | turiu mašiną | TOO-ryoo mah-SHEE-nah
row: you have a car | turi mašiną | TOO-ree mah-SHEE-nah
row: he / she has a car | turi mašiną | TOO-ree mah-SHEE-nah
row: I do not have | neturiu | neh-TOO-ryoo
ex: I have an older brother | turiu vyresnį brolį | TOO-ryoo vee-RES-nee BROH-lee
ex: I have a car | turiu mašiną | TOO-ryoo mah-SHEE-nah
ex: I do not have time | neturiu laiko | neh-TOO-ryoo LAI-koh
ex: there is a problem | yra problema | ee-RAH proh-bleh-MAH
ex: there is no water | nėra vandens | neh-RAH van-DEHNS
## Present tense
> Verbs change for person. Regular verbs (like "valgyti", to eat) take -u, -i, -a / -o: aš valgau, tu valgai, jis valgo.
row: I eat | aš valgau | ash VAL-gow
row: you eat | tu valgai | too VAL-gai
row: he / she eats | jis valgo | yis VAL-goh
row: we eat | mes valgome | mes VAL-goh-meh
ex: I am eating bread | aš valgau duoną | ash VAL-gow DOO-oh-nah
ex: you are reading a book | tu skaitai knygą | too SKAI-tai KNEE-gah
ex: she works in a bank | ji dirba banke | yee DEER-bah BAN-keh
ex: we drink tea | mes geriame arbatą | mes GEH-ryah-meh ar-BAH-tah
ex: they are sleeping | jie miega | yee-eh MYEH-gah
## Basic sentences
> The usual order is subject, verb, object: "aš skaitau knygą" (I read a book). The object ends change: knyga becomes knygą.
row: I read a book | aš skaitau knygą | ash SKAI-tow KNEE-gah
row: he drinks tea | jis geria arbatą | yis GEH-ryah ar-BAH-tah
row: a big house | didelis namas | DEE-deh-lis NAH-mas
row: a new car | nauja mašina | now-YAH mah-shee-NAH
ex: I read a book | aš skaitau knygą | ash SKAI-tow KNEE-gah
ex: he drinks tea | jis geria arbatą | yis GEH-ryah ar-BAH-tah
ex: we go to school | mes einame į mokyklą | mes AY-nah-meh ee moh-KEEK-lah
ex: they eat bread | jie valgo duoną | yee-eh VAL-goh DOO-oh-nah
ex: the teacher writes | mokytojas rašo | moh-KEE-toh-yas RAH-shoh
## Negation
> Put "ne" in front of the verb: nežinau (I do not know). With "būti", "nėra" replaces "yra": nėra vandens (there is no water). Nouns after "nėra" take the genitive.
row: I do not go | aš neinu | ash NAY-noo
row: you do not go | tu neini | too NAY-nee
row: he / she does not go | jis neina | yis NAY-nah
row: it is not good | tai nėra gerai | tie neh-RAH geh-RIE
ex: I want | aš noriu | ash NOH-ryoo
ex: I do not want | aš nenoriu | ash neh-NOH-ryoo
ex: I speak English | aš kalbu angliškai | ash kal-BOO AN-glish-kai
ex: I do not speak English | aš nekalbu angliškai | ash neh-kal-BOO AN-glish-kai
ex: I like it | man tai patinka | man tie pah-TEEN-kah
ex: I do not like it | man tai nepatinka | man tie neh-pah-TEEN-kah
ex: she is here | ji yra čia | yee ee-RAH chya
ex: she is not here | jos čia nėra | yos chya neh-RAH
ex: I have a car | turiu mašiną | TOO-ryoo mah-SHEE-nah
ex: I do not have a car | neturiu mašinos | neh-TOO-ryoo mah-SHEE-nos
ex: I understand | aš suprantu | ash soo-PRAN-too
ex: I do not understand | aš nesuprantu | ash neh-soo-PRAN-too
ex: we are tired | mes esame pavargę | mes eh-SAH-meh pah-VAR-geh
ex: we are not tired | mes nesame pavargę | mes neh-SAH-meh pah-VAR-geh
ex: he works on Sundays | jis dirba sekmadieniais | yis DEER-bah sek-mah-dee-EH-nyais
ex: he does not work on Sundays | jis nedirba sekmadieniais | yis neh-DEER-bah sek-mah-dee-EH-nyais
## Asking questions
> Put "ar" at the start to turn a sentence into a yes / no question: Ar tu esi studentas? (Are you a student?). Answer "taip" (yes) or "ne" (no).
row: Do you go? | ar tu eini? | ar too AY-nee
row: Is it good? | ar tai gerai? | ar tie geh-RIE
row: Is it right? | ar tai teisinga? | ar tie tay-SIN-gah
ex: is this your book? | ar tai tavo knyga? | ar tie TAH-voh KNEE-gah
ex: are they here? | ar jie čia? | ar yee-eh chya
ex: do you like tea? | ar tau patinka arbata? | ar tow pah-TEEN-kah ar-bah-TAH
ex: is it cold? | ar šalta? | ar shal-TAH
ex: do you speak English? | ar kalbi angliškai? | ar KAL-bee AN-glish-kai

## Possessives and liking
> Lithuanian possessives do not change: mano (my), tavo (your), jo (his), jos (her), mūsų (our), jūsų (your, formal), jų (their). "Patikti" means "to please": man patinka arbata (tea pleases me).
row: my book | mano knyga | MAH-noh KNEE-gah
row: my father | mano tėtis | MAH-noh TEH-tis
row: your book | tavo knyga | TAH-voh KNEE-gah
row: his / her book | jo / jos knyga | yoh / yos KNEE-gah
ex: my phone | mano telefonas | MAH-noh teh-leh-FOH-nas
ex: your house (formal) | jūsų namas | YOO-soo NAH-mas
ex: his sister | jo sesuo | yoh seh-SOO-oh
ex: I like music | man patinka muzika | man pah-TEEN-kah MOO-zee-kah
ex: I love football | aš myliu futbolą | ash MEE-lyoo foot-BOH-lah
## Formal vs informal
> Use "jūs" (with "esate", "galite") for one older person, a stranger or in a formal setting. Use "tu" with friends, family and children.
row: sir | pone | POH-neh
row: madam | ponia | POH-nyah
ex: how are you? (informal) | kaip sekasi? | kype seh-kah-SEE
ex: how are you? (formal) | kaip jums sekasi? | kype yooms seh-kah-SEE
ex: where are you going? (informal) | kur eini? | koor AY-nee
ex: where are you going? (formal) | kur einate? | koor AY-nah-teh
ex: please sit down (formal) | prašom sėstis | prah-SHOM SEHS-tis
## Past and future (simple)
> Past: past forms (aš valgiau, I ate). Future: end in -siu (aš valgysiu, I will eat). The past of "būti": buvau, buvai, buvo.
row: I ate | aš valgiau | ash VAL-gyow
row: I will eat | aš valgysiu | ash val-GEE-syoo
row: I will go tomorrow | rytoj eisiu | ree-TOY AY-syoo

ex: I will study tomorrow | rytoj mokysiuos | ree-TOY moh-KEE-syoo-os
ex: we went to the park | mes ėjome į parką | mes eh-YOH-meh ee PAR-kah
ex: I saw him | aš jį mačiau | ash yee MAH-chyow
ex: she wrote a letter | ji parašė laišką | yee pah-rah-SHEH LAISH-kah

## Can, must, want
> "Galėti" (can), "turėti" (must) and "norėti" (want) change for person. They are followed by the "to" form of the verb: aš galiu plaukti (I can swim).

row: I must go | turiu eiti | TOO-ryoo AY-tee

row: may I? | ar galiu? | ar GAH-lyoo

ex: I cannot swim | negaliu plaukti | neh-GAH-lyoo PLOWK-tee

## In, on, at
> Prepositions: "ant" (on), "po" (under), "šalia" (next to), "prie" (at, by), "netoli" (near). For "in" Lithuanian changes the noun ending: mokykla becomes mokykloje (at school), namai becomes namuose (at home).
row: in the house | name | NAH-meh
row: on the table | ant stalo | ant STAH-loh
row: under the table | po stalu | poh STAH-loo
row: at school | mokykloje | moh-KEEK-loh-yeh
ex: the book is on the table | knyga yra ant stalo | KNEE-gah ee-RAH ant STAH-loh
ex: the cat is under the table | katė yra po stalu | kah-TEH ee-RAH poh STAH-loo
ex: I am at school | esu mokykloje | eh-SOO moh-KEEK-loh-yeh
ex: I am at home | esu namie | eh-SOO nah-MYEH
ex: the shop is next to the bank | parduotuvė yra šalia banko | par-doo-oh-too-VEH ee-RAH shah-LYAH BAN-koh
ex: the park is near the station | parkas yra netoli stoties | PAR-kas ee-RAH neh-toh-LEE stoh-TYES
ex: we meet in the morning | susitinkame ryte | soo-see-TIN-kah-meh ree-TEH
ex: the lesson is at nine | pamoka yra devintą valandą | pah-MOH-kah ee-RAH deh-VIN-tah VAH-lan-dah
ex: the key is in the bag | raktas yra krepšyje | RAK-tas ee-RAH krep-SHEE-yeh
## Comparing things
> Add "-esnis / -esnė" to an adjective for "more": didelis, didesnis (big, bigger). For "the most", add "-iausias": didžiausias. "Už" means "than".
row: big, bigger | didelis, didesnis | DEE-deh-lis, dee-DES-nis
row: good, better | geras, geresnis | GEH-ras, geh-RES-nis
row: the biggest | didžiausias | dee-JHYOW-syas
ex: big, bigger, the biggest | didelis, didesnis, didžiausias | DEE-deh-lis, dee-DES-nis, dee-JHYOW-syas
ex: small, smaller, the smallest | mažas, mažesnis, mažiausias | MAH-zhas, mah-ZHES-nis, mah-ZHYOW-syas
ex: this is better than that | šis geresnis už tą | shis geh-RES-nis oozh tah
ex: this is worse than that | šis blogesnis už tą | shis bloh-GES-nis oozh tah
ex: she is taller than me | ji aukštesnė už mane | yee owk-SHTES-neh oozh mah-NEH
ex: Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand | Taškentas didesnis už Samarkandą | tash-KEN-tas dee-DES-nis oozh sah-mar-KAN-dah
ex: it is the cheapest | tai pigiausia | tie pee-GYOW-syah
ex: the same as | toks pat kaip | toks pat kype
