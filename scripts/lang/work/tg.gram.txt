[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Tajik has no gender. "Ӯ" or "вай" means he, she and it. Use "шумо" for one person you respect.
row: I | ман | man
row: you (informal) | ту | too
row: he / she / it | ӯ / вай | oo / vai
row: we | мо | mo
row: you (formal or plural) | шумо | shoo-MO
row: they | онҳо | on-HO
ex: I am a student | ман донишҷӯ ҳастам | man do-neesh-JOO has-TAM
ex: you are tall | ту қадбаланд ҳастӣ | too kad-bah-LAND has-TEE
ex: she is my older sister | вай апаи ман аст | vai ah-pah-ee MAN ast
ex: we are friends | мо дӯст ҳастем | mo DOOST has-TEM
ex: they are teachers | онҳо муаллим ҳастанд | on-HO moo-al-LEEM has-TAND
## Singular and plural
> Add -ҳо to the noun (китоб, китобҳо). For people, -он is also used (бача, бачагон). After a number, the noun stays singular: ду китоб (two book).
row: китоб (book) | китобҳо | kee-tob-HO
row: хона (house) | хонаҳо | kho-nah-HO
row: бача (child) | бачаҳо | bah-chah-HO
row: after a number: singular | ду китоб | doo kee-TOB
ex: one house, houses | як хона, хонаҳо | yak kho-NAH kho-nah-HO
ex: one book, books | як китоб, китобҳо | yak kee-TOB kee-tob-HO
ex: one cat, cats | як гурба, гурбаҳо | yak goor-BAH goor-bah-HO
ex: two books | ду китоб | doo kee-TOB
ex: the children | бачаҳо | bah-chah-HO
## To be: ҳастам, ҳастӣ, аст
> "Ҳастам, ҳастӣ, аст, ҳастем, ҳастед, ҳастанд" are the forms of "to be". After a word they shorten: донишҷӯям (I am a student).
row: I am | ҳастам | has-TAM
row: you are | ҳастӣ | has-TEE
row: he / she is | аст | ast
row: we are | ҳастем | has-TEM
row: you are (formal) | ҳастед | has-TED
row: they are | ҳастанд | has-TAND
ex: I am a student | ман донишҷӯ ҳастам | man do-neesh-JOO has-TAM
ex: you are tired | ту хаста ҳастӣ | too khas-TAH has-TEE
ex: he is a doctor | ӯ духтур аст | oo dookh-TOOR ast
ex: we are happy | мо хушбахт ҳастем | mo khoosh-BAKHT has-TEM
ex: they are at home | онҳо дар хона ҳастанд | on-HO dar kho-NAH has-TAND
## Doshtan and hast: have, there is
> Tajik says "I have" with the verb доштан: дорам, дорӣ, дорад. "Ҳаст" means "there is", "нест" means "there is not".
row: there is | ҳаст | hast
row: there is not | нест | nest
row: I have | ман ... дорам | do-RAM
row: I do not have | ман ... надорам | nah-do-RAM
ex: I have an older brother | ман бародари калон дорам | man bah-ro-dah-ree kah-LON do-RAM
ex: I have a car | ман мошин дорам | man mo-SHEEN do-RAM
ex: I do not have time | ман вақт надорам | man vakt nah-do-RAM
ex: there is a problem | мушкилӣ ҳаст | moosh-kee-LEE hast
ex: there is no water | об нест | ob NEST
## Present tense: ме-
> Put ме- before the verb stem and add an ending. Рафтан (to go), stem рав: ме-рав-ам.
row: I go | меравам | meh-rah-VAM
row: you go | меравӣ | meh-rah-VEE
row: he / she goes | меравад | meh-rah-VAD
row: we go | меравем | meh-rah-VEM
row: you go (formal) | меравед | meh-rah-VED
row: they go | мераванд | meh-rah-VAND
ex: I am eating bread | ман нон мехӯрам | man non meh-khoo-RAM
ex: you are reading a book | ту китоб мехонӣ | too kee-TOB meh-kho-NEE
ex: she works in a bank | вай дар бонк кор мекунад | vai dar bonk KOR meh-koo-NAD
ex: we drink tea | мо чой менӯшем | mo choy meh-noo-SHEM
ex: they are sleeping | онҳо хоб мекунанд | on-HO khob meh-koo-NAND
## Basic sentences
> The verb goes last. Order: who, where or when, what, verb. Tajik has no articles: "китоб" means "a book" or "the book".
row: I read a book | ман китоб мехонам | man kee-TOB meh-kho-NAM
row: she drinks tea | вай чой менӯшад | vai choy meh-noo-SHAD
row: we go to school | мо ба мактаб меравем | mo bah mak-TAB meh-rah-VEM
ex: I read a book | ман китоб мехонам | man kee-TOB meh-kho-NAM
ex: he drinks tea | ӯ чой менӯшад | oo choy meh-noo-SHAD
ex: we go to school | мо ба мактаб меравем | mo bah mak-TAB meh-rah-VEM
ex: they eat bread | онҳо нон мехӯранд | on-HO non meh-khoo-RAND
ex: the teacher writes | муаллим менависад | moo-al-LEEM meh-nah-vee-SAD
## Negation
> Put на- before the verb: меравам (I go), намеравам (I do not go). For "to be" use "нест" (is not). "Надорам" means "I do not have". Always compare with the positive form.
row: I go | меравам | meh-rah-VAM
row: I do not go | намеравам | nah-meh-rah-VAM
row: is | аст | ast
row: is not | нест | nest
ex: I want | ман мехоҳам | man meh-ho-HAM
ex: I do not want | ман намехоҳам | man nah-meh-ho-HAM
ex: I speak English | ман бо забони англисӣ гап мезанам | man bo zah-bo-nee ahn-glee-SEE gap meh-zah-NAM
ex: I do not speak English | ман бо забони англисӣ гап намезанам | man bo zah-bo-nee ahn-glee-SEE gap nah-meh-zah-NAM
ex: I like it | ин ба ман маъқул аст | een bah man mah-KOOL ast
ex: I do not like it | ин ба ман маъқул нест | een bah man mah-KOOL nest
ex: she is here | вай инҷост | vai een-JOST
ex: she is not here | вай инҷо нест | vai een-JO nest
ex: I have a car | ман мошин дорам | man mo-SHEEN do-RAM
ex: I do not have a car | ман мошин надорам | man mo-SHEEN nah-do-RAM
ex: I understand | ман мефаҳмам | man meh-fah-HAM
ex: I do not understand | ман намефаҳмам | man nah-meh-fah-HAM
ex: we are tired | мо хаста ҳастем | mo khas-TAH has-TEM
ex: we are not tired | мо хаста нестем | mo khas-TAH nes-TEM
ex: he works on Sundays | ӯ рӯзи якшанбе кор мекунад | oo roo-zee yak-shan-BEH KOR meh-koo-NAD
ex: he does not work on Sundays | ӯ рӯзи якшанбе кор намекунад | oo roo-zee yak-shan-BEH KOR nah-meh-koo-NAD
## Asking questions
> Say the sentence with a rising voice, or begin with "оё". The word order does not change.
row: you are a student | шумо донишҷӯ ҳастед | shoo-MO do-neesh-JOO has-TED
row: are you a student? | оё шумо донишҷӯ ҳастед? | o-YO shoo-MO do-neesh-JOO has-TED
row: he is here | ӯ инҷост | oo een-JOST
row: is he here? | оё ӯ инҷост? | o-YO oo een-JOST
ex: is this your book? | оё ин китоби шумост? | o-YO een kee-to-bee shoo-MOST
ex: are they here? | оё онҳо инҷоянд? | o-YO on-HO een-jo-YAND
ex: do you like tea? | оё шумо чойро дӯст медоред? | o-YO shoo-MO choy-RO doost meh-do-RED
ex: is it cold? | оё сард аст? | o-YO sard ast
ex: do you speak English? | оё шумо бо забони англисӣ гап мезанед? | o-YO shoo-MO bo zah-bo-nee ahn-glee-SEE gap meh-zah-NED
## Question words
> Question words stand where the answer would stand.
row: what | чӣ | chee
row: who | кӣ | kee
row: where | куҷо | koo-JO
row: when | кай | kai
row: why | чаро | chah-RO
row: how | чӣ хел | chee KHEL
row: how much | чанд | chand
row: which | кадом | kah-DOM
ex: what is your name? | номи шумо чист? | no-mee shoo-MO CHEEST
ex: who is he? | ӯ кист? | oo KEEST
ex: where do you live? | шумо дар куҷо зиндагӣ мекунед? | shoo-MO dar koo-JO zeen-dah-GEE meh-koo-NED
ex: when is the lesson? | дарс кай аст? | dars KAI ast
ex: why are you late? | чаро дер кардед? | chah-RO der kar-DED
ex: how are you? | аҳволи шумо чӣ хел? | ah-vo-lee shoo-MO chee KHEL
ex: how much is it? | чанд пул аст? | chand POOL ast
ex: which book? | кадом китоб? | kah-DOM kee-TOB
## Possessives and liking
> Endings go on the noun: -ам (my), -ат (your), -аш (his / her), -амон (our), -атон (your, formal). To say you like something, use "маъқул аст" (it is pleasing) or "дӯст доштан" (to love).
row: my book | китобам | kee-to-BAM
row: your book | китобат | kee-to-BAT
row: his / her book | китобаш | kee-to-BASH
row: our book | китобамон | kee-to-bah-MON
row: your book (formal) | китобатон | kee-to-bah-TON
ex: my phone | телефонам | teh-leh-fo-NAM
ex: your house (formal) | хонаатон | kho-nah-ah-TON
ex: his sister | хоҳараш | kho-hah-RASH
ex: I like music | ман мусиқиро дӯст медорам | man moo-see-kee-RO doost meh-do-RAM
ex: I love football | ман футболро дӯст медорам | man foot-bol-RO doost meh-do-RAM
## Formal vs informal
> ту (informal): friends, family, children. шумо (formal): strangers, older people, work. Verb endings change: -ӣ (informal), -ед (formal).
row: how are you? | аҳволат чӣ хел? / аҳволи шумо чӣ хел? | ah-vo-LAT chee KHEL / ah-vo-lee shoo-MO chee KHEL
row: you go | меравӣ / меравед | meh-rah-VEE / meh-rah-VED
row: your name | номат / номатон | no-MAT / no-mah-TON
ex: how are you? (informal) | аҳволат чӣ хел? | ah-vo-LAT chee KHEL
ex: how are you? (formal) | аҳволи шумо чӣ хел? | ah-vo-lee shoo-MO chee KHEL
ex: where are you going? (informal) | ту ба куҷо меравӣ? | too bah koo-JO meh-rah-VEE
ex: where are you going? (formal) | шумо ба куҷо меравед? | shoo-MO bah koo-JO meh-rah-VED
ex: please sit down (formal) | лутфан шинед | loot-FAN shee-NED
## Past and future (simple)
> Past: the past stem + person ending (рафтам = I went). Future: хоҳам + the short form (хоҳам рафт = I will go).
row: I went | рафтам | raf-TAM
row: you went | рафтӣ | raf-TEE
row: he went | рафт | raft
row: we went | рафтем | raf-TEM
ex: yesterday I was tired | дирӯз ман хаста будам | dee-ROOZ man khas-TAH boo-DAM
ex: I will study tomorrow | ман пагоҳ хоҳам хонд | man pah-GOH ho-HAM khond
ex: we went to the park | мо ба боғ рафтем | mo bah BOGH raf-TEM
ex: I saw him | ман ӯро дидам | man oo-RO dee-DAM
ex: she wrote a letter | вай мактуб навишт | vai mak-TOOB nah-VEESHT
## Commands
> Informal: бе- + the present stem (биё, come). Formal: add -ед (биёед). To forbid, use на- instead of бе-.
row: come (informal) | биё | bee-YO
row: come (formal) | биёед | bee-yo-ED
row: do not come | наё | nah-YO
row: sit down | шин | sheen
ex: come here | инҷо биё | een-JO bee-YO
ex: do not come here | инҷо наё | een-JO nah-YO
ex: sit down | шин | sheen
ex: do not sit down | нишин | nah-SHEEN
ex: be quiet | хомӯш бош | kho-MOOSH bosh
ex: do not be late | дер накун | der nah-KOON
ex: open the door | дарро кушо | dar-RO koo-SHO
ex: close the window | тирезаро пӯш | tee-reh-zah-RO POOSH
ex: listen to me | ба ман гӯш кун | bah man goosh KOON
ex: do not worry | хавотир нашав | kha-vo-TEER nah-SHAV
ex: speak slowly | оҳиста гап зан | o-hees-TAH gap ZAN
ex: wait a moment | як лаҳза сабр кун | yak lah-ZAH sabr KOON
## Can, must, want
> Can: метавонам + the verb (шино карда метавонам, I can swim). Must: бояд. Want: мехоҳам (бояд and мехоҳам are followed by the short form).
row: I can swim | шино карда метавонам | shee-NO kar-DAH meh-tah-vo-NAM
row: I cannot swim | шино карда наметавонам | shee-NO kar-DAH nah-meh-tah-vo-NAM
row: I must go | бояд равам | bo-YAD rah-VAM
row: I want to go | мехоҳам равам | meh-ho-HAM rah-VAM
ex: I can swim | ман шино карда метавонам | man shee-NO kar-DAH meh-tah-vo-NAM
ex: I cannot swim | ман шино карда наметавонам | man shee-NO kar-DAH nah-meh-tah-vo-NAM
ex: can you help me? | шумо ба ман кӯмак карда метавонед? | shoo-MO bah man koo-MAK kar-DAH meh-tah-vo-NED
ex: we must go | мо бояд равем | mo bo-YAD rah-VEM
ex: we must not go | мо набояд равем | mo nah-bo-YAD rah-VEM
ex: I want to eat | мехоҳам хӯрам | meh-ho-HAM khoo-RAM
ex: I do not want to eat | намехоҳам хӯрам | nah-meh-ho-HAM khoo-RAM
ex: you can come | шумо омада метавонед | shoo-MO o-mah-DAH meh-tah-vo-NED
ex: you cannot come | шумо омада наметавонед | shoo-MO o-mah-DAH nah-meh-tah-vo-NED
ex: she wants to study | вай мехоҳад хонад | vai meh-ho-HAD kho-NAD
ex: I must work today | ман имрӯз бояд кор кунам | man eem-ROOZ bo-YAD kor koo-NAM
ex: you must not run | шумо набояд давед | shoo-MO nah-bo-YAD dah-VED
## In, on, at
> Tajik uses little words in front: дар (in, at), ба (to), аз (from), бо (with), барои (for). The "-и" links nouns (izofat): китоби ман (my book).
row: in / on / at | дар | dar
row: to | ба | bah
row: from | аз | az
row: with | бо | bo
ex: the book is on the table | китоб дар рӯи миз аст | kee-TOB dar roo-ee MEEZ ast
ex: the cat is under the table | гурба дар зери миз аст | goor-BAH dar zeh-ree MEEZ ast
ex: I am at school | ман дар мактаб ҳастам | man dar mak-TAB has-TAM
ex: I am at home | ман дар хона ҳастам | man dar kho-NAH has-TAM
ex: the shop is next to the bank | дӯкон дар паҳлӯи бонк аст | doo-KON dar pah-loo-ee BONK ast
ex: the park is near the station | боғ наздики вокзал аст | bogh naz-dee-kee vok-ZAL ast
ex: we meet in the morning | мо саҳар вомехӯрем | mo sah-HAR vo-meh-khoo-REM
ex: the lesson is at nine | дарс соати нӯҳ аст | dars so-ah-tee NOOH ast
ex: the key is in the bag | калид дар халта аст | kah-LEED dar khal-TAH ast
## Comparing things
> Add -тар to the adjective for "more": калон, калонтар. For "the most" add -тарин: калонтарин. "Than" is аз.
row: big, bigger, the biggest | калон, калонтар, калонтарин | kah-LON kah-lon-TAR kah-lon-tah-REEN
row: small, smaller, the smallest | хурд, хурдтар, хурдтарин | khoord khoord-TAR khoord-tah-REEN
ex: big, bigger, the biggest | калон, калонтар, калонтарин | kah-LON kah-lon-TAR kah-lon-tah-REEN
ex: small, smaller, the smallest | хурд, хурдтар, хурдтарин | khoord khoord-TAR khoord-tah-REEN
ex: this is better than that | ин аз он беҳтар аст | een az on beh-TAR ast
ex: this is worse than that | ин аз он бадтар аст | een az on bad-TAR ast
ex: she is taller than me | вай аз ман баландтар аст | vai az man ba-land-TAR ast
ex: Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand | Тошканд аз Самарқанд калонтар аст | tosh-KAND az sah-mar-KAND kah-lon-TAR ast
ex: it is the cheapest | ин арзонтарин аст | een ar-zon-tah-REEN ast
ex: the same as | монанди | mo-nan-DEE
