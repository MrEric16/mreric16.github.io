INTRO: Kazakh uses Cyrillic with nine extra letters. There are no genders and no articles. The stress is usually on the last syllable. Learn the special letters first.
ә | a short "a", like "cat" | әке (ah-KEH)
ө | say "oh" with rounded lips, like German "ö" | өзен (uh-ZEN)
ү | say "ee" with rounded lips, like German "ü" | үй (ewy)
ұ | a short "oo", like "put" | ұл (ool)
і | a short "ih", like "sit" | бір (bihr)
ғ | gh, like a gargled French "r" | тоғыз (toh-GHUHZ)
қ | a "k" made far back in the throat | қала (kah-LAH)
ң | like "ng" in "sing" | таң (tahng)
һ | a breathy "h", only in a few borrowed words | Алла
ы | a short, flat "uh", like the "a" in "about" | алты (al-TUH)
NOTE: Vowel harmony: back vowels (а, о, ұ, ы) go with қ and ғ. Front vowels (ә, е, ө, ү, і) go with к and г. Suffixes follow the same rule.
