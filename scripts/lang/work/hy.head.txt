INTRO: Armenian has its own alphabet with 38 letters. This pack is Eastern Armenian, the standard of Armenia. There are no genders and no articles. Stress falls on the last syllable. The Latin shown is the standard romanisation.
t' / p' / k' / ts' / ch' | aspirated, with a puff of air, like English "t", "p", "k", "ts", "ch" | t'ey, p'ok'r
t / p / k / ts / ch | plain, with no puff of air, like French or Spanish | tun, pat, kar
kh | like "ch" in Scottish "loch" | khosel
gh | a gargled French "r" | gisher
zh | like "s" in "measure" | zhamanak
dz | like "ds" in "kids" | dzi
rr | a rolled "r", stronger than "r" | rrusaren
ə | a short, flat "uh", like the "a" in "about" | inə
ê | like "e" in "bed" | ê
NOTE: The apostrophe marks an aspirated consonant. The plain letter is the one without the apostrophe.
NOTE: Word-initial "e" and "o" are often said "ye" and "vo". The Armenian-script line is written by rule from the Latin.
