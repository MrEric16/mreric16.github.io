[grammar]
## Pronouns
> There is no he / she difference: "dia" means both. "Anda" is a polite you. "Kamu" is friendly and used with people your age or younger. "Kita" includes the listener (we together). "Kami" excludes the listener (we, not you).
row: I | saya / aku | SAH-yah / AH-koo
row: you (polite) | Anda | AHN-dah
row: you (friendly) | kamu | KAH-moo
row: he / she | dia | DEE-ah
row: we (with you) | kita | KEE-tah
row: we (without you) | kami | KAH-mee
row: they | mereka | meh-REH-kah
ex: I am a student | saya seorang pelajar | SAH-yah seh-OH-rahng peh-LAH-jar
ex: you are tall | kamu tinggi | KAH-moo TING-gee
ex: she is my older sister | dia kakak perempuan saya | DEE-ah KAH-kahk peh-REM-poo-ahn SAH-yah
ex: we are friends | kami berteman | KAH-mee ber-teh-MAHN
ex: they are teachers | mereka guru | meh-REH-kah GOO-roo
## Singular and plural
> Repeat the noun for a plural: buku-buku (books). After a number or "banyak" (many), the noun stays singular: dua buku (two books). A classifier such as "buah" or "ekor" often follows the number.
row: book | buku | BOO-koo
row: books | buku-buku | BOO-koo BOO-koo
row: two books | dua buku | DOO-ah BOO-koo
row: three houses | tiga rumah | TEE-gah ROO-mah
ex: one house, houses | satu rumah, rumah-rumah | SAH-too ROO-mah, ROO-mah ROO-mah
ex: one book, books | satu buku, buku-buku | SAH-too BOO-koo, BOO-koo BOO-koo
ex: one cat, cats | satu kucing, kucing-kucing | SAH-too KOO-ching, KOO-ching KOO-ching
ex: two books | dua buku | DOO-ah BOO-koo
ex: the children | anak-anak | AH-nahk AH-nahk
## To be: adalah
> Often no verb is needed: "Saya pelajar" (I student). Use "adalah" in formal writing. For places use "di" (at).
row: I am a teacher | saya guru | SAH-yah GOO-roo
row: this is a book | ini buku | EE-nee BOO-koo
row: I am at home | saya di rumah | SAH-yah dee ROO-mah
row: I am tall | saya tinggi | SAH-yah TING-gee
ex: I am a student | saya pelajar | SAH-yah peh-LAH-jar
ex: you are tired | kamu lelah | KAH-moo LEH-lah
ex: he is a doctor | dia dokter | DEE-ah DOK-ter
ex: we are happy | kami senang | KAH-mee seh-NAHNG
ex: they are at home | mereka di rumah | meh-REH-kah dee ROO-mah
## Having: punya, ada
> "Punya" means to own. "Ada" means there is, and is also used for having.
row: I have | saya punya | SAH-yah POO-nyah
row: you have | kamu punya | KAH-moo POO-nyah
row: he / she has | dia punya | DEE-ah POO-nyah
row: I do not have | saya tidak punya | SAH-yah TEE-dahk POO-nyah
ex: I have an older brother | saya punya kakak laki-laki | SAH-yah POO-nyah KAH-kahk LAH-kee LAH-kee
ex: I have a car | saya punya mobil | SAH-yah POO-nyah MOH-bil
ex: I do not have time | saya tidak punya waktu | SAH-yah TEE-dahk POO-nyah WAHK-too
ex: there is a problem | ada masalah | AH-dah mah-SAH-lah
ex: there is no water | tidak ada air | TEE-dahk AH-dah AH-eer
## Present tense
> Verbs never change. "Saya makan", "kamu makan" and "mereka makan" all use "makan". Add "sedang" before the verb to say it is happening now.
row: I eat | saya makan | SAH-yah MAH-kahn
row: you eat | kamu makan | KAH-moo MAH-kahn
row: he / she eats | dia makan | DEE-ah MAH-kahn
row: I am eating | saya sedang makan | SAH-yah seh-DAHNG MAH-kahn
ex: I am eating bread | saya sedang makan roti | SAH-yah seh-DAHNG MAH-kahn ROH-tee
ex: you are reading a book | kamu sedang membaca buku | KAH-moo seh-DAHNG mem-BAH-chah BOO-koo
ex: she works in a bank | dia bekerja di bank | DEE-ah beh-KER-jah dee BAHNK
ex: we drink tea | kami minum teh | KAH-mee MEE-noom TEH
ex: they are sleeping | mereka sedang tidur | meh-REH-kah seh-DAHNG TEE-door
## Basic sentences
> The order is subject, verb, object. Adjectives come after the noun: rumah besar (house big).
row: I read a book | saya membaca buku | SAH-yah mem-BAH-chah BOO-koo
row: he drinks tea | dia minum teh | DEE-ah MEE-noom TEH
row: a big house | rumah besar | ROO-mah beh-SAR
row: a new car | mobil baru | MOH-bil BAH-roo
ex: I read a book | saya membaca buku | SAH-yah mem-BAH-chah BOO-koo

## Negation
> Use "tidak" before verbs and adjectives. Use "bukan" before nouns. Use "belum" for "not yet".
row: I do not go | saya tidak pergi | SAH-yah TEE-dahk PER-gee
row: it is not good | tidak baik | TEE-dahk BIE-k
row: this is not a book | ini bukan buku | EE-nee BOO-kahn BOO-koo
row: I have not eaten yet | saya belum makan | SAH-yah BEH-loom MAH-kahn
ex: I want | saya mau | SAH-yah MAU
ex: I do not want | saya tidak mau | SAH-yah TEE-dahk MAU

ex: I do not speak English | saya tidak berbicara bahasa Inggris | SAH-yah TEE-dahk ber-bee-CHAH-rah bah-HAH-sah ING-gris

ex: she is here | dia ada di sini | DEE-ah AH-dah dee SEE-nee
ex: she is not here | dia tidak ada di sini | DEE-ah TEE-dahk AH-dah dee SEE-nee
ex: I have a car | saya punya mobil | SAH-yah POO-nyah MOH-bil

## Asking questions
> Raise your voice at the end, or start with "apakah". Reply with the verb: "Kamu pergi?" "Pergi." (yes) or "Tidak." (no).
row: Do you go? | apakah kamu pergi? | AH-pah-kah KAH-moo PER-gee
row: Is it good? | apakah ini baik? | AH-pah-kah EE-nee BIE-k
row: Is it right? | benar, kan? | beh-NAR, KAHN
ex: is this your book? | apakah ini bukumu? | AH-pah-kah EE-nee BOO-koo-moo
ex: are they here? | apakah mereka ada di sini? | AH-pah-kah meh-REH-kah AH-dah dee SEE-nee
ex: do you like tea? | apakah kamu suka teh? | AH-pah-kah KAH-moo SOO-kah TEH
ex: is it cold? | apakah dingin? | AH-pah-kah DING-in
ex: do you speak English? | apakah kamu berbicara bahasa Inggris? | AH-pah-kah KAH-moo ber-bee-CHAH-rah bah-HAH-sah ING-gris

## Possessives and liking
> Put the owner after the thing: buku saya (book my). Or add -ku (my), -mu (your), -nya (his, her, their): bukuku, bukumu, bukunya.
row: my book | buku saya | BOO-koo SAH-yah
row: your book | bukumu | BOO-koo-moo
row: his / her book | bukunya | BOO-koo-nyah
ex: my phone | ponsel saya | PON-sel SAH-yah
ex: your house (formal) | rumah Anda | ROO-mah AHN-dah
ex: his sister | saudara perempuannya | sah-OO-dah-rah peh-REM-poo-ahn-nyah
ex: I like music | saya suka musik | SAH-yah SOO-kah MOO-sik
ex: I love football | saya cinta sepak bola | SAH-yah CHIN-tah SEH-pahk BOH-lah
## Formal vs informal
> "Anda" and "Bapak / Ibu" are polite. "Kamu" and "aku" are friendly. Use "silakan" (please go ahead) when offering things.
row: sir | Bapak | BAH-pahk
row: madam | Ibu | EE-boo
row: please (go ahead) | silakan | see-LAH-kahn
ex: how are you? (informal) | apa kabar? | AH-pah KAH-bar
ex: how are you? (formal) | bagaimana kabar Anda? | bah-GIE-mah-nah KAH-bar AHN-dah
ex: where are you going? (informal) | kamu mau ke mana? | KAH-moo MAU keh MAH-nah
ex: where are you going? (formal) | Bapak mau ke mana? | BAH-pahk MAU keh MAH-nah
ex: please sit down (formal) | silakan duduk, Pak | see-LAH-kahn DOO-dook, PAHK
## Past and future (simple)
> No verb changes. Past: add "sudah" (already) before the verb. Future: add "akan" before the verb.
row: I ate | saya sudah makan | SAH-yah SOO-dah MAH-kahn
row: I will eat | saya akan makan | SAH-yah AH-kahn MAH-kahn
row: I will go tomorrow | saya akan pergi besok | SAH-yah AH-kahn PER-gee BEH-sok
ex: yesterday I was tired | kemarin saya lelah | keh-MAH-rin SAH-yah LEH-lah
ex: I will study tomorrow | besok saya akan belajar | BEH-sok SAH-yah AH-kahn beh-LAH-jar
ex: we went to the park | kami sudah pergi ke taman | KAH-mee SOO-dah PER-gee keh TAH-mahn
ex: I saw him | saya sudah melihatnya | SAH-yah SOO-dah meh-LEE-haht-nyah
ex: she wrote a letter | dia sudah menulis surat | DEE-ah SOO-dah meh-NOO-lis SOO-raht

## Can, must, want
> "Bisa" or "dapat" (can), "harus" (must) and "mau" (want) go before the main verb. "Tidak bisa" means cannot.
row: I can swim | saya bisa berenang | SAH-yah BEE-sah beh-REH-nahng
row: I must go | saya harus pergi | SAH-yah HAH-roos PER-gee
row: I want to go | saya mau pergi | SAH-yah MAU PER-gee
row: may I? | boleh saya? | BOH-leh SAH-yah
ex: I can swim | saya bisa berenang | SAH-yah BEE-sah beh-REH-nahng
ex: I cannot swim | saya tidak bisa berenang | SAH-yah TEE-dahk BEE-sah beh-REH-nahng
ex: can you help me? | bisa tolong saya? | BEE-sah TOH-long SAH-yah

ex: we must not go | kita tidak boleh pergi | KEE-tah TEE-dahk BOH-leh PER-gee
ex: I want to eat | saya mau makan | SAH-yah MAU MAH-kahn
ex: I do not want to eat | saya tidak mau makan | SAH-yah TEE-dahk MAU MAH-kahn
ex: you can come | kamu bisa datang | KAH-moo BEE-sah DAH-tahng
ex: you cannot come | kamu tidak bisa datang | KAH-moo TEE-dahk BEE-sah DAH-tahng

## In, on, at
> Indonesian uses little words before the place: "di" (at, in, on), "ke" (to), "dari" (from), "di atas" (on top), "di bawah" (under), "di samping" (next to), "dekat" (near).
row: in the house | di dalam rumah | dee DAH-lahm ROO-mah
row: on the table | di atas meja | dee AH-tahs MEH-jah
row: under the table | di bawah meja | dee BAH-wah MEH-jah
row: at school | di sekolah | dee seh-KOH-lah
ex: the book is on the table | buku itu ada di atas meja | BOO-koo EE-too AH-dah dee AH-tahs MEH-jah
ex: the cat is under the table | kucing itu ada di bawah meja | KOO-ching EE-too AH-dah dee BAH-wah MEH-jah
ex: I am at school | saya di sekolah | SAH-yah dee seh-KOH-lah
ex: I am at home | saya di rumah | SAH-yah dee ROO-mah
ex: the shop is next to the bank | toko itu di samping bank | TOH-koh EE-too dee SAHM-ping BAHNK
ex: the park is near the station | taman itu dekat stasiun | TAH-mahn EE-too DEH-kaht stah-SEE-oon
ex: we meet in the morning | kita bertemu pada pagi hari | KEE-tah ber-TEH-moo PAH-dah PAH-gee HAH-ree
ex: the lesson is at nine | pelajarannya jam sembilan | peh-lah-JAH-rahn-nyah JAHM sem-BEE-lahn
ex: the key is in the bag | kunci itu ada di dalam tas | KOON-chee EE-too AH-dah dee DAH-lahm TAHS
## Comparing things
> Put "lebih" (more) before the adjective: lebih besar (bigger). "Than" is "daripada". For the most, use "paling": paling besar.
row: big, bigger | besar, lebih besar | beh-SAR, LEH-bih beh-SAR
row: good, better | baik, lebih baik | BIE-k, LEH-bih BIE-k
row: the biggest | paling besar | PAH-ling beh-SAR
ex: big, bigger, the biggest | besar, lebih besar, paling besar | beh-SAR, LEH-bih beh-SAR, PAH-ling beh-SAR
ex: small, smaller, the smallest | kecil, lebih kecil, paling kecil | keh-CHIL, LEH-bih keh-CHIL, PAH-ling keh-CHIL
ex: this is better than that | ini lebih baik daripada itu | EE-nee LEH-bih BIE-k dah-ree-PAH-dah EE-too
ex: this is worse than that | ini lebih buruk daripada itu | EE-nee LEH-bih BOO-rook dah-ree-PAH-dah EE-too
ex: she is taller than me | dia lebih tinggi daripada saya | DEE-ah LEH-bih TING-gee dah-ree-PAH-dah SAH-yah
ex: Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand | Tashkent lebih besar daripada Samarkand | tahsh-KENT LEH-bih beh-SAR dah-ree-PAH-dah sah-mar-KAHND
ex: it is the cheapest | ini yang paling murah | EE-nee yahng PAH-ling MOO-rah
ex: the same as | sama dengan | SAH-mah DENG-ahn
