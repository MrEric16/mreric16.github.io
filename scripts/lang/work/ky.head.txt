INTRO: Kyrgyz uses Cyrillic with three extra letters. There are no genders and no articles. The stress is usually on the last syllable. Learn the special letters first.
ө | say "oh" with rounded lips, like German "ö" | өрдөк (ur-DUK)
ү | say "ee" with rounded lips, like German "ü" | үй (ewy)
ң | like "ng" in "sing" | миң (ming)
ы | a short, flat "uh", like the "a" in "about" | алты (al-TUH)
к / г | at the back of the mouth these are made further back, in back-vowel words | кыз (kuhz)
ж | like "j" in "jam" | жок (zhok)
NOTE: Vowel harmony: back vowels (а, о, у, ы) and front vowels (э, и, ө, ү) are not mixed in one word. Suffixes follow the same rule.
