INTRO: Greek has its own alphabet with 24 letters. The Latin shown is written by rule from the Greek, the way the word sounds. Every noun is masculine, feminine or neuter. A stress mark (ά) shows which syllable is loud, and the capitals in the say line show it too.
θ | like "th" in "think" | θάλασσα
δ | like "th" in "this" | δέκα
γ | a soft gargle before a, o, u; like "y" before e or i | γάτα, γεια
χ | like "ch" in Scottish "loch" | χέρι
ρ | a rolled "r" | ρολόι
ει / οι / η / ι / υ | all sound like "ee" | είμαι, πόλη
αι | sounds like "e" in "bed" | και
ου | sounds like "oo" in "moon" | ούτε
μπ / ντ | "b" and "d" at the start of a word; "mb" and "nd" inside | μπαμπάς, ντομάτα
NOTE: Greek has two e-sounds in writing (ε, αι) and several ee-sounds (η, ι, ει, οι, υ). You will have to learn the spelling word by word.
NOTE: The Latin line is written by rule from the Greek. Where the Latin looks odd, trust the Greek and the say line.
