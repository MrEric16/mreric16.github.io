INTRO: Romanian is written in the Latin alphabet and is a Romance language, so many words look like Italian or Spanish. It is spelled almost exactly as it sounds. Nouns are masculine, feminine or neuter. The stress is usually on the second-to-last syllable.
ă | a short, flat "uh", like the "a" in "about" | casă
â / î | a deep, throaty "uh", said far back in the mouth | România, în
ș | sh, like "ship" | șase
ț | ts, like "ts" in "cats" | mulțumesc
c (before e, i) | ch, like "church" | cinci
ch / gh (before e, i) | "k" and "g", as in "king" and "get" | chei, ghid
e (at the start) | "ye" in some words | este
NOTE: "c" before e or i is "ch", and "g" before e or i is "j". To get a hard "c" or "g" before e or i, Romanian writes "ch" or "gh".
NOTE: "i" at the end of a word is often almost silent. It softens the consonant before it: lupi (lups).
