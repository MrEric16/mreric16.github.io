[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Romanian often drops the pronoun, because the verb ending shows who is speaking. "El" is he, "ea" is she. "Tu" is informal and "dumneavoastră" is very formal.
row: I | eu | eh-oo
row: you (informal) | tu | too
row: he / she / it | el / ea | el / yah
row: we | noi | noy
row: you (formal or plural) | voi / dumneavoastră | voy / doom-nyah-VWAHS-truh
row: they | ei / ele | ey / EH-leh
ex: I am a student | eu sunt student | eh-oo soont stoo-DENT
ex: you are tall | tu ești înalt | too esht uh-NAHLT
ex: she is my older sister | ea este sora mea mai mare | yah YES-teh SOH-rah myah my MAH-reh
ex: we are friends | noi suntem prieteni | noy soon-TEM pree-eh-TEN
ex: they are teachers | ei sunt profesori | ey soont proh-feh-SOR
## Singular and plural
> The plural endings change the last letters: -e, -i, -uri. carte, cărți. casă, case. copil, copii.
row: carte (book) | cărți | kuhrts
row: casă (house) | case | KAH-seh
row: copil (child) | copii | koh-PEE
row: pisică (cat) | pisici | pee-SEECH
ex: one house, houses | o casă, case | oh KAH-suh KAH-seh
ex: one book, books | o carte, cărți | oh KAR-teh kuhrts
ex: one cat, cats | o pisică, pisici | oh pee-SEE-kuh pee-SEECH
ex: two books | două cărți | DOH-uh kuhrts
ex: the children | copiii | koh-PEE-ee
## To be: a fi
> "A fi" (to be) is irregular. Learn all the forms. The pronoun is usually dropped.
row: I am | sunt | soont
row: you are | ești | esht
row: he / she is | este | YES-teh
row: we are | suntem | soon-TEM
row: you are (formal) | sunteți | soon-TETS
row: they are | sunt | soont
ex: I am a student | sunt student | soont stoo-DENT
ex: you are tired | ești obosit | esht oh-boh-SEET
ex: he is a doctor | el este medic | el YES-teh MEH-deek
ex: we are happy | suntem fericiți | soon-TEM feh-ree-CHEETS
ex: they are at home | sunt acasă | soont ah-KAH-suh
## A avea: have
> "A avea" (to have): am, ai, are, avem, aveți, au.
row: I have | am | ahm
row: you have | ai | eye
row: he / she has | are | AH-reh
row: we have | avem | ah-VEM
row: you have (formal) | aveți | ah-VETS
row: they have | au | ah-oo
ex: I have an older brother | am un frate mai mare | ahm oon FRAH-teh my MAH-reh
ex: I have a car | am o mașină | ahm oh mah-SHEE-nuh
ex: I do not have time | nu am timp | noo ahm teemp
ex: there is a problem | există o problemă | eg-ZEES-tuh oh proh-BLEH-muh
ex: there is no water | nu există apă | noo eg-ZEES-tuh AH-puh
## Present tense
> The ending shows the person. A lucra (to work): lucrez, lucrezi, lucrează, lucrăm, lucrați, lucrează.
row: I work | lucrez | loo-KREZ
row: you work | lucrezi | loo-KREZ
row: he / she works | lucrează | loo-KRAH-zuh
row: we work | lucrăm | loo-KRUHM
row: you work (formal) | lucrați | loo-KRAHTS
row: they work | lucrează | loo-KRAH-zuh
ex: I am eating bread | mănânc pâine | muh-NUHNK PUH-ee-neh
ex: you are reading a book | citești o carte | chee-TESHT oh KAR-teh
ex: she works in a bank | lucrează la bancă | loo-KRAH-zuh lah BAHN-kuh
ex: we drink tea | bem ceai | bem chy
ex: they are sleeping | dorm | dorm
## Basic sentences
> The usual order is who, verb, what, but Romanian is flexible. The article goes after the noun: carte (a book), cartea (the book).
row: I read a book | citesc o carte | chee-TESK oh KAR-teh
row: she drinks tea | ea bea ceai | yah byah chy
row: we go to school | mergem la școală | MER-jem lah shkoh-AH-luh
ex: I read a book | citesc o carte | chee-TESK oh KAR-teh
ex: he drinks tea | el bea ceai | el byah chy
ex: we go to school | mergem la școală | MER-jem lah shkoh-AH-luh
ex: they eat bread | mănâncă pâine | muh-NUHN-kuh PUH-ee-neh
ex: the teacher writes | profesorul scrie | proh-feh-SOH-rool SKREE-eh
## Negation
> Put "nu" before the verb: merg (I go), nu merg (I do not go).
row: I go | merg | merg
row: I do not go | nu merg | noo merg
row: is | este | YES-teh
row: is not | nu este | noo YES-teh
ex: I want | vreau | vryah-oo
ex: I do not want | nu vreau | noo vryah-oo
ex: I speak English | vorbesc engleza | vor-BESK en-GLEH-zah
ex: I do not speak English | nu vorbesc engleza | noo vor-BESK en-GLEH-zah
ex: I like it | îmi place | uhm PLAH-cheh
ex: I do not like it | nu îmi place | noo uhm PLAH-cheh
ex: she is here | ea este aici | yah YES-teh ah-EECH
ex: she is not here | ea nu este aici | yah noo YES-teh ah-EECH
ex: I have a car | am o mașină | ahm oh mah-SHEE-nuh
ex: I do not have a car | nu am mașină | noo ahm mah-SHEE-nuh
ex: I understand | înțeleg | uhn-tseh-LEG
ex: I do not understand | nu înțeleg | noo uhn-tseh-LEG
ex: we are tired | suntem obosiți | soon-TEM oh-boh-SEETS
ex: we are not tired | nu suntem obosiți | noo soon-TEM oh-boh-SEETS
ex: he works on Sundays | lucrează duminica | loo-KRAH-zuh doo-MEE-nee-kah
ex: he does not work on Sundays | nu lucrează duminica | noo loo-KRAH-zuh doo-MEE-nee-kah
## Asking questions
> The word order does not change. Just raise your voice at the end. You can also start with "oare" for a softer question.
row: you are a student | ești student
row: are you a student? | ești student?
row: he is here | este aici
row: is he here? | este aici?
ex: is this your book? | aceasta este cartea ta? | ah-CHAH-stah YES-teh KAR-tyah tah
ex: are they here? | sunt aici? | soont ah-EECH
ex: do you like tea? | îți place ceaiul? | uhts PLAH-cheh CHAH-yool
ex: is it cold? | este frig? | YES-teh freeg
ex: do you speak English? | vorbiți engleza? | vor-BEETS en-GLEH-zah
## Question words
> Question words stand at the start of the sentence.
row: what | ce | cheh
row: who | cine | CHEE-neh
row: where | unde | OON-deh
row: when | când | kuhnd
row: why | de ce | deh cheh
row: how | cum | koom
row: how much | cât | kuht
row: which | care | KAH-reh
ex: what is your name? | cum te cheamă? | koom teh KYAH-muh
ex: who is he? | cine este el? | CHEE-neh YES-teh el
ex: where do you live? | unde locuiești? | OON-deh loh-kwee-ESHT
ex: when is the lesson? | când este ora? | kuhnd YES-teh OH-rah
ex: why are you late? | de ce ai întârziat? | deh cheh eye uhn-tuhr-zee-AHT
ex: how much is it? | cât costă? | kuht KOS-tuh
ex: which book? | care carte? | KAH-reh KAR-teh
## Possessives and liking
> The word comes after the noun and agrees with it: cartea mea (my book), cartea ta (your book), cartea lui (his), cartea ei (her), cartea noastră (our), cartea voastră (your, formal), cartea lor (their). To like something, say "îmi place".
row: my book | cartea mea | KAR-tyah myah
row: your book | cartea ta | KAR-tyah tah
row: his / her book | cartea lui / cartea ei | KAR-tyah looy / KAR-tyah ey
row: our book | cartea noastră | KAR-tyah NWAH-struh
row: your book (formal) | cartea dumneavoastră | KAR-tyah doom-nyah-VWAHS-truh
ex: my phone | telefonul meu | teh-leh-FOH-nool meh-oo
ex: your house (formal) | casa dumneavoastră | KAH-sah doom-nyah-VWAHS-truh
ex: his sister | sora lui | SOH-rah looy
ex: I like music | îmi place muzica | uhm PLAH-cheh MOO-zee-kah
ex: I love football | iubesc fotbalul | yoo-BESK foht-BAH-lool
## Formal vs informal
> tu (informal): friends, family, children. dumneavoastră / voi (formal): strangers, older people, work. The verb changes: -i (informal), -ți (formal).
row: how are you? | ce mai faci? / ce mai faceți? | cheh my fahch / cheh my fah-CHETS
row: you speak | vorbești / vorbiți | vor-BESHT / vor-BEETS
row: your name | numele tău / numele dumneavoastră | NOO-meh-leh tuh-oo / NOO-meh-leh doom-nyah-VWAHS-truh
ex: how are you? (informal) | ce mai faci? | cheh my fahch
ex: how are you? (formal) | ce mai faceți? | cheh my fah-CHETS
ex: where are you going? (informal) | unde mergi? | OON-deh merj
ex: where are you going? (formal) | unde mergeți? | OON-deh mer-JETS
ex: please sit down (formal) | luați loc, vă rog | loo-AH-tsee lohk, vuh rohg
## Past and future (simple)
> Past: "am" + the participle: am mers (I went). Future: "voi" + the basic verb, or "o să": voi merge, o să merg.
row: I went | am mers | ahm mers
row: you went | ai mers | eye mers
row: he went | a mers | ah mers
row: we went | am mers | ahm mers
ex: yesterday I was tired | ieri am fost obosit | yehr ahm fohst oh-boh-SEET
ex: I will study tomorrow | mâine o să studiez | MUH-ee-neh oh suh stoo-dee-EZ
ex: we went to the park | am mers în parc | ahm mers uhn park
ex: I saw him | l-am văzut | luhm vuh-ZOOT
ex: she wrote a letter | a scris o scrisoare | ah skrees oh skree-SWAH-reh

row: do not come | nu veni | noo veh-NEE

ex: do not come here | nu veni aici | noo veh-NEE ah-EECH

ex: be quiet | taci | TAH-chee
ex: do not be late | nu întârzia | noo uhn-tuhr-zee-AH

ex: close the window | închide fereastra | uhn-KEE-deh feh-RYAHS-trah

## Can, must, want
> A putea (can): pot să. A trebui (must): trebuie să. A vrea (want): vreau să. The second verb is in the "să" form.
row: I can swim | pot să înot
row: I cannot swim | nu pot să înot
row: I must go | trebuie să merg
row: I want to go | vreau să merg
ex: I can swim | pot să înot | poht suh uh-NOHT
ex: I cannot swim | nu pot să înot | noo poht suh uh-NOHT
ex: can you help me? | mă puteți ajuta? | muh poo-TETS ah-zhoo-TAH
ex: we must go | trebuie să mergem | treh-BWEE-eh suh MER-jem
ex: we must not go | nu trebuie să mergem | noo treh-BWEE-eh suh MER-jem
ex: I want to eat | vreau să mănânc | vryah-oo suh muh-NUHNK
ex: I do not want to eat | nu vreau să mănânc | noo vryah-oo suh muh-NUHNK
ex: you can come | poți să vii | pohts suh vee
ex: you cannot come | nu poți să vii | noo pohts suh vee
ex: she wants to study | ea vrea să studieze | yah vryah suh stoo-dee-EH-zeh
ex: I must work today | trebuie să lucrez azi | treh-BWEE-eh suh loo-KREZ ahz
ex: you must not run | nu trebuie să alergi | noo treh-BWEE-eh suh ah-LER-jee

row: in | în | uhn

ex: the book is on the table | cartea este pe masă | KAR-tyah YES-teh peh MAH-suh

ex: I am at home | sunt acasă | soont ah-KAH-suh

## Comparing things
> Put "mai" before the adjective for "more": mai mare. For "the most" use "cel mai": cel mai mare. "Than" is "decât".
row: big, bigger, the biggest | mare, mai mare, cel mai mare
row: small, smaller, the smallest | mic, mai mic, cel mai mic
ex: big, bigger, the biggest | mare, mai mare, cel mai mare | MAH-reh my MAH-reh chel my MAH-reh
ex: small, smaller, the smallest | mic, mai mic, cel mai mic | meek my meek chel my meek
ex: this is better than that | acesta este mai bun decât acela | ah-CHES-tah YES-teh my boon deh-KUHT ah-CHEH-lah
ex: this is worse than that | acesta este mai rău decât acela | ah-CHES-tah YES-teh my ruh-oo deh-KUHT ah-CHEH-lah
ex: she is taller than me | ea este mai înaltă decât mine | yah YES-teh my uh-NAHL-tuh deh-KUHT MEE-neh
ex: Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand | Tașkent este mai mare decât Samarkand | tahsh-KENT YES-teh my MAH-reh deh-KUHT sah-mar-KAHND
ex: it is the cheapest | este cel mai ieftin | YES-teh chel my YEF-teen
ex: the same as | la fel ca | lah fel kah
