INTRO: Hungarian is written in the Latin alphabet. It is not related to its neighbours, and it sounds very different. There are no genders and no articles for "a", only "a / az" for "the". The stress is always on the first syllable.
sz | s, like "sit" | szia
s | sh, like "ship" | sál
zs | like "s" in "measure" | zsiráf
cs | ch, like "church" | csak
gy | like "d" and "y" together, as in "did you" | gyerek
ny | like "ny" in "canyon" | nyolc
ty | like "t" and "y" together, as in "got you" | tyúk
ly / j | "y", as in "yes" | lány, jó
c | ts, like "ts" in "cats" | cukor
ö / ő | say "ur" with rounded lips; ő is longer | öt, ő
ü / ű | say "ee" with rounded lips; ű is longer | tű, ügy
á / é / í / ó / ú | the same vowel, said longer | ház
NOTE: Hungarian words get endings instead of small words. "Budapest" + "-ben" is "in Budapest": Budapesten.
NOTE: Vowel harmony: a word uses either back vowels (a, o, u) or front vowels (e, i, ö, ü) in its endings: ház-ban (in the house), kert-ben (in the garden).
