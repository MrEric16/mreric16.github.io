INTRO: Azerbaijani is written in the Latin alphabet and is spelled almost exactly as it sounds. There are no genders and no articles. The stress is usually on the last syllable. Learn the special letters first.
ə | a short "a", like "cat" | əl (al)
ı | a short, flat "uh", like the "a" in "about" | qız
ö | say "oh" with rounded lips, like German "ö" | göz
ü | say "ee" with rounded lips, like German "ü" | gün
c | j, like "jam" | cavab
ç | ch, like "church" | çay
ş | sh, like "ship" | şəhər
x | kh, like "ch" in Scottish "loch" | xeyir
q | a hard "g" made far back in the throat | qapı
ğ | soft, almost silent, makes the vowel before it longer | dağ
h | breathy h, always pronounced | hava
NOTE: Vowel harmony: back vowels (a, ı, o, u) and front vowels (e, ə, i, ö, ü) are not mixed in one word. Suffixes follow the same rule.
