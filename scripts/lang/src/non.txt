INTRO: This is Old Norse of the Viking Age, as written down in 13th-century Iceland (the sagas). The spelling used here is the normalised one that scholars use. Nobody speaks it, but Icelandic is its closest living relative. The pronunciation is a scholars' reconstruction.
ð | like "th" in "this" | faðir
þ | like "th" in "thing" | þing
á / é / í / ó / ú / ý | the same vowels, held long | hús
ǫ | like "o" in "more", but open and short | ǫl
ø / œ | like German "ö" | øxi
æ | like "a" in "cat" | ær (before)
j | like "y" in "yes" | jǫrð
r | rolled | ráð
NOTE: Nouns have three genders, four cases and singular and plural. Many masculine nouns end in -r in the nominative: dagr, hestr, maðr.
NOTE: This pool is much smaller than the Spanish one on purpose. Only well-attested words and forms are included. The pronunciation is reconstructed and nothing has been checked by a specialist, so treat it as a first guide.

[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Old Norse has "ek" (I), "þú" (you), "hann" (he), "hon" (she), "þat" (it), "vér" (we), "ér" (you plural), "þeir" (they). A special dual form "vit" means we two.
row: I | ek | ek
row: you (one) | þú | thoo
row: he | hann | hahn
row: she | hon | hohn
row: it | þat | thaht
row: we | vér | vyayr
row: you (plural) | ér | yayr
row: they | þeir | thayr
ex: I am a man | ek em maðr | ek em MAH-thr
ex: you are strong | þú ert sterkr | thoo ert STERK-r
ex: he is a king | hann er konungr | hahn er KOH-noong-r
ex: she is a woman | hon er kona | hohn er KOH-nah
ex: we are friends | vér erum vinir | vyayr EH-room VI-nir
## To be: vera
> The present of "vera": em, ert, er, erum, eruð, eru.
row: I am | em | em
row: you are | ert | ert
row: he / she is | er | er
row: we are | erum | EH-room
row: you are (plural) | eruð | EH-roo-th
row: they are | eru | EH-roo
ex: I am here | ek em hér | ek em hyayr
ex: the ship is big | skip er mikit | skip er MIK-it
ex: they are brothers | þeir eru bræðr | thayr EH-roo BRAI-thr
## Negation
> Put "eigi" or "ekki" (not) after the verb: ek veit eigi (I know not). Poetry adds the ending -a or -at to the verb: veit-a-k (I know not).
row: not | eigi / ekki | AY-gi / EK-ki
ex: I know | ek veit | ek vayt
ex: I do not know | ek veit eigi | ek vayt AY-gi
ex: he comes | hann kemr | hahn KEM-r
ex: he does not come | hann kemr eigi | hahn KEM-r AY-gi
## Commands
> The command is the bare stem: far (go), kom (come), sit (sit). Add "eigi" or "ekki" to forbid.
row: go! | far | fahr
row: come! | kom | kom
row: see! | sjá | syah
ex: come here | kom hér | kom hyayr
ex: be well | ver heill | ver hayl
ex: go | far | fahr
