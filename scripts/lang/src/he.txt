[sounds]
INTRO: Modern Hebrew is written right to left, mostly without vowel marks. Here the words are shown in Latin letters, with capitals on the stressed syllable.
a | ah, like "father" | todah
e | eh, like "bed" | erev
i | ee, like "see" | shir
o | oh, like "more" | boker
u | oo, like "food" | shulchan
kh | like "ch" in Scottish "loch" | khaver
r | throaty, at the back of the mouth, like French r | rega
tz | like "ts" in "cats" | tzohorayim
sh | like "sh" in "ship" | shalom
' | a small catch in the throat (ayin, aleph) | ma'a
ai | like "eye" | laila
ei | like "ay" in "day" | beit
NOTE: Stress is usually on the last syllable. Words ending in -a, -e are often stressed earlier.
NOTE: Hebrew has no capital letters. Every noun is masculine or feminine, and adjectives and verbs agree with it.

## Formal and informal
> Hebrew has no formal "you". Tone changes with the words you choose. What matters more is whether you speak to a man or a woman.

## Pronouns
> Hebrew has separate words for "you" to a man (ata) and to a woman (at). "They" also has masculine (hem) and feminine (hen). In the present tense, the pronoun is needed, because the verb shows only gender and number.
row: I | ani | a-NI | אני
row: you (m) | ata | A-ta | אתה
row: you (f) | at | at | את
row: he | hu | hu | הוא
row: she | hi | hi | היא
row: we | anakhnu | a-NAKH-nu | אנחנו
row: you all (m) | atem | a-TEM | אתם
row: they (m) | hem | hem | הם
ex: I am a student | ani student | a-NI stu-DENT | אני סטודנט

## Singular and plural
> Every noun is masculine or feminine. Plural: masculine usually adds -im (sefer, sfarim), feminine usually changes -a to -ot (mita, mitot). There are many irregular plurals. The adjective must agree.
row: the | ha- | ha | ה
row: masculine plural | -im | im | ים
row: feminine plural | -ot | ot | ות
ex: the boy | ha-yeled | ha-YE-led | הילד
ex: the girl | ha-yalda | ha-yal-DA | הילדה
ex: the books | ha-sfarim | ha-sfa-RIM | הספרים
ex: a red house | bayit adom | BA-yit a-DOM | בית אדום
ex: the cities | ha-arim | ha-a-RIM | הערים
ex: one cat, two cats | khatul ekhad, shnei khatulim | kha-TUL e-KHAD shnei kha-tu-LIM | חתול אחד, שני חתולים

## To be: no verb in the present
> In the present tense Hebrew has no verb "to be". Just put the two words together: "ani more" (I teacher). Past and future use the verb haya.
row: I am | ani | a-NI | אני
row: it is | ze | ze | זה
row: I was | hayiti | ha-YI-ti | הייתי
row: I will be | ehye | eh-YE | אהיה
