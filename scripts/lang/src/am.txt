NAME: Amharic
ID: am
FLAG: et
SCRIPT: Ge'ez

[sounds]
INTRO: Amharic is the working language of Ethiopia. It is written in the Ge'ez script (fidel), where each symbol is one consonant plus one vowel. Every word here has three layers: the English, the Latin letters, and the Ge'ez writing.
t' / ch' / p' / ts' | ejective sounds: pop the consonant out with a sharp burst of air, with no breath | ጠ ጨ ጰ ጸ
q | an ejective k made deep in the throat | ቀ
sh / zh / ny / j | like "ship", "vision", "canyon", "judge" | ሸ ዠ ኘ ጀ
e | like "e" in "bed" (first and fifth orders) | ሰ ሴ
u | like "oo" in "food" | ሱ
i | like "ee" in "see" | ሲ
a | like "a" in "father" | ሳ
ə | a short, weak vowel (sixth order), often almost silent | ስ
o | like "o" in "more" | ሶ
w | after a consonant it rounds the lips (a labial form) | ቋ ኳ ጓ
NOTE: Each Ge'ez symbol has seven forms (orders), one for each vowel. The consonant stays the same shape, and the vowel changes the shape slightly. Learn the consonants first.
NOTE: Amharic is written left to right, and a gap is used to separate words. Verbs end each sentence. "You" has three forms: to a man, to a woman, and formal.
NOTE: Doubled consonants (gemination) change the meaning but are not shown in the script. The Latin shows them doubled.

## Formal and informal
> Amharic has three kinds of "you": to a man (-h), to a woman (-sh) and formal (-wo, with the verb in the plural). Use the formal form with elders and strangers.

> Use the formal "irswo" for elders and strangers, with the verb in the plural. The ending changes for a man (-h) and a woman (-sh). The command changes too.
ex: you (informal) are my friend | ante gwadegnaye neh | | አንተ ጓደኛዬ ነህ
ex: you (formal) are a teacher | irswo astemari newot | | እርስዎ አስተማሪ ነዎት
ex: how are you? (to a man) | endet neh? | | እንዴት ነህ?
ex: how are you? (formal) | endet newot? | | እንዴት ነዎት?
ex: come here (to a man) | na | | ና
ex: come here (formal) | yimt'u | | ይምጡ
ex: sit down (to a man) | teqemet' | | ተቀመጥ
ex: please sit down (formal) | ebakwo yiqemet'u | | እባክዎ ይቀመጡ
ex: what is your name? (to a man) | simih man new? | | ስምህ ማን ነው?
ex: what is your name? (formal) | simwo man new? | | ስምዎ ማን ነው?
## Past and future (simple)
> Past: the stem + the personal ending -ehu (I): berahu. Future: use the present form. "Was" is neber: dekimogn neber (I was tired).
row: I did | -ehu | -ሁ
row: I will | i- ... -alehu | እ- ... -ለሁ
ex: yesterday I was tired | tinant dekimogn neber | | ትናንት ደክሞኝ ነበር
ex: I will study tomorrow | nege imaralehu | | ነገ እማራለሁ
ex: we went to the park | wede menafesha hedin | | ወደ መናፈሻ ሄድን
ex: I ate | belahu | | በላሁ
ex: she will come | timet'alech | | ትመጣለች
ex: they bought it | gezut | | ገዙት
ex: he called me | dewelelign | | ደወለልኝ
ex: next week we will travel | bemiqet'ilew samint ingwazalen | | በሚቀጥለው ሳምንት እንጓዛለን
## Commands
> The command to one man is the bare stem: na (come). To a woman it ends in -i: ni. To several people: -u. Negative: at- or at- ... -i: atimt'a (do not come).
ex: come here | izih na | | እዚህ ና
ex: do not come here | izih atimt'a | | እዚህ አትምጣ
ex: sit down | teqemet' | | ተቀመጥ
ex: do not sit down | atiqemet' | | አትቀመጥ
ex: be quiet | zim bel | | ዝም በል
ex: do not be late | atizegay | | አትዘግይ
ex: open the door | berun kifet | | በሩን ክፈት
ex: close the window | meskotun ziga | | መስኮቱን ዝጋ

ex: do not worry | atich'eneq | | አትጨነቅ
ex: speak slowly | bezigita tenager | | በዝግታ ተናገር
ex: wait a moment | tinish t'ebiq | | ትንሽ ጠብቅ
## Can, must, want
> Can: tichilaleh (you can); negative: atichilim. Must: alebign (I must); must not: yelebignim. Want: efeligalehu. These take a verbal noun: mehed (to go).
ex: I can swim | mewanyet ichilalehu | | መዋኘት እችላለሁ
ex: I cannot swim | mewanyet alichilim | | መዋኘት አልችልም
ex: can you help me? | tiredagnaleh? | | ትረዳኛለህ?
ex: we must go | mehed alebin | | መሄድ አለብን
ex: we must not go | mehed yelebinim | | መሄድ የለብንም
ex: I want to eat | mebelat efeligalehu | | መብላት እፈልጋለሁ
ex: I do not want to eat | mebelat alefeligim | | መብላት አልፈልግም
ex: you can come | memt'at tichilaleh | | መምጣት ትችላለህ
ex: you cannot come | memt'at atichilim | | መምጣት አትችልም
ex: she wants to study | memar tifeligalech | | መማር ትፈልጋለች
ex: I must work today | zare mesrat alebign | | ዛሬ መስራት አለብኝ
ex: you must not run | merot' yelebihim | | መሮጥ የለብህም
## In, on, at
> Amharic uses words after the noun (postpositions): lay (on), sir (under), wist' (in), at'egeb (next to), qirib (near). Place with "to be" ends the sentence.
row: on | lay | ላይ
row: under | sir | ስር
row: in | wist' | ውስጥ
row: next to | at'egeb | አጠገብ
row: near | qirib | ቅርብ
ex: the book is on the table | mets'hafu t'erep'ezaw lay new | | መጽሐፉ ጠረጴዛው ላይ ነው
ex: the cat is under the table | dimetwa t'erep'ezaw sir nat | | ድመቷ ጠረጴዛው ስር ናት
ex: I am at school | timihirt bet negn | | ትምህርት ቤት ነኝ
ex: I am at home | bet negn | | ቤት ነኝ
ex: the shop is next to the bank | suqu banku at'egeb new | | ሱቁ ባንኩ አጠገብ ነው
ex: the park is near the station | menafeshaw ket'abiyaw qirib new | | መናፈሻው ከጣቢያው ቅርብ ነው
ex: we meet in the morning | t'ewat engenagnalen | | ጠዋት እንገናኛለን

## Comparing things
> Use ke- (than) before the thing you compare to, and yebelt' (more): ke ya yishalal (it is better than that). "The most" is kehulu (than all).
row: than | ke- | ከ-
row: more | yebelt' | የበለጠ
row: than all | kehulu | ከሁሉ
ex: big, bigger, the biggest | tiliq, yebelt' tiliq, kehulu yemibelt' tiliq | | ትልቅ የበለጠ ትልቅ ከሁሉ የሚበልጥ ትልቅ
ex: small, smaller, the smallest | tinish, yebelt' tinish, kehulu tinish | | ትንሽ የበለጠ ትንሽ ከሁሉ ትንሽ
ex: this is better than that | yih keziya yishalal | | ይህ ከዚያ ይሻላል
ex: this is worse than that | yih keziya yebase new | | ይህ ከዚያ የባሰ ነው
ex: she is taller than me | keene tirezimalech | | ከእኔ ትረዝማለች
ex: Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand | tashkent kesamarkand tibelt'alech | | ታሽከንት ከሳምርካንድ ትበልጣለች
ex: it is the cheapest | kehulum rikash new | | ከሁሉም ርካሽ ነው
ex: the same as | lik inde | | ልክ እንደ
## Moods and wishes
> "If" is ke- ... -ä ("kehone" = if it is). Let us: in- + verb (inihid). Wishes: melkam (good) + noun.
