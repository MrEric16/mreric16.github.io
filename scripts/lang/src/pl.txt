INTRO: Polish looks scary but it is very regular. Learn the special letters and you can read it.
a | ah | kot
e | eh, like "bed" | tego
i | ee, like "see" | miły
o | oh, short | kot
u / ó | oo, like "food" | ulica, może
y | "ih", like the "i" in "bit" said further back | dobry
ą | nasal "on", like "own" through the nose | są
ę | nasal "en", like "en" through the nose | mam (ma), pięć
ł | w, like English "w" | mały
w | v, like English "v" | woda
j | y, like English "y" | jak
c | ts, like the end of "cats" | co
cz | ch, hard, like "church" | czas
ć / ci | ch, soft, tongue flatter | cześć
sz | sh, hard | szkoła
ś / si | sh, soft | dziękuję
rz / ż | zh, like "s" in "measure" | rzeka, żona
ź / zi | zh, soft | zima
dz | dz, like "kids" | dzwonek
dż | j, like "jam" | dżem
dź / dzi | j, soft | dzień
ń / ni | ny, like "canyon" | koń
ch / h | harsh h, like Scottish "loch" | chleb, hotel
r | rolled, tongue taps | rok

## Pronouns
> Polish often drops the subject, because the verb ending shows who is speaking. Pan / Pani are the formal "you".
row: I | ja | yah
row: you (informal) | ty | tih
row: he / she / it | on / ona / ono | on / OH-nah / OH-noh
row: we | my | mih
row: you all | wy | vih
row: they | oni / one | OH-nee / OH-neh
row: you (formal, m / f) | Pan / Pani | pahn / PAH-nee
ex: I am a student | ja jestem studentem | yah YES-tem stoo-DEN-tem
ex: you are tall | ty jesteś wysoki | tih YES-tesh vih-SOH-kee
ex: she is my sister | ona jest moją siostrą | OH-nah yest MOH-yon SHOS-tron
ex: we are friends | my jesteśmy przyjaciółmi | mih yes-TESH-mih pshih-yah-CHOOW-mee
ex: they are teachers | oni są nauczycielami | OH-nee son nah-oo-chih-cheh-LAH-mee
## Singular and plural
> Masculine: add -y or -i (or -e). Feminine: -a → -y or -i. Neuter: -o → -a. Some are irregular, so learn the plural with the word.
row: masculine | dom → domy | DOH-mih
row: masculine (soft) | kot → koty | KOH-tih
row: feminine | książka → książki | KSHONSH-kee
row: neuter | okno → okna | OK-nah
row: person (masculine) | student → studenci | stoo-DEN-chee
ex: one house, houses | dom, domy | dom DOH-mih
ex: one book, books | książka, książki | KSHONSH-kah KSHONSH-kee
ex: one window, windows | okno, okna | OK-noh OK-nah
ex: one cat, cats | kot, koty | kot KOH-tih
ex: one student, students | student, studenci | STOO-dent stoo-DEN-chee

## Basic sentences
> Order is flexible, but the usual order is subject, verb, object. Adjectives come before the noun and agree in gender and number.
row: subject + verb + object | Jem chleb. | yem hlep
row: adjective before noun | duży dom | DOO-zhih dom
row: add time or place | Pracuję tu dzisiaj. | prah-TSOO-yeh too JEE-shay
ex: I eat bread | jem chleb | yem hlep
ex: Anna reads a book | Anna czyta książkę | AHN-nah CHIH-tah KSHONSH-keh
ex: we live in a big house | mieszkamy w dużym domu | myesh-KAH-mih v DOO-zhim DOH-moo
ex: he works here today | on dzisiaj tu pracuje | on JEE-shay too prah-TSOO-yeh
ex: the dog is black | pies jest czarny | pyes yest CHAR-nih

## Formal vs informal
> ty / twój for friends. Pan / Pani for strangers, with the verb in the 3rd person (he / she form). Add "Pan" or "Pani" + name or title.
row: you, informal | ty | tih
row: you, formal (m) | Pan | pahn
row: you, formal (f) | Pani | PAH-nee
row: Mr | Pan + surname | pahn
row: Mrs / Ms | Pani + surname | PAH-nee
ex: you speak (informal) | ty mówisz | tih MOO-veesh
ex: you speak (formal, to a man) | Pan mówi | pahn MOO-vee
ex: how are you? (informal) | jak się masz? | yahk sheh mahsh
ex: how are you? (formal, to a woman) | jak się Pani ma? | yahk sheh PAH-nee mah
ex: thank you, Mr Nowak | dziękuję, Panie Nowak | jen-KOO-yeh PAH-nyeh NOH-vahk
## Past and future (simple)
> Past: verb stem + -ł + person ending (masculine) or -ła (feminine). Future: będę + infinitive.
row: I worked (m) | pracowałem | prah-tsoh-VAH-wem
row: I worked (f) | pracowałam | prah-tsoh-VAH-wahm
row: you worked (m) | pracowałeś | prah-tsoh-VAH-wesh
row: I will work | będę pracować | BEN-deh prah-TSOH-vahch
ex: yesterday I worked (m) | wczoraj pracowałem | FCHOH-ray prah-tsoh-VAH-wem
ex: she bought a book | kupiła książkę | koo-PEE-wah KSHONSH-keh
ex: I went home (m) | poszedłem do domu | poh-SHED-wem doh DOH-moo
ex: I will call you tomorrow | zadzwonię jutro | zahd-ZVOH-nyeh YOO-troh
ex: we will eat at eight | zjemy o ósmej | ZYEH-mih oh OOS-may
