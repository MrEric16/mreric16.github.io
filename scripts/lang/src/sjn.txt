[sounds]
INTRO: Sindarin is the Grey-elven language invented by J. R. R. Tolkien for The Lord of the Rings. It has no native speakers. Tolkien left only a few hundred words and a few sentences, so it cannot be used for everyday talk. This pool contains only words and short phrases that appear in his own writings, with the spelling used in the books.
dh | like "th" in "this" | edhel
th | like "th" in "thin" | thûl
ch | like "ch" in Scottish "loch" | ach
g | always hard, like "go" | galadh
c | always hard, like "k" | calen
r | rolled | ross
î / ê / â / ô / û | the vowel held long (the circumflex) | mîr
ae / ai / ei / au / ui | two vowels said as one sound | aran
NOTE: Words like "mae govannen" are real phrases from the books. Many others in fan lists are invented by fans and are not Tolkien's. Only words with a clear source are shown here.
NOTE: This pool is much smaller than the Spanish one on purpose. A few forms are uncertain and nothing has been checked by an expert in Tolkien's languages. Treat it as a first guide for a hobby language.

[grammar]
## Pronouns and possessives
> The article "i" means "the". Plurals are usually made by changing the vowel: adan to edain (man), edhel to edhil (elf). This is called i-affection. "Im" is I. "Le" is you (polite). Possessives follow the noun: "nîn" (my) and "lín" (your).
row: I | im | eem
row: you (polite) | le | leh
row: the | i | ee
row: my | nîn | neen
row: your | lín | leen
ex: the man | i adan | ee AH-dan
ex: the men | i edain | ee EH-dine
ex: the elf | i edhel | ee EH-thel
ex: the elves | i edhil | ee EH-thil
ex: my friend | mellon nîn | MEL-lon neen
ex: your friend | mellon lín | MEL-lon leen
