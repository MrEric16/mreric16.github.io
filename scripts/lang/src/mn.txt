NAME: Mongolian
ID: mn
FLAG: mn

[sounds]
INTRO: This is Khalkha Mongolian, the standard language of Mongolia, written in Cyrillic. Vowels follow harmony: back vowels (а, о, у, ы) and front vowels (э, ө, ү, и) do not mix in one word.
ө | like "ur" in "fur", rounded lips | өдөр (UH-dur)
ү | like German "ü" | үг (ug)
х | kh, like Scottish "loch" | хоол (khool)
г | g (at the end of a word it is soft, like a voiced "kh") | гэр (ger)
ж | j, like "judge" | жаргал (jar-GAL)
з | dz, like "adze" | зуун (dzoon)
ц | ts, like "cats" | цай (tsai)
ч | ch, like "church" | чи (chee)
ш | sh | шөнө (SHU-nu)
ы | like "ee" in "see" said further back | ы (ee)
ъ / ь | a sign with no sound: it only shows hard or soft | хэлэх (KHE-lekh)
NOTE: Long vowels are written double: аа, ээ, оо, өө, уу, үү. Stress falls on the first syllable, unless the next syllable has a long vowel.
NOTE: Mongolian has no gender and no articles. The verb comes last. Mongolian is written in Cyrillic, so there is no separate transliteration.

## Formal and informal
