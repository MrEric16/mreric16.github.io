[sounds]
INTRO: Na'vi is the language of the blue people of Pandora in the film Avatar. It was invented by the linguist Paul Frommer for the film. It has no native speakers, but fans use it and it has a full grammar. This pool holds only words and phrases that are confirmed in Frommer's published language and the films.
tx / px / kx | sharp "t", "p", "k" said with a pop, like a small click of air | kaltxì
ì | a short, tense "i", like "i" in "sit" | kaltxì
ä | like "a" in "cat" | ngeyä
e | like "e" in "bed" | kameie
ll / rr | a short, light "l" or rolled "r" that forms a syllable | pxel
' | a catch in the throat, as in "uh-oh" | 'awve
ng | like "ng" in "sing", also at the start of a word | ngati
ts | like "ts" in "cats", also at the start of a word | tsmukan
NOTE: Na'vi counts in eights, not tens, so the number after 7 is "vol" (8).
NOTE: This pool is small on purpose. Nothing here has been checked by the author of the language. The pronunciations are simple English-style guides, not exact.
