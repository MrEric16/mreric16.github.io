[sounds]
INTRO: Italian is spelled almost exactly as it sounds. Vowels are pure and short, and every letter is pronounced.
a | ah | casa
e | eh, like "bed" (or "ay") | bene
i | ee, like "see" | sì
o | oh, like "more" | solo
u | oo, like "food" | uno
c (a, o, u) | k | casa
c (e, i) | ch, like "church" | ciao
ch | k | che
g (a, o, u) | hard g, like "go" | gatto
g (e, i) | j, like "gem" | gelato
gh | hard g | spaghetti
sc (e, i) | sh | pesce
sci | sh | sciarpa
gli | ly, like "million" | figlio
gn | ny, like "canyon" | gnocchi
h | silent | ho
z | ts or dz | pizza
r | rolled, tongue taps | rosso
double letters | hold the sound a little longer | pizza, nonno

## Pronouns
> Italian drops subject pronouns most of the time, because the verb ending shows who is speaking.
row: I | io | EE-oh
row: you (informal) | tu | too
row: he / she | lui / lei | LOO-ee / lay
row: we | noi | noy
row: you all | voi | voy
row: they | loro | LOH-roh
row: you (formal) | Lei | lay
ex: I am a student | io sono studente | EE-oh SOH-noh stoo-DEHN-teh
ex: you are tall | tu sei alto | too say AHL-toh
ex: she is my sister | lei è mia sorella | lay eh MEE-ah soh-REHL-lah
ex: we are friends | noi siamo amici | noy SYAH-moh ah-MEE-chee
ex: they are teachers | loro sono insegnanti | LOH-roh SOH-noh een-sehn-YAHN-tee
## Singular and plural
> Masculine -o → -i. Feminine -a → -e. Words in -e → -i. The article changes too: il → i, la → le, lo → gli.
row: -o to -i (m) | il libro → i libri | ee LEE-bree
row: -a to -e (f) | la casa → le case | leh KAH-zeh
row: -e to -i | il cane → i cani | ee KAH-nee
row: lo to gli | lo studente → gli studenti | lyee stoo-DEHN-tee
ex: one book, two books | un libro, due libri | oon LEE-broh DOO-eh LEE-bree
ex: one house, houses | una casa, le case | OO-nah KAH-zah leh KAH-zeh
ex: one dog, dogs | un cane, i cani | oon KAH-neh ee KAH-nee
ex: one student, students | uno studente, gli studenti | OO-noh stoo-DEHN-teh lyee stoo-DEHN-tee
ex: one friend (f), friends | un'amica, le amiche | oon-ah-MEE-kah leh ah-MEE-keh

row: lui / lei | parla | PAR-lah

## Basic sentences
> Order: subject, verb, rest. Adjectives usually come after the noun and agree with it. You can drop the subject.
row: subject + verb + rest | Mangio il pane. | MAHN-joh eel PAH-neh
row: adjective after noun | una casa grande | OO-nah KAH-zah GRAHN-deh
row: add time or place at the end | Lavoro qui oggi. | lah-VOH-roh kwee OHD-jee
ex: I eat bread | mangio il pane | MAHN-joh eel PAH-neh
ex: Maria reads a book | Maria legge un libro | mah-REE-ah LEJ-jeh oon LEE-broh
ex: we live in a big house | abitiamo in una casa grande | ah-bee-TYAH-moh een OO-nah KAH-zah GRAHN-deh
ex: he works here today | lavora qui oggi | lah-VOH-rah kwee OHD-jee
ex: the dog is black | il cane è nero | eel KAH-neh eh NEH-roh
## Negation
> Put non before the verb. Double negatives are normal: non ho niente.

## Asking questions

row: add "right?" | vero? | VEH-roh
ex: do you speak English? | parli inglese? | PAR-lee een-GLEH-zeh
ex: is she a doctor? | è un medico? | eh oon MEH-dee-koh

ex: are you tired? | sei stanco? | say STAHN-koh
ex: is it far? | è lontano? | eh lohn-TAH-noh

## Formal vs informal
> tu / tuo for friends. Lei / suo for strangers. Lei is not "she" here: it is the polite you, and the verb is in the he / she form.
row: you, informal | tu | too
row: you, formal | Lei | lay
row: your, informal | tuo / tua | TOO-oh / TOO-ah
row: your, formal | suo / sua | SOO-oh / SOO-ah
ex: you speak (informal) | tu parli | too PAR-lee
ex: you speak (formal) | Lei parla | lay PAR-lah
ex: how are you? (informal) | come stai? | KOH-meh sty
ex: how are you? (formal) | come sta? | KOH-meh stah
ex: thank you, sir | grazie, signore | GRAHT-see-eh seen-YOH-reh
## Past and future (simple)
> Past: avere (or essere) + past participle: ho parlato, sono andato. Future: infinitive + -ò, -ai, -à, -emo, -ete, -anno.
row: I spoke | ho parlato | oh par-LAH-toh
row: I ate | ho mangiato | oh mahn-JAH-toh
row: I went | sono andato | SOH-noh ahn-DAH-toh
row: I will speak | parlerò | par-leh-ROH
ex: yesterday I worked | ieri ho lavorato | YEH-ree oh lah-voh-RAH-toh
ex: she bought a book | ha comprato un libro | ah kohm-PRAH-toh oon LEE-broh
ex: I went home | sono tornato a casa | SOH-noh tor-NAH-toh ah KAH-zah
ex: I will call you tomorrow | ti chiamerò domani | tee kyah-meh-ROH doh-MAH-nee
ex: we will eat at eight | mangeremo alle otto | mahn-jeh-REH-moh AHL-leh OT-toh
