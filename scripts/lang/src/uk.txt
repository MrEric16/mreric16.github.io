[sounds]
INTRO: Ukrainian uses Cyrillic, but it differs from Russian in a few letters and sounds. Learn these first.
і | ee, like "see" | ліс (lees)
и | "ih", like the "i" in "bit" | мир (mihr)
ї | yee | їжа (YEE-zhah)
є | ye, like "yes" | є (ye)
г | h, soft breathy (not "g") | гора (hoh-RAH)
ґ | g, hard, rare letter | ґанок (GAH-nok)
е | eh | мене (meh-NEH)
щ | shch | щастя (SHCHAHS-tyah)
ь | soft sign: softens the consonant before it | день (den')
' | apostrophe: hard break before я, ю, є, ї | м'ясо (MYAH-so)
ч | ch | час (chahs)
ж | zh | жінка (ZHEEN-kah)
в | v (at the end of a word or before a consonant it sounds close to "w") | вода (voh-DAH)
о | oh (unstressed it is still "o", not "a" as in Russian) | молоко (moh-loh-KOH)

## Pronouns
> Ukrainian often drops the pronoun, but learn them. ви is both formal "you" and plural "you".
row: I | я | yah
row: you (informal) | ти | tih
row: he | він | veen
row: she | вона | voh-NAH
row: it | воно | voh-NOH
row: we | ми | mih
row: you (formal / plural) | ви | vih
row: they | вони | voh-NIH
ex: I am a student | я студент | yah stoo-DENT
ex: you are tall | ти високий | tih vih-SOH-kihy
ex: she is my sister | вона моя сестра | voh-NAH moh-YAH sehs-TRAH
ex: we are friends | ми друзі | mih DROO-zee
ex: they are teachers | вони вчителі | voh-NIH VCHIH-teh-lee
## Singular and plural
> Masculine: add -и or -і (or -ы after hard consonants). Feminine -а → -и. Neuter -о → -а. Some words are irregular.
row: masculine | стіл → столи | steel → stoh-LIH
row: masculine | брат → брати | braht → BRAH-tih
row: feminine | книга → книги | KNIH-hah → KNIH-hih
row: feminine (soft) | неділя → неділі | neh-DEE-lya → neh-DEE-lee
row: neuter | вікно → вікна | veek-NOH → VEEK-nah
row: irregular | людина → люди | lyoo-DIH-nah → LYOO-dih
ex: one table, tables | стіл, столи | steel stoh-LIH
ex: one book, books | книга, книги | KNIH-hah KNIH-hih
ex: one window, windows | вікно, вікна | veek-NOH VEEK-nah
ex: one friend, friends | друг, друзі | drooh DROO-zee
ex: one child, children | дитина, діти | dih-TIH-nah DEE-tih
## Бути: to be
> In the present tense "to be" is usually dropped: Я студент. (I am a student). Use є for "there is" or for emphasis.
row: I am | я є / (omitted) | yah ye
row: you are | ти є / (omitted) | tih ye
row: he / she is | він / вона є / (omitted) | veen ye
row: we are | ми є / (omitted) | mih ye
row: they are | вони є / (omitted) | voh-NIH ye
ex: I am English | я англієць | yah ahn-HLEE-yets'
ex: he is a doctor | він лікар | veen LEE-kahr
ex: we are tired | ми втомлені | mih VTOM-leh-nee
ex: are you from Kyiv? | ти з Києва? | tih z KIH-yeh-vah
ex: they are at home | вони вдома | voh-NIH VDOH-mah

## Regular verbs (present tense)
> Verbs end in -ти. Many have -ю, -єш, -є, -ємо, -єте, -ють. Працювати (to work):

## Basic sentences
> Word order is flexible: usually subject, verb, object. Adjectives come before the noun and agree with it.
row: subject + verb + object | Я їм хліб. | yah yeem khleeb
row: adjective before noun | великий будинок | veh-LIH-kihy boo-DIH-nok
row: add time or place | Я працюю тут сьогодні. | yah prah-TSYOO-yoo toot s'oh-HOD-nee
ex: I eat bread | я їм хліб | yah yeem khleeb
ex: Anna reads a book | Анна читає книгу | AHN-nah chih-TAH-yeh KNIH-hoo
ex: we live in a big house | ми живемо у великому будинку | mih zhih-VEH-moh oo veh-LIH-koh-moo boo-DIN-koo
ex: he works here today | він сьогодні працює тут | veen s'oh-HOD-nee prah-TSYOO-yeh toot
ex: the dog is black | собака чорна | soh-BAH-kah CHOR-nah

## Asking questions

## Formal vs informal
> ти for friends, family and children. ви for strangers, elders, teachers, and always for more than one person. Add the name and patronymic when you want to be very polite.
row: you, informal | ти | tih
row: you, formal / plural | ви | vih
row: your, informal | твій | tveey
row: your, formal | ваш | vahsh
ex: you speak (informal) | ти говориш | tih hoh-VOH-rihsh
ex: you speak (formal) | ви говорите | vih hoh-VOH-rih-teh
ex: how are you? (informal) | як ти? | yahk tih
ex: how are you? (formal) | як ви? | yahk vih
ex: thank you, Mr Ivanenko | дякую, пане Іваненко | DYAH-koo-yoo PAH-neh ee-vah-NEN-koh
