INTRO: Hindi spelling in Latin letters is easy to read once you know the special sounds.
a | short "uh", like "about" | kab
aa | long "ah", like "father" | aam
i | short "i", like "sit" | in
ee | long "ee", like "see" | eent
u | short "oo", like "put" | un
oo | long "oo", like "food" | door
e | "ay", like "say" | ek
ai | like "bad" (short "a") | hai
o | "oh" | aur
au | like "aw" in "law" | aur
th | t with a puff of air, no English "th" | thanda (cold)
dh | d with a puff of air | dhoop
kh, gh, ch, jh | with a puff of air | khaana, ghar
t / d | tongue touches the back of the teeth | tum, dil
T / D | tongue curled back (retroflex) | Thanda, Dar
r | quick tap | raat
n (end) | nasal, say it through the nose | hain

## Formal and informal

## Pronouns
> Hindi has no he / she difference in the pronoun for "he, she, it": vah. The verb shows the gender. aap is the polite "you".
row: I | main | main
row: you (very close) | tu | too
row: you (friendly) | tum | toom
row: you (polite) | aap | aap
row: he / she / it | vah (woh) | woh
row: this (he / she / it, near) | yeh | yeh
row: we | hum | hoom
row: they | ve (woh) | woh
ex: I am a student | main vidyaarthi hoon | main vid-YAAR-thee hoon
ex: you are tall | tum lambe ho | toom LUM-beh hoh
ex: she is my sister | vah meri behen hai | woh MEH-ree BAY-hen hai
ex: we are friends | hum dost hain | hoom dohst hain
ex: they are teachers | ve adhyaapak hain | woh uh-DHYAA-puk hain
## Singular and plural
> Masculine ending -aa becomes -e (ladkaa → ladke). Feminine -ee or -i becomes -iyaan (ladki → ladkiyaan), other feminine nouns add -en (kitaab → kitaaben). Many masculine nouns do not change (ghar).
row: masculine -aa | ladkaa → ladke | LUD-kaa → LUD-keh
row: masculine (no change) | ghar → ghar | gur
row: feminine -ee | ladki → ladkiyaan | LUD-kee → LUD-kee-yaan
row: feminine | kitaab → kitaaben | ki-TAAB → ki-TAA-ben
row: feminine | aurat → auraten | OW-rut → OW-ru-ten
ex: one boy, boys | ladkaa, ladke | LUD-kaa LUD-keh
ex: one girl, girls | ladki, ladkiyaan | LUD-kee LUD-kee-yaan
ex: one book, books | kitaab, kitaaben | ki-TAAB ki-TAA-ben
ex: one house, houses | ghar, ghar | gur gur
ex: one room, rooms | kamra, kamre | KUM-raa KUM-reh
