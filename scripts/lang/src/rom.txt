INTRO: Romani is the language of the Roma people, who live across Europe and beyond. It comes from India (it is a cousin of Hindi and Punjabi) and has many dialects: Vlax, Balkan, Sinti, Carpathian and more. This pack uses a Vlax-style (Kalderash) spelling with a Latin respelling in the "say" line. Dialects differ a lot in vocabulary and sound.
č | "ch" as in "church" | čhavo
š | "sh" as in "shop" | šukar
ž | "s" as in "measure" | žanav
x | "kh" like "ch" in Scottish "loch" | xav
ʰ | a puff of breath after the letter | čhon
NOTE: Many Roma are bilingual, and the spelling here is one of several in use. There is no single standard Romani. Expect real speakers to say words differently.
NOTE: This pool is much smaller than the Spanish one on purpose and has not been checked by a Romani speaker.

[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Romani verbs change by person: -av (I), -es (you), -el (he/she), -as (we), -en (you pl. and they) in the present for most verbs.
row: I | me | meh
row: you | tu | too
row: he | vo | vo
row: she | voj | voy
row: we | amen | A-men
row: they | von | von
ex: I am a rom | Me som rom | meh som rom
ex: you are a student | Tu san studento | too san stoo-DEN-to
ex: he is a teacher | Vo si sikavno | vo see SEE-kav-no
## Present tense (xa- to eat)
> Take the stem and add the person ending.
row: I eat | xav | khav
row: you eat | xas | khas
row: he eats | xal | khal
row: we eat | xas | khas
row: they eat | xan | khan
ex: I eat bread | Me xav maro | meh khav MA-ro
ex: he eats meat | Vo xal mas | vo khal mas
ex: we eat | Amen xas | A-men khas
## Negation
> Put "na" before the verb: "me na xav" (I do not eat). "Nane" means "there is not". For a command, use "ma": "ma xa" (do not eat).
row: not | na | na
row: there is not | nane | NA-neh
row: do not (command) | ma | ma
ex: I eat | Me xav | meh khav
ex: I do not eat | Me na xav | meh na khav
ex: eat! | Xa! | kha
ex: do not eat! | Ma xa! | ma kha
