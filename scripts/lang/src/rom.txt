NAME: Romani (Romani čhib)
ID: rom
FLAG: rom

[sounds]
INTRO: Romani is the language of the Roma people, who live across Europe and beyond. It comes from India (it is a cousin of Hindi and Punjabi) and has many dialects: Vlax, Balkan, Sinti, Carpathian and more. This pack uses a Vlax-style (Kalderash) spelling with a Latin respelling in the "say" line. Dialects differ a lot in vocabulary and sound.
č | "ch" as in "church" | čhavo
š | "sh" as in "shop" | šukar
ž | "s" as in "measure" | žanav
x | "kh", like "ch" in Scottish "loch" | xav
ʰ | a puff of breath after the letter (kh, ph, th, čh) | čhon
NOTE: Many Roma are bilingual, and there is no single standard Romani. Expect real speakers to say words differently. Modern words (airport, university, phone) are usually borrowed from the local language, so they differ from country to country.
NOTE: The pool is unreviewed by a Romani speaker. Everyday words are Vlax. Numbers above 100 and school words are the most uncertain.

## Pronouns
> Romani has separate subject and object forms. Subject: me, tu, vov / voj, amen, tumen, on. "Tu" is informal and "tumen" is the polite you, as well as the plural.
