[sounds]
INTRO: Portuguese swallows many unstressed vowels, so it sounds quicker than it looks. Learn these sounds first.
a | ah (a weak "uh" when unstressed) | casa
e | eh when stressed; a short "uh" or silent when unstressed | café, de
i | ee | si
o | oh when stressed; "oo" at the end of a word | todo, livro
u | oo | tu
ã / õ | nasal vowels: air goes through the nose, no n sound | irmão, limões
ão | nasal "ow", like "ow" in "now" through the nose | pão
nh | ny, like "canyon" | vinho
lh | ly, like "million" | filho
ch | sh | chave
x | usually sh | peixe
j / g (e, i) | zh, like "s" in "measure" | janela, gente
g (a, o, u) | hard g | gato
ç | s | maçã
c (e, i) | s | cinco
c (a, o, u) | k | casa
qu | k (before e, i) | queijo
s (between vowels) | z | casa
s (end of word) | sh (in Portugal) | pais
r (start of word), rr | guttural, like French r | rio, carro
r (middle) | one quick tap | caro
h | silent | hoje

## Formal and informal
> tu (informal): friends, family, young people. o senhor / a senhora (formal): strangers, older people, work. With the formal "you", the verb is in the he / she form. Você is common but can feel cool in Portugal. Use o senhor or a senhora when unsure.

## Pronouns
> The pronoun is often dropped, because the verb ending shows who is speaking. For formal "you", use o senhor / a senhora.
row: I | eu | EH-oo
row: you (informal) | tu | too
row: he / she | ele / ela | EH-l / EH-lah
row: we | nós | nohsh
row: you all (informal) | vocês | voh-SAYSH
row: they | eles / elas | EH-lsh / EH-lahsh
row: you (formal) | o senhor / a senhora | oo seh-NYOR / ah seh-NYOH-rah
ex: I am a student | eu sou estudante | EH-oo soh shtoo-DAHN-t
ex: you are tall | tu és alto | too ehsh AHL-too
ex: she is my sister | ela é minha irmã | EH-lah eh MEEN-yah eer-MAHN
ex: we are friends | nós somos amigos | nohsh SOH-moosh ah-MEE-goosh
ex: they are teachers | eles são professores | EH-lsh sown proo-feh-SOH-rsh
## Singular and plural
> Most plurals add -s: casa → casas. Words ending in a consonant add -es. Words in -ão change: pão → pães, mão → mãos, limão → limões. Words in -l change: animal → animais. Articles change: o → os, a → as, um → uns, uma → umas.
row: the (m / f) | o / a | oo / ah
row: the (plural) | os / as | oosh / ahsh
row: a (m / f) | um / uma | oom / OO-mah
row: add -s | casa → casas | KAH-zahsh
row: add -es (consonant) | flor → flores | FLOH-rsh
row: -ão changes | pão → pães | paynsh
ex: one cat, two cats | um gato, dois gatos | oom GAH-too doysh GAH-toosh
ex: the book, the books | o livro, os livros | oo LEE-vroo oosh LEE-vroosh
ex: a girl, girls | uma rapariga, raparigas | OO-mah rah-pah-REE-gah rah-pah-REE-gahsh
ex: the flower, the flowers | a flor, as flores | ah flor ahsh FLOH-rsh
ex: the bread, the loaves | o pão, os pães | oo pown oosh paynsh
