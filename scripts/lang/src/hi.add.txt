## Negation
> Put nahin before the verb (usually right before "hai" or the main verb): main nahin jaata. "There is not" = nahin hai. In the present tense the "hona" part may be dropped: main nahin jaata (I do not go).
row: not | nahin | nuh-HEEN | नहीं
row: there is not | nahin hai | nuh-HEEN hai | नहीं है
row: nothing | kuchh nahin | kooch nuh-HEEN | कुछ नहीं
row: nobody | koi nahin | KOH-ee nuh-HEEN | कोई नहीं
ex: I want | mujhe chahiye | MOO-jheh chaa-HEE-yeh | मुझे चाहिए
ex: I do not want | mujhe nahin chahiye | MOO-jheh nuh-HEEN chaa-HEE-yeh | मुझे नहीं चाहिए
ex: I speak English | main angrezi bolta hoon | main un-gray-ZEE BOL-taa hoon | मैं अंग्रेज़ी बोलता हूँ
ex: I do not speak English | main angrezi nahin bolta | main un-gray-ZEE nuh-HEEN BOL-taa | मैं अंग्रेज़ी नहीं बोलता
ex: I like it | mujhe yeh pasand hai | MOO-jheh yeh pu-SUND hai | मुझे यह पसंद है
ex: I do not like it | mujhe yeh pasand nahin hai | MOO-jheh yeh pu-SUND nuh-HEEN hai | मुझे यह पसंद नहीं है
ex: she is here | vah yahaan hai | woh yuh-HAAN hai | वह यहाँ है
ex: she is not here | vah yahaan nahin hai | woh yuh-HAAN nuh-HEEN hai | वह यहाँ नहीं है
ex: I have a car | mere paas gaadi hai | MEH-reh paas GAA-dee hai | मेरे पास गाड़ी है
ex: I do not have a car | mere paas gaadi nahin hai | MEH-reh paas GAA-dee nuh-HEEN hai | मेरे पास गाड़ी नहीं है
ex: I understand | main samajhta hoon | main suh-MUJH-taa hoon | मैं समझता हूँ
ex: I do not understand | main nahin samajhta | main nuh-HEEN suh-MUJH-taa | मैं नहीं समझता

## Can, must, want

row: I can | main sakta hoon | main SUK-taa hoon | मैं सकता हूँ
row: I must | mujhe ... jaana hoga | MOO-jheh ... jaa-NAA HOH-gaa | मुझे ... जाना होगा
row: I want to | main ... chahta hoon | main CHAAH-taa hoon | मैं ... चाहता हूँ
ex: I can swim | main tair sakta hoon | main tair SUK-taa hoon | मैं तैर सकता हूँ
ex: I cannot swim | main tair nahin sakta | main tair nuh-HEEN SUK-taa | मैं तैर नहीं सकता

## Comparing things
> Add se (than) after the second thing, and zyaada (more): mujhse bada (bigger than me). Most = sabse + adjective: sabse bada.
row: bigger than | se bada | seh BUH-daa | से बड़ा
row: the biggest | sabse bada | SUB-seh BUH-daa | सबसे बड़ा
row: better | behtar | beh-TUR | बेहतर
ex: big, bigger, the biggest | bada, usse bada, sabse bada | BUH-daa OOS-seh BUH-daa SUB-seh BUH-daa | बड़ा, उससे बड़ा, सबसे बड़ा
ex: small, smaller, the smallest | chhota, usse chhota, sabse chhota | CHHOH-taa OOS-seh CHHOH-taa SUB-seh CHHOH-taa | छोटा, उससे छोटा, सबसे छोटा
