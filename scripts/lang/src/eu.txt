[sounds]
INTRO: Basque (Euskara) is spoken in the Basque Country. It is unrelated to any other language in the world. This is standard Basque (Batua). It is spelled quite regularly.
a | ah, like "father" | amona
e | eh, like "bed" | etxe
i | ee, like "see" | ibai
o | oh, like "more" | ona
u | oo, like "food" | urdina
tx | ch, like "church" | etxe
x | sh, like "ship" | kaixo
ts / tz | ts, like "cats" | mutil
z | s (a sharp, hissy s) | zu
s | s (softer, a bit like "sh") | sagar
j | y, like "yes" (in some regions a harsh h) | jaten
h | silent, never pronounced | hori
rr | strongly rolled r | gorri
ll, ñ | ly, ny (as in Spanish) | mutil
NOTE: Stress is light and falls usually on the second syllable of a word, but it varies by region. The say column shows approximate sounds.
NOTE: The verb goes last. Basque uses cases (endings) instead of prepositions: etxe (house), etxean (in the house), etxera (to the house).

## Can, must, want
