[sounds]
INTRO: Turkish is spelled exactly as it sounds. There are no genders and no articles. Learn the special letters first.
a | ah, like "father" | araba
e | eh, like "bed" | ev
i | ee, like "see" | iki
ı (no dot) | a short, flat "uh", like the "a" in "about" | kız
o | oh, like "more" | okul
ö | say "oh" with rounded lips, like German ö | göz
u | oo, like "food" | uzun
ü | say "ee" with rounded lips, like German ü | gül
c | j, like "jam" | cam
ç | ch, like "church" | çay
ş | sh | şeker
j | zh, like "s" in "measure" | jandarma
ğ | silent, makes the vowel before it longer | dağ
h | breathy h, always pronounced | hava
r | tapped, tongue touches the ridge | araba
y | y, like English "y" | yarın

## Pronouns
> Turkish drops the pronoun most of the time, because the verb ending shows who is speaking. Pronouns are for emphasis.

row: you (informal) | sen | sen
row: he / she / it | o | oh
row: we | biz | beez
row: you all / you (formal) | siz | seez
row: they | onlar | on-LAR
ex: I am a student | ben öğrenciyim | ben uh-ren-jee-YEEM
ex: you are tall | sen uzunsun | sen oo-zoon-SOON
ex: she is my sister | o benim kız kardeşim | oh beh-NEEM kuhz kar-deh-SHEEM
ex: we are friends | biz arkadaşız | beez ar-kah-dah-SUHZ
ex: they are teachers | onlar öğretmen | on-LAR uh-ret-MEN
## Singular and plural
> Add -ler or -lar. Use -ler after e, i, ö, ü, and -lar after a, ı, o, u (vowel harmony). After a number, the noun stays singular: iki kitap (two book).
row: ev (house) | evler | ev-LER
row: kitap (book) | kitaplar | kee-tahp-LAR
row: göz (eye) | gözler | gurz-LER
row: after a number: singular | iki kitap | ee-KEE kee-TAHP
ex: one house, houses | bir ev, evler | beer ev ev-LER
ex: one book, books | bir kitap, kitaplar | beer kee-TAHP kee-tahp-LAR
ex: one cat, cats | bir kedi, kediler | beer keh-DEE keh-dee-LER
ex: two books | iki kitap | ee-KEE kee-TAHP
ex: the children | çocuklar | choh-jook-LAR
## To be: -im, -sin, -dir
> There is no separate verb "to be". Add an ending to the noun or adjective. After a vowel, a buffer y appears.
row: I am | -im / -ım / -um / -üm | eem
row: you are | -sin / -sın / -sun / -sün | seen
row: he / she is | (no ending) or -dir | deer
row: we are | -iz / -ız / -uz / -üz | eez
row: you all are | -siniz | see-NEEZ
row: they are | -ler / -lar | ler
ex: I am a student | öğrenciyim | uh-ren-jee-YEEM
ex: you are tired | yorgunsun | yor-goon-SOON
ex: he is a doctor | o doktor | oh dok-TOR
ex: we are happy | mutluyuz | moot-loo-YOOZ
ex: they are at home | onlar evde | on-LAR ev-DEH
## Var and yok: have, there is
> Turkish says "my X exists" (var) or "my X does not exist" (yok). Ending shows the owner: -im (my), -in (your), -i (his).
row: there is | var | var
row: there is not | yok | yok
row: I have | ... -im var | var
row: I do not have | ... -im yok | yok
ex: I have a brother | erkek kardeşim var | er-KEK kar-deh-SHEEM var
ex: I have a car | arabam var | ah-rah-BAHM var
ex: I do not have time | zamanım yok | zah-mah-NUHM yok
ex: there is a problem | bir sorun var | beer soh-ROON var
ex: there is no water | su yok | soo yok
## Present tense: -iyor
> Verb stem + -iyor + person ending. Gelmek (to come): gel-iyor-um.
row: I come | geliyorum | geh-lee-yoh-ROOM
row: you come | geliyorsun | geh-lee-yor-SOON
row: he / she comes | geliyor | geh-lee-YOR
row: we come | geliyoruz | geh-lee-yoh-ROOZ
row: you all come | geliyorsunuz | geh-lee-yor-soo-NOOZ
row: they come | geliyorlar | geh-lee-yor-LAR
ex: I am eating bread | ekmek yiyorum | ek-MEK yee-yoh-ROOM
ex: you are reading a book | kitap okuyorsun | kee-TAHP oh-koo-yor-SOON
ex: she works in a bank | bankada çalışıyor | bahn-kah-DAH chah-luh-shuh-YOR
ex: we are going home | eve gidiyoruz | eh-VEH gee-dee-yoh-ROOZ
ex: they are drinking tea | çay içiyorlar | chye ee-chee-yor-LAR
## Basic sentences
> Verb comes last. Order: subject, time, place, object, verb. Adjectives come before the noun.
row: subject + object + verb | Ben ekmek yiyorum. | ben ek-MEK yee-yoh-ROOM
row: adjective before noun | büyük ev | bew-YEWK ev
row: time and place first | Bugün okulda çalışıyorum. | boo-GEWN oh-kool-DAH chah-luh-shuh-yoh-ROOM
ex: I eat bread | ekmek yiyorum | ek-MEK yee-yoh-ROOM
ex: Ali reads a book | Ali kitap okuyor | AH-lee kee-TAHP oh-koo-YOR
ex: we live in a big house | büyük bir evde yaşıyoruz | bew-YEWK beer ev-DEH yah-shuh-yoh-ROOZ
ex: he works here today | bugün burada çalışıyor | boo-GEWN boo-rah-DAH chah-luh-shuh-YOR
ex: the dog is black | köpek siyah | kur-PEK see-YAH
## Negation
> Verbs: add -ma / -me after the stem. Nouns and adjectives: değil (is not). Var → yok.
row: not (verb) | -ma / -me | mah / meh
row: is not | değil | deh-EEL
row: there is not | yok | yok
row: nothing | hiçbir şey | heech-BEER shay
row: never | asla / hiç | AHS-lah / heech
## Asking questions
> Add the little word mi / mı / mu / mü after the word you ask about. It follows vowel harmony. No change of word order.
row: statement | Türkçe konuşuyorsun. | tewrk-CHEH koh-noo-shoo-yor-SOON
row: question | Türkçe konuşuyor musun? | tewrk-CHEH koh-noo-shoo-YOR moo-SOON
row: after a noun or adjective | pahalı mı? | pah-hah-LUH muh
ex: do you speak English? | İngilizce konuşuyor musun? | een-gee-leez-JEH koh-noo-shoo-YOR moo-SOON
ex: is she a doctor? | o doktor mu? | oh dok-TOR moo
ex: do you have a pen? | kalemin var mı? | kah-leh-MEEN var muh
ex: are you tired? | yorgun musun? | yor-GOON moo-SOON
ex: is it far? | uzak mı? | oo-ZAHK muh

## Possessives
> Add an ending to the noun: -im (my), -in (your), -i (his / her), -imiz (our), -iniz (your pl.), -leri (their). Vowel harmony changes the vowel.
row: my | -im / -ım / -um / -üm | eem
row: your | -in / -ın / -un / -ün | een
row: his / her | -i / -ı / -u / -ü | ee
row: our | -imiz / -ımız | ee-meez
ex: my house | evim | eh-VEEM
ex: your friends | arkadaşların | ar-kah-dahsh-lah-RUHN
ex: his sister | kız kardeşi | kuhz kar-deh-SHEE
ex: our teacher | öğretmenimiz | uh-ret-meh-nee-MEEZ
ex: my mother | annem | AHN-nem
## Formal vs informal
> sen (and -sin, -im) for friends. siz (and -siniz) for strangers, older people, and groups. Add -ler or "Bey" (Mr) / "Hanım" (Mrs) after names for respect.
row: you, informal | sen | sen
row: you, formal | siz | seez
row: Mr | Bey (after the name) | bay
row: Mrs / Ms | Hanım (after the name) | hah-NUHM
ex: you speak (informal) | konuşuyorsun | koh-noo-shoo-yor-SOON
ex: you speak (formal) | konuşuyorsunuz | koh-noo-shoo-yor-soo-NOOZ
ex: how are you? (informal) | nasılsın? | nah-suhl-SUHN
ex: how are you? (formal) | nasılsınız? | nah-suhl-suh-NUHZ
ex: thank you, Mr Ali | teşekkürler Ali Bey | tesh-ek-kewr-LER ah-LEE bay
## Past and future (simple)
> Past: stem + -di + person ending (-m, -n, -, -k, -niz, -ler). Future: stem + -ecek / -acak + person ending.
row: I went | gittim | geet-TEEM
row: you went | gittin | geet-TEEN
row: he / she went | gitti | geet-TEE
row: I will go | gideceğim | gee-deh-jeh-EEM
row: we will go | gideceğiz | gee-deh-jeh-EEZ
ex: yesterday I worked | dün çalıştım | dewn chah-luhsh-TUHM
ex: she bought a book | bir kitap aldı | beer kee-TAHP ahl-DUH
ex: I went home | eve gittim | eh-VEH geet-TEEM
ex: I will call you tomorrow | yarın seni arayacağım | yah-RUHN seh-NEE ah-rah-yah-jah-UHM
ex: we will eat at eight | saat sekizde yiyeceğiz | sah-AHT seh-keez-DEH yee-yeh-jeh-EEZ
