NAME: Toki Pona
ID: tok
FLAG: tok

[sounds]
INTRO: Toki Pona has only 14 letters and about 130 core words. Every letter has one sound. Names are made to fit the sounds and are written with a capital letter.
a | ah, like "father" | pona
e | eh, like "bed" | mije
i | ee, like "see" | ilo
o | oh, like "more" | toki
u | oo, like "food" | suli
j | y, like "yes" | jan
k | k, like "kit" | kala
l | l, like "lip" | lipu
m | m, like "man" | moku
n | n, like "no" (at the end of a syllable it is a nasal "n") | nasa
p | p, like "pen" (no puff of air) | pan
s | s, like "sun" | sona
t | t, like "top" | tawa
w | w, like "water" | wile
NOTE: The stress is always on the first syllable: TO-ki PO-na. Every syllable is a consonant plus a vowel (and sometimes n). The letter n is never followed by m or n.
NOTE: The words do not change. There is no plural, no tense and no gender. Words are joined together to make new meanings, so many English words below are two or three Toki Pona words.

## Formal and informal
> Toki Pona has no formal or informal speech. Everybody is "sina" (you). To be polite, use "o" (please / let) and "pona".

## Pronouns
> Only three pronouns: mi (I, we), sina (you), ona (he, she, it, they). To show "we", "you all" or "they", add "mute" (many): mi mute. After mi or sina alone, "li" is dropped; after anything else it stays.
