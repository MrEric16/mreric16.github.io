[sounds]
INTRO: This is Old English (Anglo-Saxon) as written around the year 1000, the language of Beowulf and King Alfred. It looks foreign but its words are the roots of English. The spelling here is the normalised one used in textbooks, with bars over long vowels. Nobody speaks it, so the pronunciation is a scholars' reconstruction.
þ / ð | like "th" in "thing" or "this" | þæt, ðæt
æ | like "a" in "cat" | æppel
ā / ē / ī / ō / ū | the same vowels, held long | hūs
ċ | like "ch" in "church" | ċild
ġ | like "y" in "yes" | ġē
sc | like "sh" in "ship" | scip
h | a rough breath at the start; like "ch" in "loch" after a vowel | niht
f / s / þ | voiced (v, z, th in "this") between vowels, voiceless elsewhere | hlāford
NOTE: All the letters are said, including the "k" in "cniht" (knight) and the "h" in "hlāf" (loaf).
NOTE: Old English has four cases, three genders and a different "you" for one (þū) and many (ġē).
NOTE: This pool is much smaller than the Spanish one on purpose. Only well-attested words and forms are included. Nothing has been checked by a specialist, so treat it as a first guide.

[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Old English has "ic" (I), "þū" (you, one person), "hē" (he), "hēo" (she), "hit" (it), "wē" (we), "ġē" (you, plural), "hīe" (they). There is also a dual "wit" (we two) and "ġit" (you two).
row: I | ic | ik
row: you (one) | þū | thoo
row: he | hē | hay
row: she | hēo | HAY-oh
row: it | hit | hit
row: we | wē | way
row: you (plural) | ġē | yay
row: they | hīe | HEE-eh
ex: I am a man | ic eom mann | ik ay-om mahn
ex: you are strong | þū eart strang | thoo ehrt strahng
ex: he is a king | hē is cyning | hay is KUN-ing
ex: she is a woman | hēo is wīf | HAY-oh is weef
ex: we are friends | wē sind frēond | way sind FRAY-ond
## To be: bēon
> The present of "to be": eom, eart, is, sind (or sindon). Learn all of them: ic eom, þū eart, hē is, wē sind, ġē sind, hīe sind.
row: I am | eom | ay-om
row: you are | eart | ehrt
row: he / she is | is | is
row: we are | sind | sind
row: you are (plural) | sind | sind
row: they are | sind | sind
ex: I am here | ic eom hēr | ik ay-om hair
ex: the king is good | se cyning is gōd | seh KUN-ing is gohd
ex: they are brothers | hīe sind brōþor | HEE-eh sind BROH-thor
## Negation
> Put "ne" before the verb: ic ne wāt (I know not). Some verbs join with "ne": nis (is not), næs (was not), nolde (would not).
row: not | ne | neh
ex: I know | ic wāt | ik waht
ex: I do not know | ic ne wāt | ik neh waht
ex: he is good | hē is gōd | hay is gohd
ex: he is not good | hē nis gōd | hay nis gohd
## Commands
> The command is the bare stem: cum (come), far (go), wes (be). Plural adds -aþ: cumaþ.
row: come! | cum | koom
row: go! | far | fahr
row: be! | wes | wes
ex: come here | cum hider | koom HI-der
ex: be well | wes hāl | wes hahl
ex: go | far | fahr
