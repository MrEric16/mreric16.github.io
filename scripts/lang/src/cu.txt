INTRO: Old Church Slavonic is the oldest written Slavic language, created in the 9th century by the brothers Cyril and Methodius for the Slavs of Moravia. It was first written in Glagolitic, then in Cyrillic, which is what is used here. It is the ancestor of Russian, Bulgarian, Serbian and others, and the liturgical language of many Orthodox churches. The pronunciation is reconstructed.
ѣ | like "ya" in "yard", or a long "e" | вѣра
ѫ | a nasal "o", like French "on" | рѫка
ѧ | a nasal "e", like French "in" | пѧть
ъ / ь | very short vowels, "oo" and "ee", dropped in later Slavic languages | домъ
ы | like "i" in "bit" said further back | рыба
щ | "sht" | нощь
NOTE: Nouns have seven cases, three genders and singular, dual and plural. Many masculine nouns end in -ъ: домъ, градъ, богъ.
NOTE: This pool is much smaller than the Spanish one on purpose. Only well-attested words and forms are included. The pronunciation is reconstructed and nothing has been checked by a specialist, so treat it as a first guide.

[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Old Church Slavonic has singular, dual (two) and plural: азъ (I), ты (you), мы (we), вы (you, many).
row: I | азъ | AH-zoo
row: you (one) | ты | tih
row: he | онъ | OH-noo
row: she | она | oh-NAH
row: it | оно | oh-NOH
row: we | мы | mih
row: you (many) | вы | vih
row: they | они | oh-NEE
ex: I am a king | азъ есмь цѣсарь | AH-zoo ES-mee TSYEH-sah-ree
ex: you are a man | ты еси мѫжь | tih EH-see mohn-ZHEE
ex: we are brothers | мы есмъ братрии | mih ES-moo BRAH-tree-ee
## To be: быти
> The verb "to be" changes for each person.
row: I am | есмь | ES-mee
row: you are | еси | EH-see
row: he / she / it is | естъ | ES-too
row: we are | есмъ | ES-moo
row: you (many) are | есте | ES-teh
row: they are | сѫтъ | SOHN-too
ex: I am good | азъ есмь добръ | AH-zoo ES-mee DOH-broo
ex: he is a king | онъ естъ цѣсарь | OH-noo ES-too TSYEH-sah-ree
ex: they are men | они сѫтъ мѫжи | oh-NEE SOHN-too MOHN-zhee
## Negation
> Put "не" before the verb: "не вѣмь" (I do not know). For "there is not", say "нѣстъ".
row: not | не | neh
row: there is not | нѣстъ | NYEH-stoo
ex: I know | вѣмь | VYEH-mee
ex: I do not know | не вѣмь | neh VYEH-mee
ex: he is good | онъ естъ добръ | OH-noo ES-too DOH-broo
ex: he is not good | онъ нѣстъ добръ | OH-noo NYEH-stoo DOH-broo
## Commands
> The command uses the verb stem: ид-и (go!), глагол-и (say!). Add "не" to forbid.
row: go! | иди | EE-dee
row: say! | глаголи | glah-GOH-lee
row: do not go | не иди | neh EE-dee
ex: eat! | ѣжь | yehzh
ex: do not eat! | не ѣжь | neh yehzh
