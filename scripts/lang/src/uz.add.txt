## Pronouns
> Uzbek often drops the pronoun, because the verb ending shows who is speaking. Pronouns are for emphasis. There is no he / she difference: "u" means both.
row: I | men | men
row: you (informal) | sen | sen
row: he / she / it | u | oo
row: we | biz | biz
row: you (formal or plural) | siz | siz
row: they | ular | oo-LAR
ex: I am a student | men talabaman | men ta-la-ba-MAN
ex: you are tall | sen balandsan | sen ba-land-SAN
ex: she is my sister | u mening opam | oo me-NING o-PAM
ex: we are friends | biz do'stmiz | biz dawst-MIZ
ex: they are teachers | ular o'qituvchi | oo-LAR aw-ki-tooy-CHEE
## Singular and plural
> Add -lar to the noun. It is the same for every word, so there is no vowel harmony in the plural. After a number, the noun stays singular: ikki kitob (two book).
row: kitob (book) | kitoblar | ki-tob-LAR
row: uy (house) | uylar | ooy-LAR
row: bola (child) | bolalar | bo-la-LAR
row: after a number: singular | ikki kitob | ik-KEE ki-TOB
ex: one house, houses | bitta uy, uylar | bit-TA ooy ooy-LAR
ex: one book, books | bitta kitob, kitoblar | bit-TA ki-TOB ki-tob-LAR
ex: one cat, cats | bitta mushuk, mushuklar | bit-TA moo-SHOOK moo-shook-LAR
ex: two books | ikkita kitob | ik-ki-TA ki-TOB
ex: the children | bolalar | bo-la-LAR
## To be: -man, -san, -(dir)
> There is no separate verb "to be". Add an ending to the noun or adjective: talaba-man (I am a student). The ending for "he / she is" is none, or -dir.

row: you are | -san | san
row: he / she is | (no ending) or -dir | dir
row: we are | -miz | miz
row: you are (formal or plural) | -siz | siz
row: they are | -lar | lar
ex: I am a student | men talabaman | men ta-la-ba-MAN
ex: you are tired | sen charchagansan | sen char-cha-gan-SAN

ex: they are at home | ular uyda | oo-LAR ooy-DA
## Bor and yo'q: have, there is
> Uzbek says "at me there is" (bor) or "at me there is not" (yo'q). The ending -da means "at" or "in".
row: there is | bor | bor
row: there is not | yo'q | yawk
row: I have | menda ... bor | men-DA bor
row: I do not have | menda ... yo'q | men-DA yawk
ex: I have a brother | menda aka bor | men-DA a-KA bor
ex: I have a car | menda mashina bor | men-DA ma-shi-NA bor
ex: I do not have time | menda vaqt yo'q | men-DA vakt yawk
ex: there is a problem | muammo bor | moo-am-MO bor
ex: there is no water | suv yo'q | soov yawk
