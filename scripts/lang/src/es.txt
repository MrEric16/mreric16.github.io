[sounds]
INTRO: Spanish is spelled the way it sounds. Learn these and you can read anything.
a | ah, like "father" | casa
e | eh, like "bed" | mesa
i | ee, like "see" | sí
o | oh, like "more" (short) | solo
u | oo, like "food" | uno
h | silent, never pronounced | hola
j | harsh h, like Scottish "loch" | rojo
g (e, i) | same harsh h | gente
g (a, o, u) | hard g, like "go" | gato
ll | y, like "yes" | calle
ñ | ny, like "canyon" | año
qu | k | queso
c (a, o, u) | k | casa
c (e, i) | s (in Spain: th) | cena
z | s (in Spain: th) | zapato
v | b, soft; v and b sound the same | vaca
r | one quick tap of the tongue | pero
rr | rolled r, tongue trills | perro

## Pronouns
> Spanish often drops the pronoun, because the verb ending already shows who is speaking.
row: I | yo | YOH
row: you (informal) | tú | too
row: he / she / you (formal) | él / ella / usted | ehl / EH-yah / oos-TEHD
row: we | nosotros | noh-SOH-trohs
row: you all (Spain) | vosotros | boh-SOH-trohs
row: they / you all | ellos / ustedes | EH-yohs / oos-TEH-dehs
ex: I am a student | yo soy estudiante | yoh soy ehs-too-dee-AHN-teh
ex: you are tall | tú eres alto | too EH-rehs AHL-toh
ex: she is my sister | ella es mi hermana | EH-yah ehs mee ehr-MAH-nah
ex: we are friends | nosotros somos amigos | noh-SOH-trohs SOH-mohs ah-MEE-gohs
ex: they are teachers | ellos son profesores | EH-yohs sohn proh-feh-SOH-rehs
## Singular and plural
> Nouns are masculine or feminine. Most ending in -o are masculine, most ending in -a feminine. Learn the word with its article.
row: the (m / f) | el / la | ehl / lah
row: the (plural m / f) | los / las | lohs / lahs
row: a (m / f) | un / una | oon / OO-nah
> Plural: add -s after a vowel, -es after a consonant. Adjectives agree with the noun.
ex: the boy | el niño | ehl NEE-nyoh
ex: the girl | la niña | lah NEE-nyah
ex: the books | los libros | lohs LEE-brohs
ex: a red house | una casa roja | OO-nah KAH-sah RROH-hah
ex: the cities | las ciudades | lahs see-oo-DAH-dehs
ex: one cat, two cats | un gato, dos gatos | oon GAH-toh dohs GAH-tohs
ex: one pen, two pens | un bolígrafo, dos bolígrafos | oon boh-LEE-grah-foh dohs boh-LEE-grah-fohs
ex: a lesson, lessons | una clase, clases | OO-nah KLAH-seh KLAH-sehs
## Ser: to be (who or what)
> Use ser for identity, origin, jobs, descriptions and time. It is irregular.
row: I am | soy | soy
row: you are | eres | EH-rehs
row: he / she is | es | ehs
row: we are | somos | SOH-mohs
row: you all are (Spain) | sois | soys
row: they are | son | sohn
ex: I am English | soy inglés | soy een-GLEHS
ex: he is a doctor | él es médico | ehl ehs MEH-dee-koh
ex: it is Monday | es lunes | ehs LOO-nehs
ex: we are from Uzbekistan | somos de Uzbekistán | SOH-mohs deh ooz-beh-krees-TAHN
ex: they are tall | son altos | sohn AHL-tohs

row: you all are (Spain) | estáis | ehs-TAYS
row: they are | están | ehs-TAHN
ex: I am at home | estoy en casa | ehs-TOY ehn KAH-sah

## Regular verbs (present tense)
> Take the ending off the infinitive, then add the new ending. Three families: -ar, -er, -ir.
row: hablar (to speak): yo | hablo | AH-bloh
row: tú | hablas | AH-blahs
row: él / ella | habla | AH-blah
row: nosotros | hablamos | ah-BLAH-mohs
row: ellos | hablan | AH-blahn
row: comer: yo, tú, él, nosotros, ellos | como, comes, come, comemos, comen | KOH-moh...
row: vivir: yo, tú, él, nosotros, ellos | vivo, vives, vive, vivimos, viven | BEE-boh...
ex: I speak English | hablo inglés | AH-bloh een-GLEHS
ex: you eat a lot | comes mucho | KOH-mehs MOO-choh
ex: she lives in Madrid | ella vive en Madrid | EH-yah BEE-beh ehn mah-DREED
ex: we work every day | trabajamos todos los días | trah-bah-HAH-mohs TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs
ex: they drink water | beben agua | BEH-behn AH-gwah
## Ir: to go
row: I go | voy | boy
row: you go | vas | bahs
row: he / she goes | va | bah
row: we go | vamos | BAH-mohs
row: they go | van | bahn

ex: I am going to school | voy al colegio | boy ahl koh-LEH-hee-oh

## Basic sentences
> Order: subject, verb, the rest. The adjective usually comes after the noun. You can drop the pronoun.
row: subject + verb + rest | Yo como pan. | yoh KOH-moh pahn
row: adjective after noun | una casa grande | OO-nah KAH-sah GRAHN-deh
row: add time or place at the end | Trabajo aquí hoy. | trah-BAH-hoh ah-KEE oy
ex: I eat bread | como pan | KOH-moh pahn
ex: Maria reads a book | María lee un libro | mah-REE-ah LEH-eh oon LEE-broh
ex: we live in a big house | vivimos en una casa grande | bee-BEE-mohs ehn OO-nah KAH-sah GRAHN-deh
ex: he works here today | él trabaja aquí hoy | ehl trah-BAH-hah ah-KEE oy
ex: the dog is black | el perro es negro | ehl PEH-rroh ehs NEH-groh
## Negation
> Put no before the verb. Double negatives are normal: no tengo nada (I do not have anything).
row: no + verb | no hablo | noh AH-bloh
row: nothing | nada | NAH-dah
row: nobody | nadie | NAH-dee-eh
row: never | nunca | NOON-kah
## Asking questions
> Yes or no question: same words as a statement, with a rising voice and ¿ ?. Often the subject goes after the verb.
row: statement | Tú hablas inglés. | too AH-blahs een-GLEHS
row: question | ¿Hablas inglés? | AH-blahs een-GLEHS
row: add "right?" | ¿verdad? | behr-DAHD
ex: do you speak English? | ¿hablas inglés? | AH-blahs een-GLEHS
ex: is she a doctor? | ¿es médica? | ehs MEH-dee-kah
ex: do you have a pen? | ¿tienes un bolígrafo? | tee-EH-nehs oon boh-LEE-grah-foh
ex: are you tired? | ¿estás cansado? | ehs-TAHS kahn-SAH-doh
ex: is it far? | ¿está lejos? | ehs-TAH LEH-hohs

## Formal vs informal
> Informal: tú (one person), vosotros (several, Spain). Formal: usted and ustedes, with the verb in the he / she form.
row: you, informal | tú | too
row: you, formal | usted | oos-TEHD
row: you all, informal (Spain) | vosotros | boh-SOH-trohs
row: you all, formal | ustedes | oos-TEH-dehs
ex: you speak (informal) | tú hablas | too AH-blahs
ex: you speak (formal) | usted habla | oos-TEHD AH-blah
ex: you are tired (informal) | estás cansado | ehs-TAHS kahn-SAH-doh
ex: you are tired (formal) | está cansado | ehs-TAH kahn-SAH-doh
ex: your name (formal) | su nombre | soo NOHM-breh
## Past and future (simple)
> Past (preterite) of -ar verbs: -é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -aron. Future: infinitive + -é, -ás, -á, -emos, -án.
row: I spoke | hablé | ah-BLEH
row: you spoke | hablaste | ah-BLAHS-teh
row: he / she spoke | habló | ah-BLOH
row: I will speak | hablaré | ah-blah-REH
row: we will speak | hablaremos | ah-blah-REH-mohs
ex: yesterday I worked | ayer trabajé | ah-YEHR trah-bah-HEH
ex: she bought a book | ella compró un libro | EH-yah kohm-PROH oon LEE-broh
ex: I will call you tomorrow | te llamaré mañana | teh yah-mah-REH mah-NYAH-nah
ex: we will eat at eight | comeremos a las ocho | koh-meh-REH-mohs ah lahs OH-choh
ex: they travelled to Spain | viajaron a España | bee-ah-HAH-rohn ah ehs-PAH-nyah
