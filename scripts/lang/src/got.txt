INTRO: Gothic is the oldest Germanic language we have a real text in. It comes from the Bible that Bishop Wulfila (Ulfilas) translated in the 4th century AD. It died out centuries ago. The Latin spelling here is the scholars' transliteration of the Gothic alphabet. The sounds are reconstructed, and the Gothic alphabet itself is not shown because many devices cannot display it.
þ | like "th" in "thing" | þu
ƕ | "hw", like the old Scots "wh" in "which" | ƕas
ai / aí | short "e", like "bed"; aí is the same sound in a few words | ains
ei | long "ee" | weis
au / aú | short "o", like "more" but short | augo
ô / ê / û | long "oh", long "ay", long "oo" | fôtus
g (before g, k) | "ng", as in "sing" | briggan
NOTE: Gothic nouns have three genders and four main cases. Many masculine nouns end in -s in the nominative: dags (day), wulfs (wolf), hlaifs (bread).
NOTE: This pool is much smaller than the Spanish one on purpose. Gothic survives in only one main text (the Bible), so there are no words for modern things and some everyday words are missing. The pronunciation is reconstructed, and nothing has been checked by a specialist. Treat it as a first guide.

[grammar]
## Pronouns
> Gothic has singular, dual (two) and plural for "I" and "you": wit (we two), jut (you two), weis (we, many), jus (you, many).
row: I | ik | ik
row: you (one) | þu | thoo
row: he | is | is
row: she | si | see
row: it | ita | EE-tah
row: we two | wit | wit
row: we (many) | weis | wees
row: you (many) | jus | yoos
ex: I am a king | ik im þiudans | ik im THEE-oo-dans
ex: you are a man | þu is manna | thoo is MAN-nah
ex: we are brothers | weis sijum broþrjus | wees SEE-yoom BROHTH-ryoos
## To be: wisan
> The verb "to be" changes for each person.
row: I am | im | im
row: you are | is | is
row: he / she / it is | ist | ist
row: we are | sijum | SEE-yoom
row: you (many) are | sijuþ | SEE-yooth
row: they are | sind | sind
ex: I am good | ik im goþs | ik im gohths
ex: he is a king | is ist þiudans | is ist THEE-oo-dans
ex: they are men | sind mans | sind mahns
## Negation
> Put "ni" before the verb: "ik ni wait" (I do not know). For a ban, use "ni" with the verb: "ni gagg" (do not go).
row: not | ni | nee
row: no one | ni manna | nee MAN-nah
ex: I know | ik wait | ik wet
ex: I do not know | ik ni wait | ik nee wet
ex: he is good | is ist goþs | is ist gohths
ex: he is not good | is ni ist goþs | is nee ist gohths
## Commands
> The command uses the verb stem: "gagg" (go!), "qim" (come!). Add "ni" to forbid.
row: go! | gagg! | gahng
row: come! | qim! | kweem
row: do not go | ni gagg | nee gahng
ex: eat! | it! | it
ex: do not eat! | ni it | nee it
