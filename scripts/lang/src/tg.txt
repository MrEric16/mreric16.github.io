NAME: Tajik
ID: tg
FLAG: tj

[sounds]
INTRO: Tajik is a form of Persian, written in Cyrillic with six extra letters. There are no genders and no articles. The stress is usually on the last syllable. Learn the special letters first.
ӣ | a long "ee", like "see" | ман ҳастӣ
ӯ | a long "oo" with rounded lips, like German "ö" without the "r" | рӯз (rooz)
қ | a "k" made far back in the throat | қалам (kah-LAM)
ғ | gh, like a gargled French "r" | ғалат (gha-LAT)
ҳ | a breathy "h", always pronounced | ҳамин (hah-MEEN)
ҷ | j, like "jam" | ҷой (joy)
ё / ю / я | "yo", "yoo" and "yah" | ёр (yor)
NOTE: Tajik is Persian written in Cyrillic, so it shares most words with Persian (Farsi). It has no word order change for questions: the voice goes up.

## Pronouns
> Tajik has no gender. "Ӯ" or "вай" means he, she and it. Use "шумо" for one person you respect.
row: I | ман | man
row: you (informal) | ту | too
row: he / she / it | ӯ / вай | oo / vai
row: we | мо | mo
row: you (formal or plural) | шумо | shoo-MO
row: they | онҳо | on-HO
ex: I am a student | ман донишҷӯ ҳастам | man do-neesh-JOO has-TAM

ex: she is my older sister | вай апаи ман аст | vai ah-pah-ee MAN ast
ex: we are friends | мо дӯст ҳастем | mo DOOST has-TEM
ex: they are teachers | онҳо муаллим ҳастанд | on-HO moo-al-LEEM has-TAND

> Add -ҳо to the noun (китоб, китобҳо). For people, -он is also used (бача, бачагон). After a number, the noun stays singular: ду китоб (two book).
