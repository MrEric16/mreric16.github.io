NAME: Latvian (Latviešu)
ID: lv
FLAG: lv

[sounds]
INTRO: Latvian uses the Latin alphabet with extra marks: macrons (ā, ē, ī, ū) for long vowels, and cedillas and carons for special consonants. It is a Baltic language, related to Lithuanian but not to Russian. Stress is almost always on the first syllable.
ā ē ī ū | the same vowel, held longer | māte
ē | long "e", like "air" without the "r" | ēst
ī | long "ee", like "see" | rīts
š | "sh" as in "ship" | šodien
č | "ch" as in "church" | čau
ž | "s" as in "measure" | žurnāls
ķ ģ | "ty" and "dy" sounds (soft k and g) | ķirsis
ļ ņ | soft "l" and soft "n", like "ly" in "million" and "ny" in "canyon" | ļoti
NOTE: Latvian has six cases and no articles. Stress is on the first syllable of every word, which makes it easy to read.
NOTE: The pool is unreviewed. Some rarer words are best guesses.

## Pronouns
> Latvian has "tu" (you, informal) and "jūs" (you, formal or plural). Verb forms change for each person. There is no he / she difference in the plural except for gender in the past: "viņi" (they, men) and "viņas" (they, women).
