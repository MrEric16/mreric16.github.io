## Negation
> Put an before the verb (short form) or add -ji anayo to the stem (long form). For "there is not" use eopseoyo; for "is not" use ani-eyo.
row: not (short) | an + verb | ahn
row: not (long) | stem + -ji anayo | jee ah-nah-yoh
row: there is not | eopseoyo | uhp-suh-yoh
row: is not | anieyo | ah-nee-eh-yoh

## Can, must, want
> -l su isseoyo = can (hal su isseoyo), -l su eopseoyo = cannot, -eoya haeyo = must (gaya haeyo), -go sipeoyo = want to (meokgo sipeoyo). Must not = -myeon an dwaeyo.
row: I can | -l su isseoyo | soo ee-suh-yoh | 수 있어요
row: I must | -eoya haeyo | uh-yah hae-yoh | 야 해요
row: I want to | -go sipeoyo | goh shee-puh-yoh | 고 싶어요
ex: I can swim | suyeong-eul hal su isseoyo | soo-yuhng-eul hahl soo ee-suh-yoh | 수영을 할 수 있어요
ex: I cannot swim | suyeong-eul hal su eopseoyo | soo-yuhng-eul hahl soo uhp-suh-yoh | 수영을 할 수 없어요
ex: can you help me? | dowa jul su isseoyo? | doh-wah jool soo ee-suh-yoh | 도와 줄 수 있어요?

ex: we must not go | gamyeon an dwaeyo | gah-myuhn ahn dweh-yoh | 가면 안 돼요
