[sounds]
INTRO: Dutch is written in the Latin alphabet. It sounds a lot like a mix of English and German. Nouns are "de" or "het" words, and you must learn which for each noun. The stress is usually on the first syllable.
g / ch | a scratchy sound at the back of the throat, like "ch" in Scottish "loch" | goed, acht
ui | a sound like "ow" in "house", with rounded lips | huis
ij / ei | like "ay" in "day" but a bit sharper | tijd, trein
oe | like "oo" in "moon" | goed
eu | like "ur" in "fur" with rounded lips | deur
ou / au | like "ow" in "cow" | koud
w | between English "w" and "v" | water
sch | "s" plus a scratchy "ch" | school
NOTE: Long vowels are written double in a closed syllable (aa, ee, oo, uu), and single in an open syllable (maken, not maaken).

## Pronouns
> Dutch always uses the pronoun. "Je" is informal and "u" is formal. "Hij" is he, "zij" or "ze" is she, "het" is it.
