This article is about a very old fish. It is called the Greenland shark, and it may be the oldest animal with a backbone on Earth.

Let's start with its age. A Greenland shark can live for about four hundred years. Some of the sharks swimming today may have been babies when Shakespeare was alive. Scientists still argue about the exact number. Some say three hundred years, and some say five hundred.

These sharks are big. Some grow to five metres long. But they are very slow swimmers. They live in the cold, dark water of the North Atlantic and the Arctic, near Greenland, Canada and Iceland. Their bodies hold special chemicals that work like anti-freeze. This keeps them safe from the cold. It also makes their meat poisonous.

Now, here is the strange part. Old bodies usually break down. But the Greenland shark seems to fight that.

Take its eyes. For years, people thought these sharks were nearly blind. It looked that way because small parasites hang from their eyes. But new research found the opposite. Their eyes work very well in the dark, like a camera made for low light. Even in a shark over one hundred years old, the eyes still look healthy. Human eyes get worse with age. Theirs do not.

Next, the heart. A heart that beats for centuries should be worn out. And scientists did find scars in the hearts of these sharks, the kind that cause trouble in other animals. But the hearts still worked well. One shark's heart weighed thirty-four kilograms. A human heart weighs about three hundred grams.

Scientists also think the shark is very good at fixing the damage inside its own cells. Some of the genes that repair damage seem to work extra hard.

In April 2026, a dead Greenland shark washed up on a beach in Ireland. It was the first one ever found there. It was about three metres long. Experts think it was around one hundred and fifty years old. For a Greenland shark, that is still young. It had not yet had babies.

A team of scientists studied the body. They found no clear reason for its death. They took samples from its eyes and its muscles, to learn its real age. The skin was saved, and a museum hopes to show the shark one day.

The sad part is how little we know. We do not know where these sharks have their babies. We do not even know how many babies they have. So it is hard to protect them.

The Greenland shark lives slowly, quietly and for a very long time. And it still has many secrets.
