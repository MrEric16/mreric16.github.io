This article is about the snow leopard, one of the hardest animals in the world to see. People call it the ghost of the mountain. And it lives right here in Uzbekistan.

Snow leopards are quiet, they live alone, and their fur hides them well against the rocks. They are most active at dawn and in the evening. Some scientists spend their whole lives studying them and see one only once.

Two wildlife presenters went to look for one. They travelled to a nature reserve in the mountains in the south of Uzbekistan. It is called Gissar. It is the biggest protected area in the country. It is about the size of New York City.

The city of Samarkand even has the snow leopard on its emblem. A local guide said the animal is part of the country's identity, and a symbol of protection. Now, people need to protect it in return.

The reserve was created in 1985, when Uzbekistan was part of the Soviet Union. In those days, ordinary visitors could not enter. Only rangers and scientists could go in. Farming and building were also strictly limited. Because of this, the mountains stayed wild. More than two hundred and seventy kinds of animals live there. So do more than one thousand two hundred kinds of plants.

The team hiked for days in thin air and in freezing wind. They looked for clues. They found fox tracks. They found the footprint of a lynx. Then, high on a ridge, a ranger shouted. He had found snow leopard droppings. It was proof that a snow leopard had passed that way not long before.

Rangers also hide cameras in the mountains. There are more than seventy of them. The cameras take photos when something walks past. They had already caught bears, wild goats and a porcupine. And then, one night, the camera filmed a snow leopard with its very long tail. Everyone cheered.

There is good news. The number of snow leopards in Gissar has grown since the 1980s. There are now sixty-one of them. Around the world there are somewhere between four thousand and six thousand five hundred. The animal is no longer called endangered. It is now called vulnerable.

But there are still problems. Snow leopards can attack farm animals, so some farmers are angry. One herder said the leopards are beautiful but scary. He said he does not mind them if there are enough wild animals for them to eat. Scientists are making plans to pay farmers when a leopard kills their animals.

Snow leopards also walk across the borders of twelve countries. So the countries must work together.

The team did not see a snow leopard in the wild. But the cameras and tracks proved it is there. And that is the first step to protecting it.
