This article is about a day when a man did something that many people thought was impossible. He ran a whole marathon in less than two hours.

A marathon is a very long race. It is twenty-six point two miles, or about forty-two kilometres. The runner was a Kenyan athlete, then thirty-four years old. His time was one hour, fifty-nine minutes and forty seconds. That means he ran each mile in about four and a half minutes, again and again, for two hours. For most people, even one fast mile is hard.

But there is an important thing to know. This time is not an official world record. Here is why.

The race was not a normal race. A chemical company organised it and paid for it. It was held on a flat loop of road of about six miles, in the city of Vienna. The weather was almost perfect. He was the only person racing. Other top runners helped him as pacers. They ran in front of him in a V shape, to block the wind. They also swapped in and out during the race, which is not allowed in an official race. A car also helped to guide him with a laser line on the road. Nothing like this is allowed at an official event.

Why does this matter so much? In a marathon, small things add up. Cold rain, strong wind, hills, not drinking enough water, or not eating enough, can all slow down a runner. That is why the first time a runner goes under two hours, it has to be in perfect conditions. And before this day, no one even knew if it was possible. The same runner had tried in an earlier race, and missed by less than one minute.

There is another story that helps us understand. For a long time, doctors believed that nobody could run a mile in under four minutes. Some even thought it could kill a person. Then, in nineteen fifty-four, a young English doctor did it. Less than two months later, another man did it faster. Today, even good high-school athletes can run a mile that fast. So once something is shown to be possible, many people start doing it. The Kenyan runner said exactly this. He said that no human is limited. He hopes more people will run under two hours.

But the marathon is much longer than a mile. That makes it harder. More things can go wrong. And at a normal race, pacers cannot come and go. So a runner trying to beat two hours at a real race would soon be running alone.

Science had already given a hint. In nineteen ninety-one, a scientist worked out the best possible marathon time for a perfect runner. He used the body's oxygen use, its muscle limits and its energy use. His answer was a little under one hour fifty-eight minutes.

Shoes are also part of the story. The company that made his shoes uses a special foam and a plate made of carbon. This gives the runner a small push forward. Some people think this is unfair. But the writer says that even with the shoes, the idea that no one can run this fast has been broken.

Finally, the people who worked with him say he has an amazing ability to ignore pain. Maybe that is what makes the difference. Maybe, in the future, other runners will learn from him.
