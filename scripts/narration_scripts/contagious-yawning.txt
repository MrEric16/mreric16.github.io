This short article asks a simple question. Why is yawning contagious?

If you are listening to this, there is a good chance you just yawned. Athletes do it. Politicians do it. Lots of animals do it too. But why does one yawn start a chain?

Scientists at a university in England found that a part of the brain that controls movement is also behind this reflex. So when you see someone yawn, that part of your brain wakes up and you yawn too.

Some researchers think yawning is also a way to communicate. It may be a way to show that you care. A different study found that people catch yawns more from friends and family than from strangers. That fits the idea that feelings play a part.

So if you yawn in a meeting, you can say you are not bored. You are just very kind.
