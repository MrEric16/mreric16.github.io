This article tells the story of one of the most famous logos in the world. It is the Nike swoosh. And the person who drew it was paid only thirty-five dollars.

It started in 1971. A man named Phil Knight owned a small company with his old running coach. They sold running shoes from Japan in the United States. But they were about to make their own shoes, under a new name. The new name would come from the Greek goddess of victory. That name was Nike.

They needed a logo. Phil Knight also taught accounting part-time at a university in Portland. There, he met a design student named Carolyn Davidson. He asked her to draw some ideas. His only instruction was that the logo should have something to do with movement.

She made several sketches. One of them looked like a wing. It was the swoosh.

Knight showed the drawings to the other people in his small company. They picked the swoosh. But Knight was not excited. Davidson remembers what he told her. He said he didn't love it, but maybe it would grow on him.

Her bill for the work was thirty-five dollars.

Of course, the swoosh did grow on him, and on the whole world. In a survey in 2020, people in the United States were asked which logos they knew best. The swoosh came in fourth. Only Apple, McDonald's, and Coca-Cola were ahead of it.

A designer explained why it works so well. The swoosh is simple. It has two points, a start and an end. In the middle, it gets wider. This is like exercise. You start out slowly, and you build up your power. He also said that great ideas can come from anyone. It does not matter if the artist is a student.

What happened to the student? Later, she became a full-time worker at Nike, and she worked there for four years. Then, in 1983, the company gave her a surprise gift. It was a gift of shares in the company. Knight later said it was five hundred shares.

Davidson said it feels strange to see her design everywhere today. But she also said the real reason for its fame was the company's good business sense. Without that, she said, it would have been just another drawing.
