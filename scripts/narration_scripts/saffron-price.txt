This article explains why saffron is the most expensive spice in the world. It costs far more than other spices that look similar. So why is it so costly?

Let's start with a comparison. Many cooks use turmeric to give food a yellow colour. It is cheap. In one Japanese shop, turmeric costs about fourteen yen for one gram. Saffron costs about seventeen hundred yen for one gram. That means saffron is more than one hundred and twenty times more expensive. Saffron is so pricey that it is sold in tiny bottles, with less than half a gram inside.

What is saffron? It is a spice that comes from the middle part of a flower. It gives food a bright yellow colour, so it is often used in dishes like saffron rice and paella. Cooks love it because the food looks beautiful.

The first reason for the price is the number of flowers. You need about one hundred and seventy thousand flowers to make just one kilogram of saffron. Think about that. It is a huge field of flowers for a very small bag of spice.

The second reason is the work. Saffron has to be picked by hand. Workers take the spice out of each flower, one flower at a time. A machine cannot do this job. It takes a lot of people and a lot of time.

The third reason is the season. The time for picking is very short. It lasts for only a few days each year. The farmers must work quickly, because the harvest is over before they know it.

Put these things together, and you can see why the spice has a nickname. People call it "red gold."

Saffron is also very, very old. One famous story is about a king who started the first big kingdom in the ancient land of Mesopotamia. The story says that the king was born in "the city of saffron." That story is about four thousand three hundred years old. So people knew about saffron even then.

More recently, scientists studied the history of the plant. In 2022, a study said that people in Greece started to grow saffron about three thousand seven hundred years ago. So people have been farming it for a very long time.

Today, many places grow saffron. One city in Japan has a special way to do it. The farmers grow the flowers indoors, and they make them bloom inside, away from the weather. They say that this saffron has several times more of the active parts than saffron from other countries.

So the next time you see a tiny bottle of saffron, remember how much work is inside it. It took hundreds of flowers and many pairs of hands. Perhaps it is not so strange that it costs so much.
