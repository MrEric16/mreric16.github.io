This article tells the story of a huge city under the ground. It had room for twenty thousand people. And one man found it by accident, in his own house.

It happened in 1963 in Turkey. A man was fixing his house. He knocked down a wall in his basement. Behind it, there was a secret room. And behind that room, there were more rooms, and tunnels, and more tunnels. He had found an ancient city called Derinkuyu.

The city has eighteen floors. It goes more than seventy metres deep. Up to twenty thousand people lived there. Some of them even built small churches down in the rock.

Derinkuyu is only one of more than two hundred underground cities in the area. Some are joined by tunnels many miles long.

How could people make a city under the ground? The answer is volcanoes. Millions of years ago, volcanoes covered the area with thick ash. Over time, the ash turned into a soft rock. It was strong enough to build in, but soft enough to cut with simple tools. So people simply dug into the ground.

Nobody knows exactly who started the city. Some experts think it was a people called the Phrygians, about two thousand seven hundred years ago. Others think it was much earlier, by a people called the Hittites. Over the years, many groups used it. Most used it for the same reason. When enemies came, they went underground and hid.

Today, visitors can walk through the tunnels and rooms. Only a small part of the city is open. The rest is still a secret.
