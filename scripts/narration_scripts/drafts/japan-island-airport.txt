This article is about an airport built on a man-made island in the sea. It is one of the most expensive building projects in history. And it has one big problem. It is sinking.

The airport is called Kansai International. It stands in Osaka Bay, in Japan. Let's start with why Japan built it in the sea. In the nineteen-sixties, the Kansai region, around Osaka and Kobe, was losing business to Tokyo. Leaders wanted a new airport to bring it back. The old airport nearby was too crowded, and there was no room to grow. Building a new one on land was hard, too. Farmers had protested against another airport, because their land had been taken. So planners had a different idea. Build it offshore. There was also an extra gain. An airport in the sea has no neighbours to complain about the noise, so it can work twenty-four hours a day.

At first, people called it a floating airport. But it does not really float. It stands on an artificial island. Engineers made the island from the bottom of the sea. The island is four kilometres long and two and a half kilometres wide. It sits three miles out from the coast, in water about eighteen metres deep.

How do you build an island like this? You need a lot of earth. About one hundred and eighty million cubic metres of rock and soil came from three nearby mountains. They put it on top of the sand on the sea floor. Then they built a wall around the island. It used forty-eight thousand huge concrete blocks, and sixty-nine giant steel boxes. Without this wall, the sea would flood the island. And they had to think about danger. Japan sits in an area where earthquakes and typhoons are common. So the engineers used millions of sand pillars to make the ground stronger.

The terminal building is long and low. The designer wanted air traffic controllers to see all the planes on the runways. It is the longest airport terminal in the world, at one thousand seven hundred metres. Inside, the ceiling is shaped like a wing. Air blows up from one side, flows along the curved roof, and goes down on the other side. This helps to move the air around the whole building. And the terminal stands on columns that can be made taller. You put thick metal plates at their base. This is how they fight the sinking.

A long bridge connects the island to the town on the coast. It carries cars on top and trains underneath. It is three thousand seven hundred and fifty metres long, and it cost about one billion dollars on its own.

After twenty years of planning and three years of building, the airport opened in September nineteen ninety-four. The total cost was fifteen billion dollars. That makes it the most expensive civil engineering project in the world, so far.

But the money problems did not end. The airport has a lot of debt. Interest payments alone cost about five hundred and sixty million dollars every year. To pay for it, the airport charged very high landing fees. At one time, it was the most expensive airport in the world for landing. One big jet paid about seven thousand five hundred dollars, compared with about two thousand five hundred at an airport in New York. Of course, this made airlines think twice about coming.

Now the sinking. The engineers expected the island to go down. But they were wrong about how much. They guessed that it would sink about six to eight metres. In fact, by the time the airport opened, it had already sunk almost twelve metres. By the year two thousand, the island was only four metres above sea level. That is the lowest safe height. Scientists now think it may reach sea level by the year twenty fifty-six.

What did they do? They spent over one hundred and fifty million dollars to make the sea wall taller and stronger. They dug under the main terminal and put iron plates under special jacks. They raised the building in stages. Even today, workers have to adjust the jacks every two years. And the middle of the island, where the main buildings stand, sinks the most.

Today the airport is busy. In twenty nineteen, it handled almost twenty-nine million passengers. Twenty-two million of them were international travellers. More than eighty airlines flew there, to over one hundred places. It has a second terminal just for low-cost airlines. That terminal is very simple. It has only one floor, and passengers walk or take a bus to their planes. The government also wanted more people to use the new airport. So it put limits on the old airport nearby. Since two thousand six, only planes with two engines can use it. Later, a private company took over both airports, with a contract for forty-five years.

Then, in two thousand eighteen, a very powerful typhoon hit. It was the strongest storm in Japan for twenty-five years. The airport was flooded and had to close. The storm also damaged the bridge to the mainland.

Later, the airport added a second island and a second runway. This time the engineers added the sinking into their plans from the start. Today, the airport is working on a big renovation. They hope to have it finished for a big world fair in Osaka.

So the Kansai airport is a story about big dreams, big costs, and the sea. It shows that even the best engineers cannot fully beat nature, and that a project this large needs care and patience for many years after it opens. It still works well today. But every year, the engineers have to fight to keep it above the water.
