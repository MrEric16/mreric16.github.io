This article is about a tiny animal that might be the toughest animal on Earth. It is called the tardigrade. Some people call it the water bear.

It is very small. It is only about half a millimetre long. It has a round body, four pairs of short legs with claws, and a mouth with tiny teeth. Tardigrades live in moss and in water almost everywhere on Earth. One new kind was even found in a car park in Japan. Its eggs have long wiggly strands on top, like spaghetti. Scientists think these help the eggs to stick to things.

Here are some of their strange powers.

First, they can survive heat and cold that would kill almost everything else. They can handle up to about one hundred and fifty degrees Celsius. They can also handle two hundred degrees below zero. How? They push all the water out of their bodies. They pull in their legs and curl up into a dry little ball. In this state, they look almost dead. When the danger is over, they take in water, and they come back to life, without any damage. Scientists found that a special protein, which only tardigrades make, may help them to recover.

Second, they can survive in space. Scientists sent dried tardigrades and eggs to space for ten days. They were out in the empty space and the strong sunlight. Later, scientists woke them up. Most survived the empty space well. Fewer survived the radiation, but some did.

Third, they can stay frozen for decades. In one case, two tardigrades were kept in a freezer for more than thirty years. Then scientists put them in water. One of them started moving the next day. After twenty-two days, it had eggs. In the end, it laid nineteen eggs, and fourteen of them hatched.

And one more strange fact. When a tardigrade hatches, it already looks like a small adult. It has no baby stage, like a caterpillar. It only gets bigger.

So this tiny animal has been around for more than five hundred million years. And it can survive almost anything.
