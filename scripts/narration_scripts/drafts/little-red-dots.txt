This article is about a mystery in space. Scientists found strange red dots in pictures from the James Webb telescope, and they still do not know exactly what they are.

The telescope was launched on Christmas Day in twenty twenty-one. Astronomers hoped it would show the very first galaxies. But a team led by a scientist in Australia saw something they did not expect. There were thirteen bright red blobs. When they measured the brightness and the colour, they found that six of them looked almost as heavy as our own galaxy. Yet they were from only about six hundred million years after the start of the universe. That made no sense, because galaxies were not supposed to grow so big so fast. The team called them universe breakers.

But these objects were much too big for what scientists expected, so they kept studying them. Four years later, they have found about one thousand of these little red dots, including a few dozen in the modern universe. They do not fit any known type of object.

To learn more, astronomers used a method called spectroscopy. It spreads light out into its colours, and each colour tells you which chemical elements are in the glowing material. The light from these dots showed a blurred signal from hydrogen. A blur like that means very fast movement, thousands of kilometres every second. It suggests something really heavy, with gas spinning round it, like a giant black hole.

So scientists changed their idea. Perhaps these were black holes pulling in matter, with dust turning their light red. But more data pointed somewhere else. Black holes like that glow brightly in X-rays, but only one of the dots does. They also flicker in brightness. The little red dots hardly change at all. So they were not normal black holes.

Then another clue came from the shape of the light. It dropped in strength and then suddenly jumped up. Astronomers see this shape in very thick gas, like the atmosphere around a certain kind of hot star. So the new idea is a power source, maybe a black hole, wrapped in a thick cocoon of gas, like a star's atmosphere. It has been called a black hole star. One scientist said it is a pretty crazy conclusion, but it is what the data are telling us.

That is the leading idea for now. But with about a thousand dots, not all look the same. One expert said that we talk about them as if they were one class of objects, but there is no reason to think they are. One dot sends out X-rays. Another sits behind a huge cluster of galaxies, whose gravity bends its light. This gives several images, where the light took different paths, and the paths differ by one hundred and thirty years. This allowed scientists to see that its brightness changes, by about two times. So some dots may be more like normal black holes after all.

Maybe the differences come from the angle we see them from. If the gas around them is clumpy, they look different depending on whether we look through a thick clump or a gap. Maybe the dots are the young stage of today's giant black holes, before they make strong X-rays. As one scientist said, they did not disappear. They became something else, like little villages eaten by a city.

Scientists have also found a few dozen similar objects close to us, less than two billion light-years away. Because they are near, they are easier to study. In four years, astronomers have written more than seven hundred papers about the dots, and the answer is still not clear.

The telescope was built to find the first galaxies. But one scientist said that secretly you hope to find something you have never seen before. The little red dots, she said, really deliver that.
