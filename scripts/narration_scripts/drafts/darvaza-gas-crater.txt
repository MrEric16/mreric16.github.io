This article is about a huge hole in the desert that has been on fire for decades. People call it the Door to Hell.

The hole is in the Karakum Desert, in the north of Turkmenistan. It is about sixty-nine metres wide and thirty metres deep. That is almost as big as a football pitch. It lies near a small village, about two hundred and sixty kilometres north of the capital city.

What you see is hard to forget. Thousands of small flames dance on the walls and on the floor of the crater. You can hear them. You can feel the heat from far away. At some places on the edge, the heat is too strong to stand near. You can even smell the burning from a distance.

Why is it burning? The answer is natural gas. Deep under the ground, there is a lot of gas called methane. In this place, it leaks out through cracks in the rock. When the gas reaches the air, it catches fire. Each small flame shows a spot where gas comes out of the wall or the floor.

How did the hole begin? Many websites tell the same story. They say that it started around nineteen seventy-one. Scientists from the Soviet Union were looking for oil and gas. They started to drill, and they hit a big empty space under the ground. The ground began to fall in. The workers escaped, but their machines were lost. People think that parts of the drilling machine are still lying at one side of the crater.

But here is the important part. The writer of this article says that no one has shown an official paper or a witness for this story. So we do not really know. The story may be true, but nobody has proved it.

What kind of hole is it? A scientist would not call it a crater. A crater is made by a volcano or by something that hits the Earth from space. This is a sinkhole. A sinkhole forms when the ground collapses into an empty space underneath. In this area, the rock below is limestone. Water can slowly dissolve limestone and make big caves. When the top layer cannot hold any more, it falls in.

There are two more sinkholes nearby. People call one the Water Crater, because it holds water. The other is the Mud Crater. It has a pool of thick mud that bubbles because of the gas. Sometimes it has a few flames too.

Today, the place is a tourist stop. The government built a low fence and a stone path to a viewing place. There is also a camp with round tents called yurts. This is because the desert gets very cold at night. And at night, the crater looks best of all.

Turkmenistan has some of the largest gas resources in the world. But it sells less than it could. This is because it has few ways to carry the gas to other countries. Most of what it sells goes to China, Russia and Iran.

There is one more fact. The leader of the country once ordered the fire to be put out. But so far, the flames are still burning.
