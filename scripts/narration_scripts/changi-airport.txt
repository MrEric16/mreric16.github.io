This article is about the best airport in the world. It is Changi Airport in Singapore. It has just won the top airport award for the second year in a row. In total, it has won that award fourteen times.

Imagine you land after a very long flight. You are tired. But at Changi, the lines are short and the bags come quickly. Robot cleaners keep the floors spotless. In less than fifteen minutes, you can be outside the airport.

And if you have to wait for your next flight, there is a lot to do. There is a free cinema that is open all day and all night. There is a garden full of butterflies. There is also a cactus garden and a sunflower garden on the roof. The airport even has the tallest indoor waterfall in the world. There is a robot that makes drinks. And on a long break, you can join a free tour of the city.

But the surprise is this. The fun parts are not the real secret. The real secret is what you cannot see.

About sixty thousand people work at the airport. Computers help them to find problems before passengers notice them. The signs are clear, so tired travellers do not get lost. There are five hundred toilets. Each one has a screen where you can give a score. If the score goes down, a cleaner arrives in minutes.

An expert says the order is simple. First comes speed. Then comes a good feeling. Then comes the show.

Even the fun parts have a job. They make people walk to different corners of the building. So the airport never feels crowded.

Some of this came from need. Singapore does not have enough workers for jobs like passport checks. So the airport turned to machines. In 2024, it became the first airport to do passport checks with no passport at all. It uses your face and your eyes instead.

Changi also never sits still. It has a lab where workers try new ideas, even crazy ones. One idea is a group of drones that fly in storms. Singapore gets a lot of lightning, and lightning can close a runway. The drones are meant to stop that.

The airport's story started in the 1970s. The country was small, and it needed to look welcoming and well run. The airport cost a lot. But the leaders later said it was the best money they ever spent.

So why does Changi keep winning? People remember the waterfall. But the real win is that you get there with no stress.
