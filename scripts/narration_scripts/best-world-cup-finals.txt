This article is about the World Cup final. A football writer picked his ten best finals of all time. He looked at goals, drama, big stars, and the stories that people still tell today. Here are some of them.

Let's start with the most dramatic. In twenty twenty-two, Argentina played France in Qatar. Argentina were two goals ahead, and it looked easy. Then a young French striker scored twice in only ninety seconds. The game went to extra time. Argentina scored again. France came back again, and the match ended three all. Then it went to penalties. Argentina won. The writer says it was probably the most dramatic final ever. And it was the moment when a great player finally won the one big prize he did not have yet. The party in Argentina lasted for days.

Now go back in time to nineteen fifty-eight. Brazil had a seventeen-year-old boy in their team. This was Pele. He had come to the tournament with a knee injury. Brazil won five goals to two, still the highest-scoring World Cup final. Pele scored two. His first goal was amazing. He caught the ball on his chest, lifted it over a defender, and kicked it into the corner. Eight years earlier, Brazil had lost the deciding match of the World Cup at home, and Pele's father had cried by the radio. Pele told him not to cry, he would win the World Cup for him. And he did.

Next, the final in nineteen fifty-four, called the Miracle of Bern. Hungary had a golden team. They had not lost in more than thirty games. They had beaten West Germany eight goals to three in the group stage. In the final, they led two goals to nothing after only eight minutes. But West Germany came back, and scored the winner with six minutes left. It is one of the biggest surprises in football history.

Then nineteen seventy-four. The Netherlands played brilliant, modern football. They scored in the first minute, before the Germans even touched the ball. But the hosts, West Germany, won. Four years later, the Dutch lost the final again, to Argentina.

In nineteen eighty-six, it was all about one man, Diego Maradona. Argentina went two goals ahead. West Germany came back to two all. But six minutes from the end, Maradona played one perfect pass, and Argentina scored the winning goal.

Nineteen ninety-eight is remembered for France. They had never won the World Cup, and they played at home. Their team had players from many different backgrounds. A French defender said this sent a very powerful message to society. France won three nil. But people also remember that the best player in the world, a Brazilian striker, was ill before the game. He was nowhere near his best.

And then there is nineteen sixty-six. England won on home ground. The match is famous for one man, who scored three goals. It is still the only hat-trick in a World Cup final for a winning team. Germany scored a goal in the last minute to make it two all. In extra time, England scored a controversial goal that hit the bar. People still argue about it today. England have not won the World Cup since.

The writer ends with a question. He asks you to make your own list. So, which World Cup final is your favourite?
