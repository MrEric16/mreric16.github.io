This article tells the story of Snake, the little game on old Nokia phones. Almost everyone who had one of those phones played it. And it was written by one man.

Let's go back to the 1990s. Mobile phones were still new. They could make calls and send short messages, and not much more. A young software engineer from Finland worked at Nokia. He believed that games could make a phone more fun to use.

The idea for Snake was not new. In 1976, an arcade game called Blockade had the same idea. You control a line that grows longer. You collect food, and you must not hit anything. Other versions of it appeared on computers in the eighties and nineties.

In 1997, the engineer had a job. He had to make new features for one of Nokia's newest phones, the Nokia 6110. He wanted to make a simple game that people would enjoy. And he wanted to show what the phone could do.

He picked the Snake idea because it was easy to understand. It also needed very little computer power. And the phone did not have much. Its screen was small, with only black and white. Its processor was very slow compared with phones today.

So he made smart choices. He used just the four arrow buttons, so anyone could start playing at once. He kept the pictures simple, so everything was easy to see. And he wrote very small code. The whole game took up only about two kilobytes.

He added one more special thing. Two people could play together, using the infrared light on the phones. It was one of the first multiplayer games on a mobile phone.

The game came on the phone already installed. That was the secret. Millions of people got it the moment they turned on their phone. It was an instant hit. It showed that people love games on their phones. It showed that a simple idea can become famous around the world.

Three years later, in 2000, a new version appeared on the Nokia 3310. It was one of the most famous phones ever made.

The engineer kept working at Nokia and helped make other mobile games. In 2005, he received an award for his influence on mobile gaming. In 2015, he even helped make a new version of Snake. It was not as popular as the old one.

Today, our phones are much more powerful. But the story of Snake is a good lesson. A small, simple game can last a very long time.
