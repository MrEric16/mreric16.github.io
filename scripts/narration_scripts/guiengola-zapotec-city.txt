This article tells how scientists found a lost city in a forest. For years, people thought the place was only a fort. Then a laser scan showed something much bigger.

The place is called Guiengola. It is in the south of Mexico, in the state of Oaxaca. It was built by a people called the Zapotecs. They lived in this region for a very long time, starting at least 2,500 years ago.

Experts have known about the site for a long time. But they thought it was a fort, where soldiers stayed. The reason is the forest. A thick roof of trees covers the whole place. You can reach the site on a footpath, but you cannot see much. To understand the whole site, you would have to walk around for years.

Then a scientist had an idea. He is an anthropologist at a university in Canada. In late 2022, he scanned the site from a plane. He used a technology called lidar. It sends out laser light, and it can see through the leaves to the ground. The job took only two hours.

The scan showed a whole city. It covered about 890 acres, and it had more than 1,100 buildings. There were about four kilometres of walls, a web of roads, temples, and courts for ball games. There were neighbourhoods for people of different classes. At its best time, at least five thousand people lived there all year.

The city was built in the 1300s. People lived in it for about 150 years. Then they left, a few decades before the Spanish arrived.

There is also a famous story about the place. The Zapotecs had many contacts with the Aztecs, often in battle. According to Spanish records, the Aztecs attacked Guiengola in 1497. The Zapotecs stopped them. Today, the Zapotec people are proud of this place. The scientist says people often think the Aztec empire grew with almost no trouble. But Guiengola shows that this was not true.

The scientist has a special connection to the site. His mother's family comes from a town nearby. When he was a child, he heard his relatives talk about the ruins. It was one of the reasons he became an archaeologist.

The city is only five or six hundred years old, so it is in very good shape. He calls it a city frozen in time. And now, the next job is to study it more. The scientist plans to return to the site later this year.
