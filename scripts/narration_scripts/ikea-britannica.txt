This article is about IKEA. It is the biggest furniture seller in the world. You can find one of its stores in many countries. And the company's story is full of clever choices.

First, where is IKEA from? It is from Sweden. You can see it right away. The stores are painted blue and yellow, like the Swedish flag. The restaurants sell Swedish food. And many of the products have Swedish names. But today, the company's main office is in the Netherlands.

The founder was a boy from a farm in the south of Sweden. He was very good at business, even as a child. He started the company in 1943, when he was only seventeen. The name is made from letters. It uses his own first letters, and then the first letters of the name of his family farm, and of the village where he lived.

At first, he did not sell furniture. He sold small things like pens and lighters. Five years later, he began to sell furniture. In 1951, he printed the first catalogue. A few years later, he opened a showroom in a small town. People could look at the furniture and place an order there. The first real store opened in 1958.

Then came a big idea. In 1956, the company started to sell furniture in flat boxes. This is called flat-packing. A flat box is cheaper to move. It also takes less space in a truck and in a shop. That helped the company lower its prices. And customers carry the box home and build the furniture themselves.

There was also a problem. In the late 1950s, other shops did not like IKEA's low prices. They pushed Swedish factories to stop working with it. But IKEA did not give up. It began to design its own furniture. And it found factories in other countries. The first ones were in Denmark and Poland. Later, it even built its own factory company.

Then IKEA grew across the world. It opened the first shop outside Sweden in Norway in 1963. Then came Switzerland in 1973. Germany followed in 1974, and Australia in 1975. After that came France, the United States, Britain, China and Russia.

A normal IKEA store is huge. It is about as big as two and a half football fields.

One more thing is unusual. IKEA does not have owners who buy and sell shares. Instead, the company is controlled by a long chain of smaller companies and charities. People say this was set up partly because taxes in Sweden were very high.

So IKEA is more than a shop with cheap tables. It is a story about a young boy with an idea, a flat box and a lot of courage.
